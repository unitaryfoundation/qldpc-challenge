[[310,20,12]] — two-block group-algebra code over C31 ⋊ C5 (ZSZ(31,5,2))

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-6 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 288 and 340. Before this campaign the weight-6 frontier there ran 288-16-16, 288-12-18, 294-10-20, 310-10-22, 330-22-9, 330-68-6 and 340-16-18: at n <= 310 every weight-6 entry with k >= 20 had d <= 10 (and k = 20..30 only appeared at d <= 10 below n = 300). The hypothesis was that two-block group-algebra (2BGA) codes on split metacyclic groups C_l1 ⋊ C_l2 of order 150 to 200 could reach k = 20 at d = 12 below the [[336,24,12]] filed from the same campaign. [[310,20,12]] does: no board entry has n <= 310, k >= 20, d >= 12 and check weight <= 6, and the d - 2 margin holds ([[310,20,10]] is not dominated either).

What was searched

Random-support 2BGA codes H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] with a, b three group elements each, both containing the identity (no loss of generality). The group menu was every non-abelian group of order 150 to 200 the kit builds from Cayley-table constructors: dihedral D_n, metacyclic C_n ⋊_r C_k (up to two actions r per (n, k)), C_m x D_k, C_m x A4, C_m x S4, D_a x D_b, C_m x (C_n ⋊ C_k) for eight small metacyclic factors, and the split metacyclic presentations ZSZ(l1, l2, q), l2 <= 8, of research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py.

A generic weight-3 element is invertible on most irreducible blocks of F_2[G], so k >= 20 is rare: the sweep ran a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter over 400,000 draws (seed 62), of which 239 had k >= 20 and a connected Tanner graph. Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 31 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8). This code was drawn twice (two different support pairs on ZSZ(31,5,2), both [[310,20,12]] through 2,000,000 trials). The other survivors were [[336,20,16]], [[336,24,12]] and [[378,24,16]], filed separately, and points those dominate ([[360,24,12]], [[378,24,12]]).

Dead ends

  • Cyclic generalized-bicycle codes with a designed divisor g | x^m - 1 at

    • check weight 6 (weight-3 a and b): at odd m from 111 to 200 and deg g >= 8 there are essentially no weight-3 multiples of g; the few that exist are lifts from a subring Z_{m/s} and give direct sums.

  • Random-support bivariate bicycle codes at check weight 6: 200,000

    • prefiltered draws (185 with k >= 12) gave only points dominated by the 2BGA finds of this campaign.

  • Non-abelian lifted products with 1 x 2 bases and weight-(2,2) entries

    • (check weight 6) give k = |G| + 6 at d = 6, already dominated by the board's 258-88-6.

  • None of the 27 prefiltered 2BGA draws with k >= 28 (up to k = 48)

    • advanced the frontier even at 400 screen trials.

Reproduction

The group is research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py's zsz(31, 5, 2): C_31 ⋊ C_5 with y x = x^2 y (2 has order 5 mod 31), element x^i y^j at index 5 i + j, identity at 0; order 155, n = 310. With x = (1, 0), y = (0, 1):

a = 1 + x^20 + x^28 (indices 0, 100, 140), b = 1 + x^12 y^2 + x^25 y (indices 0, 62, 126).
