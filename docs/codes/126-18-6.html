[[126,18,6]] — 2BGA coset code on D9xC7 / H, check weight 6

Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-6 cell at n in 111..220, where the board's frontier for k >= 12 at d >= 8 is thin (frontier map of 2026-10-17). A (126, 18, 6) point at check weight 6 needed d >= 6 to clear every board entry with n' <= 126, k' >= 18, w' <= 6; the gate's novelty check reports it advances the cell on d, k, n (board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py; literature novelty unverified).

What was searched

Coset-code sweep over direct products D_m x C_k, metacyclic, A4/S4/A5/S5 x C_k, D_a x D_b with cyclic non-normal H of index 56..110 and |N_G(H)| >= 6. Funnel for this job: 894132 candidates built, 71129 distinct with k >= 10 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 6 promoted to 20,000 trials, 6 to 200,000 trials, 6 survived with the reading still above the board's weight-6 frontier (any reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).
