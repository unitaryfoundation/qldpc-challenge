Direction & hypothesis

Hackathon (issue #1155) weight-8 cell. The 2026-09-16 girth-cap fieldnote (fieldnotes/2026-09-16-lifted-product-girth-cap.md) capped one-row non-abelian lifted products with a weight-2 entry at d <= 8 for |G| <= 140, but left the (3,2)/(3,2) profile (check weight 8, rate 1/5) reaching d = 9 at n = 350, 390. A frontier scan showed d >= 8 at n = 5|G| for |G| in {63,64,66,68,72,74,76} are all board-advancing. This is a d = 9 point at |G| = 64 (n = 320).
