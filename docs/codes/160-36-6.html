[[160,36,6]] — non-abelian lifted product over ZSZ(8,4,5), check weight 6

Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-6 cell at n in 111..220, where the board's frontier for k >= 12 at d >= 8 is thin (frontier map of 2026-10-17). A (160, 36, 6) point at check weight 6 needed d >= 6 to clear every board entry with n' <= 160, k' >= 36, w' <= 6; the gate's novelty check reports it advances the cell on k, n (board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py; literature novelty unverified).

What was searched

Non-abelian lifted-product sweep over 1x2, 1x3 and 2x3 base shapes with entry weights 1..3 on dihedral, metacyclic, ZSZ and C_m x A4/S4/D_k groups. Funnel for this job: 44400 candidates built, 31235 distinct with k >= 10 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 2 promoted to 20,000 trials, 2 to 200,000 trials, 2 survived with the reading still above the board's weight-6 frontier (any reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).

Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 6: the lightest logical operator found is embedded in the submission (distance.X / distance.Z witnesses) and is checked by the verifier. Confirmation ladder on the box (gf2_fast, research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, 30 threads):

Dead ends

  • Random (undesigned) bivariate bicycle supports in this band: 250,000 weight-6 draws gave only

    • 204 codes with k >= 10 and none above the frontier at 400 trials; 230,000 weight-8 draws gave 3,529 with k >= 10 and 5 survivors at 200,000 trials.

  • Random 2BGA supports on 150 non-abelian groups of order 56..110 (43,800 weight-6 draws): 146 with

    • k >= 10, none above the frontier at 400 trials.

  • Designed-common-zero weight-6 bicycle codes (45,176 draws, 7,306 with k >= 10): all readings at or

    • below the frontier; the [[150,16,8]] cell is reproduced many times but never beaten.
