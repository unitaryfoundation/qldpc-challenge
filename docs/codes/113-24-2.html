Direction & hypothesis

Produced by campaign cc-deletion-peel-scaled-2026-11-18: the scaled sector-targeted deletion/peel/puncture sweep of code-calculus v0.3.0 over the board's own CSS parents (n <= 120, check weight <= 8; 889 parents). Cell aimed: weight-4 x unrestricted. Codes produced by deletion cannot raise a check weight, so a child of a weight-4 parent stays in the weight-4 class or lighter; the card aimed the route at the cells where kd^2/n was led by [[120,8,12]] (9.600) among CSS entries with w <= 6, n <= 120, and the weight-4 unrestricted cell was thin at high k. Hypothesis (the campaign card's): removal moves extend the v0.1 rounds' yield to depth-3 chains at recorded cost, and each new (n, k, d, w) Pareto point in a populated cell is a frontier extension; this entry advanced its cell on d+k+n.

What was searched

248 children over the first 81 parents of the pool (n = 108..120), each child exported, gated, and screened before its chain leaned on it. This entry is the step-2 child of the depth-3 chain rooted at codes/116-21-2.json: [[116,21,2]] -> [[115,22,2]] -> [[114,23,2]] -> [[113,24,2]]. Every step of the chain was gate-passed and board-advancing; the steps remove displayed checks (the planner's deletion move), so the check weight never rises along the chain (w = 4 throughout).

Evidence trail

Gate verdict (verify/validate_candidate.py, seed 11): passed, board-advancing in the weight-4 x unrestricted cell, advances_by d+k+n. Witnesses carried in the entry: X side upper bound 2 (witness [10, 14]), Z side upper bound 2 (witness [2, 5]); d = min = 2. Staged screening after the gate: the verifier's submit dry-run at 2,000 / 20,000 / 200,000 fast trials with seeds 1 and 2 reproduced the packaged distance at every rung (no downgrade). Final claim: witness-backed upper bounds on both sides, not exact.

Dead ends

51 of the 81 parents processed produced no child the gate would pass; the refusals were mostly exact duplicates of adjacent board entries (removing one displayed check re-derives a neighbour's display) or children dominated on (n, k, d, w) by the dense weight-8 population already on the board. 14 children were refused by the gate's own refutation search for over-claimed distances. 54 gate-passed children were dominated and recorded non-frontier, not submitted.
