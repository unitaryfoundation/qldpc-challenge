novelty novelty not audited
construction Random Clifford encoder (16-20 layers of random two-qubit Clifford gates on random qubit pairs) gives a parent [[74,34]] code; all of its logical operators of weight below 10 are enumerated and 5 logical operators are added as stabilizers (expurgation), chosen by solving over GF(2) so that each low-weight logical anticommutes with one of them. The generators listed here are a low-weight generating set of the same stabilizer group, found by an information-set search, to meet the weight-32 cap.
