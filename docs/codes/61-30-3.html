[[61,30,3]] — code-calculus k-gap fill (single-step child of [[62,21,6]])

Direction & hypothesis

k-gap mode of the reachability campaign: for an n already on the CSS board, find single-step children that raise k above the current maximum for that n. The planner (unitaryfoundation/code-calculus-dev @ 74889f1, v0.3.0) derives deletion/peel/puncture children of board parents; this child comes from [[62,21,6]] (codes/62-21-6.json) by one planner.plan step. Hypothesis: removal moves raise k fast enough to land new Pareto points, trading distance down.

What was searched

The campaign processed 1093 CSS parents with n <= 100, generated planner children, and staged only those whose k exceeds the current board maximum for that n (292 staged). Screening ran at 500 RIS trials per side with gf2_fast pre-filtering; this candidate's witnesses were then confirmed at 14500 trials per side survived (research/kit/surrogate.lightest_logical, seeds [0]). Siblings that screened to a lighter logical were dropped before gating.

Dead ends

The campaign's dominant failure mode is distance collapse: 222 of the 292 staged children witnessed to d = 1, and 10 of the 23 deeper ones were dominated at the gate (e.g. a d = 5 sibling dominated by [[80,32,6]]). Staged filenames carrying the planner ledger's optimistic bound routinely over-claim d relative to the witness; the witnessed value is the claim here.
