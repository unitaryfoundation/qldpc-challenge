[[936,76,10]] — weight-5 lifted product over F_2[Z_36 x|_19 Z_2] with 2x3 single-element base matrices

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6 (maximum check weight 5). The board's [[624,52,9]] is a lifted product with 2x3 base matrices whose entries are single group elements, so n = 13|G|, k = |G| + 4 and every check has weight 5 (three left-acting entries plus two right-acting ones). The hypothesis was that the same shape over slightly larger non-abelian groups keeps the distance at or above 9 while k grows with |G|; when this code was gated, the smallest distance that advanced the board at this (n, k, w) was 9.

What was searched

  • Lifted products from lifted_product_base in research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py

    • with 2x3 base matrices A (left regular representation) and B (right regular representation), every entry a single group element, over groups of order 47..76: ZSZ(l1, l2, q) metacyclic groups from zsz in the same module and the kit's small non-abelian groups. 6,744 connected candidates screened at 300 gf2_fast RIS trials (pair depth 8).

  • d = 10 appeared only at n = 780, 832 and 936. All seven candidates in these

    • three cells held d = 10 at 300, 5,000 and 300,000 trials; the best one per cell went to the gate and to fresh-seed ladders.

  • Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,

    • w down; equality counts as dominated).

Dead ends

  • Shape A = [g1, 1 + g2], B = [1 + g3, g4] (check weight 5, n = 5|G|) over

    • groups of order 121..200: d = 3 in all 2,848 candidates at 300 trials.

  • Abelian groups never exceeded d = 4 in the companion weight-6 sweep of the

    • same family (2,643 candidates), so the search kept to non-abelian groups.
