Direction & hypothesis

For a cyclic generalized-bicycle code on x^m - 1, k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), so the dimension follows from a polynomial gcd and can be set rather than waited for: build a divisor g of chosen degree and draw a and b as low-weight codewords of its ideal. Two mechanisms cap this family from opposite ends. A proper divisor t of m gives a quotient-fold ceiling of (m/t) d_t, which binds at low dimension, and the single-block cyclic code of dimension deg g carries a light logical inside one circulant block at high dimension. Degree 48 sits where both are weak here.

What was searched

m = 241 with ord_2(241) = 24, so the cyclotomic cosets of x^241 - 1 over GF(2) number 11 and the reachable divisor degrees are the subset sums of their sizes. The divisor used here is 2 cosets of total degree 48, chosen among the reachable picks at that degree to maximise the longest run of consecutive residues it leaves out. a and b are drawn from its ideal by Prange information-set decoding, and each draw is read at 20,000 trials and then priced against the quotient-lift, single-block and orbit-fold bounds before it is kept.

Draws with two even weights are rejected: (x + 1) divides a binary polynomial exactly when its weight is even, so two even weights would add the root-1 coset to the common gcd and land the draw one degree above the target. Here a has weight 11 and b has weight 11.

Evidence trail

deg gcd(a, x^241 - 1) = 48, deg gcd(b, x^241 - 1) = 48 and deg gcd(a, b, x^241 - 1) = 48, so the leak between the single-block kernels and k is 0.

A 24-seed gate at 2,000,000 information-set trials per seed settled at 48. The per-seed spread: 1 seed found 48, 4 seeds found 52, 8 seeds found 54, 11 seeds found 56. The single-block logicals form the cyclic code of dimension 48 whose zeros are the 193 residues the divisor leaves out; the longest run of consecutive residues among them is 49, so the Bose-Chaudhuri-Hocquenghem bound puts the lightest single-block logical at 50 or above, and a direct search confined to one circulant block at 12 seeds of 600,000 trials independently finds nothing below 62. The orbit fold over every equal-orbit partition of the qubits returns nothing lighter than the claim.

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, not certified. Both witnesses are in the submitted JSON, commute with the opposite check matrix and lie outside its row space; the X witness weighs 52 and the Z witness weighs 48, and the claim is the lighter of the two.

Dead ends

  • Nothing else from this front survived its gate.
  • m = 241 was added late, from a ranking of every reachable design point by the longest unpicked run of its coset pick. It is one of ten primes under the blocklength cap with a workable ord_2, and at a prime modulus every quotient fold is vacuous.
  • Degree 24 at this modulus has a run of 70 against a cell threshold of 32 and is the widest structural headroom of any point tried; the present code sits at degree 48, whose run is 49, so its single block admits logicals as light as 50 and the search rather than the structure is what limits it.
