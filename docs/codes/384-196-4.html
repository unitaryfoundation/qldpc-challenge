Direction & hypothesis

weight-any x unrestricted. A 51%-rate color code: nothing at n<=384 combines k>=196 with d>=4 in the any-weight cell (nearest: [[376,190,4]] at k=190, w=8; [[372,130,16]] trades down to k=130 for d=16).

What was searched

27 finite quotients of the triangle groups △(r,s,t) and the quadrilateral groups □(r,s,t,u) (arXiv:2610.03682's L=1 families), enumerated exhaustively/sample-wise as transitive generating tuples of involutions in S_deg for degrees 6-9 with exact corner-rotation orders (the smooth-surface condition), built into CSS codes, verified against kit css.verify_css, and screened at 400 RIS trials (numpy backend). 12 quotients fell in the n=200-1000 band, 6 were Pareto-non-dominated in their cell against all 1940 CSS board entries and were staged; each survivor then ran a fresh-seed confirmation ladder (500k + 3x1M trials per side, gf2_fast accelerator) before promotion.

Reproduction

Permutations of {0..7} written as image tuples (images of 0,1,..., in order): a=[0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 4, 7, 6], b=[0, 1, 4, 6, 2, 7, 3, 5], c=[4, 6, 3, 2, 0, 5, 1, 7]. Group them: G = <a, b, c> under function composition has order 384, and the corner-rotation orders are exactly (ab, bc, ca) = (6, 6, 6), which is also the freeness (smooth-surface) condition. Tiles are the 384 elements of G; a qubit sits on each tile. Corner c of tile g is identified with corner c of tile g∘s for the two sides s adjacent to that corner (corner c lies on sides c and c+1 mod 3), so each vertex's stabilizer support is the set of tiles of its corners. Both an X and a Z stabilizer are placed on every vertex (supports are identical; pairwise intersections are 0 or 2 tiles, so H_X H_Z^T = 0 automatically). n=384; k = n - rank(H_X) - rank(H_Z) over GF(2). The staged entry's exact matrices are the authority; this recipe rebuilds them deterministically.
