Direction & hypothesis

For a cyclic generalized-bicycle code on x^m - 1, k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), so the dimension follows from a polynomial gcd and can be set rather than waited for: build a divisor g of chosen degree and draw a and b as low-weight codewords of its ideal. Two mechanisms cap this family from opposite ends. A proper divisor t of m gives a quotient-fold ceiling of (m/t) d_t, which binds at low dimension, and the single-block cyclic code of dimension deg g carries a light logical inside one circulant block at high dimension. Degree 49 sits where both are weak here.

What was searched

m = 241 with ord_2(241) = 24, so the cyclotomic cosets of x^241 - 1 over GF(2) number 11 and the reachable divisor degrees are the subset sums of their sizes. The divisor used here is 3 cosets of total degree 49, chosen among the reachable picks at that degree to maximise the longest run of consecutive residues it leaves out. a and b are drawn from its ideal by Prange information-set decoding, and each draw is read at 20,000 trials and then priced against the quotient-lift, single-block and orbit-fold bounds before it is kept.

The divisor carries the root-1 coset, so both polynomial weights are even by construction and (x + 1) divides the common gcd by design rather than by accident. Here a has weight 12 and b has weight 12.

Evidence trail

deg gcd(a, x^241 - 1) = 49, deg gcd(b, x^241 - 1) = 49 and deg gcd(a, b, x^241 - 1) = 49, so the leak between the single-block kernels and k is 0.

A 24-seed gate at 2,000,000 information-set trials per seed settled at 51. The per-seed spread: 1 seed found 51, 1 seed found 53, 4 seeds found 54, 12 seeds found 55, 6 seeds found 56. The single-block logicals form the cyclic code of dimension 49 whose zeros are the 192 residues the divisor leaves out; the longest run of consecutive residues among them is 36, so the Bose-Chaudhuri-Hocquenghem bound puts the lightest single-block logical at 37 or above, and a direct search confined to one circulant block at 12 seeds of 600,000 trials independently finds nothing below 57. The orbit fold over every equal-orbit partition of the qubits returns nothing lighter than the claim. A single uninterrupted run of 8,000,000 trials, matching the depth the verifier itself reaches, reads 55.

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, not certified. Both witnesses are in the submitted JSON, commute with the opposite check matrix and lie outside its row space; the X witness weighs 51 and the Z witness weighs 54, and the claim is the lighter of the two.
