[[640,24,24]] CLP code, check weight 6

Primary reference: arXiv:2610.12782, *Coset Lifted Product Codes*, Rajpurohit, Lalitha, Garani. This parameter set is not in the current version of the paper; it comes from the same search extended to longer codes and will appear in the updated version, with the data in https://github.com/rajendrarajpurohit/CLP_QLDPC (folder large_m/640_24_24).

Direction and hypothesis

The paper's search covered 120 <= n <= 400 (m = [G:H] from 30 to 100). The question was whether the same construction scales: does the 1x1 over 2x2 layout at check weight 6 keep its distance growing when m goes to 120 to 250, that is n from 480 to 1000, and where do such codes sit on this board's weight-6 frontier.

What was searched

25,240 pairs (G,H), H cyclic of order 2 to 9, core free, [G:N_G(H)] in {2,3,4}, [N_G(H):H] >= 4, from SmallGroups of order 240 to 2000 (orders with more than 2000 groups skipped). 1,500 random draws per pair of a with |a| = 3 and B with entry weights 2 1 / 1 2 or 1 2 / 2 1, then 20,000 draws per pair on the 6,836 pairs with 560 <= n <= 700 whose best k was at least 16, logging any draw whose (n, k, d) was not Pareto dominated by the weight-6 cell of this board on 24 Oct 2026. Screening ladder: 50 and 500 QDistRnd rounds with an early abort at the required distance, then 20,000, then 200,000 on both sides.

This code

G = SmallGroup(800,453), H = <f6> = C5, m = 160. a = 1 + f3*f5*f6^2*f7^2 + f2*f3*f6; B = [[1 + f4*f7, 1], [1, f1*f4*f6*f7 + f1*f5*f6^3*f7^3]].

Dead ends

Shapes with |a| = 2 or |a| = 4 (B entry weights up to 2 2 / 2 2) raise k to 24 and far beyond at these lengths but over 9,560 pairs never paired that k with a distance above the frontier for its k. Bounds from the 200,000 round stage overestimate by 2 to 16 at n near 1000; only the 10^6 figures are reported.
