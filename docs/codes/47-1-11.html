Direction & hypothesis

Target the unrestricted weight-6 general-stabilizer cell by varying inversion-symmetric cyclic supports. At upstream ff48155b8897e6796950c64d860e9102d59c36e9 this entry extends the Pareto frontier: no existing entry dominates it. What was searched

The completed campaign examined 4,151 unique seed instances across 4,526 runs: complete normalized two-color odd supports through n=53, sampled odd n=55..101 and even n=24..80, and a connected k=3 branch. The normalized seed has X support {1,-1} and Z support {u,-u,v,-v}; screening used randomized information-set search, followed by exhaustive checks for selected survivors. Initial odd screening used seed 100301 and 50 RIS trials; refinement used seed 100400 plus the candidate index and 100 trials. Search success alone is not a lower bound. Evidence trail

PROVED/VERIFIED externally: stabilizer rank 46, k=1, commuting cyclic generators of Pauli weight 6. A complete cyclic-orbit radius-5 meet-in-the-middle enumeration processed 40,911,898 representatives (identity plus every weight-at-most-5 Pauli containing site 0). Keys use the minimum cyclic syndrome and global X/Z parity labels. These labels are invariant under shifts and form the two logical commutation bits because n is odd and the stabilizer rank is n-1. No equal-syndrome pair had different labels, excluding every nontrivial logical of weight at most 10. The stored weight-11 witness supplies the opposite bound.
