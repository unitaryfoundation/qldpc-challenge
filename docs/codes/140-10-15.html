[[140,10,15]] — bivariate bicycle code on Z_7 x Z_10, check weight 8

Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-8 cell at n in 111..220, where the board's frontier for k >= 12 at d >= 8 is thin (frontier map of 2026-10-17). A (140, 10, 15) point at check weight 8 needed d >= 15 to clear every board entry with n' <= 140, k' >= 10, w' <= 8; the gate's novelty check reports it advances the cell on d, k, n (board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py; literature novelty unverified).

What was searched

Random bivariate bicycle sweep over every torus Z_l x Z_m with l*m in 56..110, A and B random 4- and 4-term polynomials each containing 1. Funnel for this job: 230000 candidates built, 3529 distinct with k >= 10 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 9 promoted to 20,000 trials, 5 to 200,000 trials, 5 survived with the reading still above the board's weight-8 frontier (any reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).

Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 15: the lightest logical operator found is embedded in the submission (distance.X / distance.Z witnesses) and is checked by the verifier. Confirmation ladder on the box (gf2_fast, research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, 30 threads):

Dead ends

  • Random (undesigned) bivariate bicycle supports in this band: 250,000 weight-6 draws gave only

  • Random 2BGA supports on 150 non-abelian groups of order 56..110 (43,800 weight-6 draws): 146 with
