[[640,128,3]] — Lifted-product code over F2[C32] with full-rank augmentation

Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: weight-6 x unrestricted (CSS), at w = 5 (n = 640, k = 128). Following the power-of-two group regularity principle of arXiv:2610.06820 (*Designing Group-Valued Codes with Full Regular Low-Weight Bases*), a lifted-product (LP) code over a power-of-two order group $G = C_{32}$ whose base matrices $A, B$ have full row rank over $\mathbb{F}_2$ under the augmentation map $g \mapsto 1$ guarantees a full regular canonical basis. Using a $(2 \times 4) \times (2 \times 4)$ monomial protograph with row weight 3 and column weight 2 yields w = 5, n = 20|G| = 640, and k = 4|G| = 128, where d >= 2 advances the weight-6 x unrestricted frontier.

What was searched

Random monomial assignments on the $(2 \times 4) \times (2 \times 4)$ protograph [[1, 1, 1, 0], [0, 1, 1, 1]] over $\mathbb{F}_2[C_{32}]$, screened with gf2_fast at 5,000 trials (seed = 2).

Evidence trail

  • Screen (5,000 gf2_fast trials): d <= 3.

  • Gate (verify/validate_candidate.py): passed: true, board_advancing: true, exact_duplicate_of: null, wl_equivalent_of: null, no lighter logical found in 8,000 RIS refutation trials.
  • Final claim: witness-backed upper_bound, d <= 3.

Dead ends

  • $(1 \times 3) \times (1 \times 3)$ LP protographs with weight-1 and weight-2 entries over power-of-two groups collapsed to d <= 3 at w = 7..8 (dominated on weight-8).
  • w = 6 $(2 \times 4) \times (2 \times 4)$ LP protographs over D16 and Dic4 topped out at d = 4 (below the w = 6 frontier threshold).
