[[320,16,24]] — weight-8 bivariate bicycle code on Z_40 x Z_4

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 300 and 340. Before this filing the weight-8 frontier there held 292-22-23, 294-24-20, 300-12-25, 300-32-15, 306-26-19, 308-10-26, 310-12-26 and 336-24-24: at n <= 320 no entry with k >= 16 reached d = 24 (the best was 292-22-23), and every entry with d >= 24 had k <= 12. The hypothesis was that four-plus-four-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on rectangular tori Z_l x Z_m with 2lm in that range, which the board covers far less densely than the weight-6 BB family, could put a k = 16, d = 24 point there. [[320,16,24]] does: no board entry has n <= 320, k >= 16, d >= 24 and check weight <= 8, and the k - 2 margin holds as well ([[320,14,24]] is not dominated either).

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A and B four monomials each, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 222 <= 2lm <= 400, the torus drawn uniformly from that list and the other three monomials of each polynomial uniformly from the torus.

Random supports rarely give k >= 16, so the sweep ran a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter: 100,000 draws (seed 55), of which 449 had k >= 16 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 7 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing. Five of the seven collapsed at 20,000 trials to points dominated by 252-20-21, 292-22-23, 336-24-24 or 378-18-27 (screen-depth bounds of 32 to 40 at n = 360 to 392 fell to 20 to 24). The two survivors were this code and a [[240,20,18]] on Z_20 x Z_6, which is filed separately.

Dead ends

  • Random-support BB at check weight 8 above n = 340: every screen-depth

    • survivor with k >= 16 fell below the 336-24-24 / 378-18-27 / 372-14-29 staircase by 20,000 trials.

  • Cyclic generalized-bicycle codes with a designed divisor g | x^m - 1,

    • weight-4 a and b (check weight 8): weight-4 multiples of g exist only for deg g <= ~22 at odd m from 111 to 200, and 4,000 such draws at 300 <= n <= 400 gave nothing advancing even at 400 trials.

  • Random-support 2BGA on non-abelian groups at check weight 8, 16,000
