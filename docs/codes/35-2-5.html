Direction & hypothesis

Weight-4 unrestricted CSS cell. Transform a connected CSS parent (36-2-6.json, a board entry) with the priced construction calculus to obtain a connected child that is not dominated by any existing entry in its track cell.

What was searched

Single-step deletion (C-DELETE-CHECK), peel (E-PEEL), and puncture (E-PUNCTURE) moves enumerated with machine.planner.plan from github.com/MathysRennela/code-calculus-dev @ 71c1adf (machine/priced_calculus.py, machine/planner.py). Parent: codes/36-2-6.json. Rule used for this survivor: E-PEEL. Connected children only; bare-qubit children and dominated candidates dropped. Witness search at 1000 RIS trials per side (research/kit/surrogate.lightest_logical, seeds 0/1), then verify/validate_candidate.py with full refutation.
