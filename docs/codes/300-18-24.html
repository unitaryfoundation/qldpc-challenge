[[300,18,24]] — weight-8 bivariate bicycle code on Z_30 x Z_5

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted (maximum check weight 8), CSS board, n between 221 and 400. Before this code, the frontier (board plus this campaign's open PRs) at check weight <= 8, d >= 12 and n from 240 to 300 ran through 240-10-22, 240-18-20, 240-24-17, 240-28-16, 248-16-22, 248-24-18, 248-66-12, 252-20-21, 252-22-19, 252-26-18, 256-68-12, 264-70-12. The hypothesis was that random 4 + 4-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on tori with 2lm in 300..400, prefiltered for k >= 18, still reach points there that the board does not hold.

[[300,18,24]] does: no board entry has n <= 300, k >= 18, d >= 24 and check weight <= 8. It is not a d-only gain (no board entry has the same n, k and weight with a lower d).

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A of 4 and B of 4 monomials, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 300 <= 2lm <= 400; the torus is drawn uniformly from that list and the other monomials uniformly from the torus.

This sweep (seed 604) drew 400,020 codes in a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter; 946 had k >= 18 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 22 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and 21 were taken to 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing; 2 survived that and were staged.

Dead ends

  • In this sweep 19 of 21 screen-depth survivors collapsed below the frontier by 2,000,000 trials: 400-trial readings run several units high at this n.
  • Earlier in this campaign on the same band: cyclic generalized-bicycle codes with a designed divisor at weights (3,3) and (4,4), random-support 2BGA on non-abelian groups at check weight 8, non-abelian lifted products, and weight-6 BB/2BGA at k >= 28 gave nothing that survived 20,000 trials on the frontier.
  • Random 2 + 6 splits at check weight 8 never reached k >= 18 with an advancing distance.
