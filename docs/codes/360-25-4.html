[[360,25,4]] even-d multiband dense-packed surface code (d=4, rows=3, m=13, pitch=2)

Direction & hypothesis

Target the local-2d-single / weight-4 frontier at d = 4, k = 25 via the even-d (rows = 3, pitch = d - 2) regime of research/multiband_surface.py (Open Problem 4 of fieldnotes/2026-09-01-multiband-dense-packing-method.md). Because the even-d law n(4, m) = 28m - 4, k = 2m - 1 saves 10m - 8 qubits relative to punctured d = 5 two-band codes (38m - 14), the qubit savings grow linearly with m.

What was searched

Evaluated the d = 4, rows = 3, pitch = 2 ladder across m in {2..33} in research/multiband_surface.py and compared against the verified board in local-2d-single / weight-4. At m = 13, the construction yields [[360,25,4]] (kd^2/n = 1.111, max check weight 4, single-layer 2D coordinates).

Evidence trail

Confirmation ladder for the submitted code, each rung a fresh seed unless marked otherwise, reading the lightest logical found:

| budget (trials/side) | lightest logical | | ---: | ---: | | 20,000 (NumPy RIS witness) | 4 | | 200,000 (fast RIS seed 101) | 4 | | 200,000 (fast RIS seed 102) | 4 |

3 fresh-seed rung(s) agree at the best bound, the deepest of them at 200,000 trials per side. The submitted witness reproduces it: X: d <= 4 (upper_bound), Z: d <= 4 (upper_bound).

Also checked with verify/sat_certify.py (CryptoMiniSat 5.14 SAT, UNSAT below weight 4 on both sides in 0.19 s); claimed as a witness-backed upper bound.
