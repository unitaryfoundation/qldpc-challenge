[[856,218,20]] pair-partition CPM lift at P=107

Direction & hypothesis

fieldnotes/2026-09-20-screening-traps-at-n-900.md closed the top of the (3,8) pair-partition window — the P=113 draw reads 22 at the 20,000-trial screen and measures 20 at every deeper rung — while leaving open both the d=21 question for P in 97..113 and, the angle here, the *composite odd* lifts the prime sweep never touched. Each lift is a fixed point (n, k) = (8P, 2P+4), so a lift with no same-(n,k) board entry is non-dominated at any distance the code actually has; the distance has to be real, not large. Against the full board, [[856,218]] is non-dominated from d >= 4 upward, which is why this is an (n, k) advance and not a distance claim: the entry does not depend on the witnessed bound being big.

What was searched

250 raw draws per lift of the 36-equation matching system over F_107 (seed stream 130 = 23 + P), keeping only draws with no 4- or 6-cycle in either exponent array, each screened at 20,000 RIS trials; 36 survived at P=107 (all CSS, max check weight 8). Every survivor was tested against the board's (n, k, d, w) Pareto cells and none was dominated. One draw per lift was carried forward: draw=43, highest screen reading at (856,218).
