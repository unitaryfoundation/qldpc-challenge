Direction & hypothesis

Every cyclic generalized-bicycle code I have submitted fixed its dimension by a residue construction, which pins k between 20 and 34 and leaves the score kd^2/n capped near 250 whatever the distance. The board's best entry in this family, codes/682-142-82.json, reaches 1400 instead with k = 142, and its construction names the mechanism: a divisor ideal of degree 71. Since k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), choosing a divisor of x^m - 1 and drawing a and b from its ideal selects the dimension rather than inheriting it.

Two facts make the construction work, both checked rather than assumed. x^m - 1 is squarefree over GF(2) only for odd m, which rules out the even moduli the residue sweep uses. And the usable a and b are the low-weight codewords of the cyclic code generated by the divisor, typically lighter than the divisor itself: in codes/682-142-82.json the divisor has weight 28 while its a and b have weight 16.

What was searched

For m = 315 the divisor is built as a product of cyclotomic-coset minimal polynomials over GF(2^12), so its degree is chosen; sampling sparse polynomials and taking gcds with x^m - 1 instead lands on a target degree almost never and kept 0 of 12 draws. The polynomials a and b are then found by Prange information-set decoding inside the resulting ideal. Sweeping the ratio deg g / m from 0.04 to 0.30 over many coset subsets produced 18 Pareto-distinct candidates at m = 315, of which 14 survived the symmetric fold of research/foldbound.py.

Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound with witnesses on both sides, and the two sides came from different instruments because only one of them can reach the binding operator.

research/foldbound.py, uncapped, 1e6/fold X: 63 (2 blocks, t = 5, orbit 63) random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6 Z: 90 (minimum 90, median 94) verify/validate_candidate.py passed, not refuted

The weight-63 X operator is not reachable by random information-set decoding. Twenty-four seeds at 2,000,000 trials each bottom out at weight 90, twenty-seven above the claim, and research/kit-style confirmation by escalating RIS consumed a 3,000 second budget without finding anything at all. The fold exhibits it directly, and it was re-verified against the committed matrices here: in the kernel of H_Z, outside the row space of H_X, weight recounted. The Z witness at weight 90 is the lightest the scan found on that side. Since d = min over sides, the submitted distance is 63.

Dead ends

The high-dimension corner the board leader occupies does not tolerate an arbitrary divisor. At deg g / m = 0.20 a random coset subset gave [[630,126,18]], dominated by codes/444-154-18.json, and at 0.25 and higher the distance collapsed outright, to 7 and then 5. The leader sits at 0.21 with d = 82, so a good subset exists there, but the subset choice is the whole problem and random selection at that ratio is worthless. The productive band in this sweep was 0.04 to 0.16.

Triage figures from this construction cannot be trusted. Ranking candidates by a 20,000-trial RIS reading overstated one by a factor of five: a claimed kd^2/n of 571.4 became 115.7 once folded. Every candidate in the batch lost distance at every increase in search depth, one going 106, then 100, then 96 as trials went from 20,000 to 200,000 to 2,000,000. This code is the one that survived the full ladder.
