[[43,1,13]] — cyclic XYZ stabilizer code at n=43, k=1

Direction & hypothesis

Target: stabilizer, weight-9plus × unrestricted, a non-CSS code with n small enough to be tractable and d high. The cyclic XYZ construction of arXiv:2611.30654v1 (Baldelli et al.) builds stabilizer codes whose three Pauli-type check spaces are cyclic, with a single uncovered cyclotomic factor giving k = 1. For n = 43 the three nonzero cosets are all self-negating, so the construction is reversible and Pauli-symmetric; the paper reports d = 13, matching the best known stabilizer distance for (n,k) = (43,1).

What was searched

Implemented the construction for n = 43: factored x^43 - 1 over F_2, assigned the three degree-14 irreducible factors to X, Y, Z, formed the circulant parity checks H_X, H_Y, H_Z, and assembled the stabilizer generators (X-type, Y-type with identical X/Z support, Z-type). One candidate was built and gated; no sweep over other partitions was performed for this n (the three factors are forced to distinct types, so the construction is unique up to Pauli relabeling).

Reproduction

n = 43. Factor x^43 - 1 over F_2 into M_0 = x+1 and three self-reciprocal degree-14 factors M_1, M_3, M_7. Set f_X = M_1, f_Y = M_3, f_Z = M_7 (any permutation gives an equivalent code up to Pauli relabeling). Then H_σ is the 43 × 43 circulant matrix whose rows are the cyclic shifts of the coefficients of h_σ = (x^43-1)/f_σ; take the first 14 rows of each. Generators: X-type rows of H_X as pure X; Z-type rows of H_Z as pure Z; Y-type rows of H_Y as Y (same X and Z support). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30654v1 .
