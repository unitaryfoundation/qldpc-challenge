Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted (maximum check weight 8), CSS board, n between 221 and 400. Before this code, the frontier (board plus this campaign's open PRs) at check weight <= 8, d >= 14 and n from 280 to 340 ran through 288-12-24, 292-22-23, 294-24-20, 300-12-25, 300-32-15, 306-26-19, 308-10-26, 310-12-26, 330-10-27, 336-24-24, 336-28-20. The hypothesis was that random 3 + 5-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on tori with 2lm in 222..400, prefiltered for k >= 16, still reach points there that the board does not hold.

[[340,16,28]] does: no board entry has n <= 340, k >= 16, d >= 28 and check weight <= 8. It is not a d-only gain (no board entry has the same n, k and weight with a lower d).

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A of 3 and B of 5 monomials, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 222 <= 2lm <= 400; the torus is drawn uniformly from that list and the other monomials uniformly from the torus.

This sweep (seed 603) drew 400,020 codes in a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter; 205 had k >= 16 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 12 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and 12 were taken to 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing; 2 survived that and were staged.
