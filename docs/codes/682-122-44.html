Direction & hypothesis

Target: the best kd²/n among codes of check weight at most 16 (and 18, 20). The leader at w ≤ 16 was codes/674-86-56.json ([[674,86,87]], w = 16, kd²/n = 965.8), a cyclic generalized bicycle over Z_337; at w ≤ 20 it was codes/682-122-52.json (1173.7, w = 20). The Z_341 Frobenius family (see notes/682-172-52-b.md and notes/682-132-64.md) had so far been worked at weights 10–16 per polynomial (w = 20–32); the question was whether weight-8 words (w = 16) still carry shared divisors of degree ≥ 41 (k ≥ 82) with distances in the 80s.

The family: a(x) over Z_341 invariant under j → 32 j (mod 341), so each full-order decic of x^341 − 1 divides a with probability 2⁻⁵ instead of 2⁻¹⁰; b = a(x⁴) has the same zero set, the pair shares the divisor and k = 2 deg gcd(a, x^341 − 1).

What was searched
