[[720,74,8]]: double cover of the {4,5} surface code of the map R19.3

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: check weight at most 6 (here 5 and 4), unrestricted locality (no layout submitted). The {4,5} surface codes of regular maps reach d = 8 at [[360,38,8]], but the next {4,5} maps of the census with d >= 8 are [[660,68,8]], [[960,98,8]] and [[1280,130,8]], so there are large gaps in n. A two-sheeted cyclic cover doubles V, E and F, so k = 2 - 2 chi_0 = 2 k_0 - 2 for the base's chi_0 and k_0 = 38. A shortest nontrivial cycle of the base graph with voltage 0 lifts to a nontrivial cycle of the same length (the projection maps boundaries to boundaries), while a nontrivial cycle of the cover may project to a trivial one, so a double cover can lose distance. Hypothesis: for suitable voltages it keeps d = 8, and double covers of [[360,38,8]] give [[720,74,8]] at check weight 5.

What was searched

A census of all regular maps of type {p,q}, 3 <= p,q <= 8, 1/p + 1/q < 1/2, with at most 2000 edges (chiral {8,8} maps up to 1000 edges): the normal subgroups of index 2E of the triangle groups <r,s | r^p, s^q, (rs)^2> in which r, s, rs keep their orders (Magma's LowIndexNormalSubgroups), the non-orientable maps from the extended triangle groups, and 2000 cyclic covers of the orientable maps with n <= 1000 (random voltage classes, seeds recorded). In total 2174 orientable and 340 non-orientable maps (up to duality and mirror image) and 2000 covers, 7063 codes with their X/Z duals, each with exact d_X and d_Z by the programs below. At board commit 8042f9da, 254 parameter points of the census were undominated; we submit nine of them, one per pull request, and left out the points we found in the literature: [[896,386,6]] ({7,7}) and [[960,194,8]] ({5,5}) are data points of Fig. 3.7 of Breuckmann's thesis (arXiv:1802.01520), [[588,254,6]] and [[273,119,5]] are in its Table 3.1. This code is a double cover of the {4,5} map R19.3 (voltage in the construction of the entry). All ten sampled double covers of R19.3 (inequivalent voltage classes up to the automorphisms of the base map) are [[720,74,8]]; we submit the first. Sources checked without finding it: Breuckmann and Terhal (arXiv:1506.04029), Breuckmann et al. (arXiv:1703.00590), Breuckmann's thesis (arXiv:1802.01520), Mahmoud and Rayan (arXiv:2610.10948), Breuckmann and Burton (arXiv:2202.06647), Mahmoud, Ali and Rayan (arXiv:2504.07800), the catalogs of QEC-pages (Quantum_LDPC_Codes) and qecdb.org; Conder's lists contain the maps, not the codes.

Evidence trail

  • k = 74 = 720 - 287 - 359 (ranks over GF(2)) = 2 - chi; H_X H_Z^T = 0; every qubit lies in two X-checks and two Z-checks, so both sides are graph-like (computed).
  • Upper bound: an X-type and a Z-type logical operator of weight 8, in the entry.
  • Lower bound, exact, by four computations: (1) graphlike_A, shortest cycles with logical annotations in F_2^k; (2) graphlike_B, shortest paths in the double covers of the check graph given by the logical operators, written independently; their exactness is proved in github.com/TimoKellerMath/quantum-error-correction @ 2e97a5361e7b9327128baa242bdedd333f7d7e12, hyperbolic-map-codes/src/graphlike_A/NOTES.md and hyperbolic-map-codes/src/graphlike_B/NOTES.md, and they agree on all 7063 census codes; (3) an enumeration of all circuits of length at most 8 with a row-space test: the graph of the map has girth 4: its 360 circuits of length 4 and 720 of length 6 are all trivial, 6108 of the 8268 circuits of length 8 are nontrivial (Z-type logical operators of weight 8); the dual graph has 288 circuits of length 5, all trivial, and 100 of its 820 circuits of length 8 are nontrivial; (4) tropidist (Cheng, Guo and Pan, arXiv:2611.08247) at the board's pinned commit b03ec2d4077f, one thread, 600 s limit, on the board's css-sides problems: exact 8 and 8 in 1.9 s and 1.8 s, recorded in certs/720-74-8.json.
  • Final claim: d_X = d_Z = 8, exact (computer-assisted; the four computations agree). 100 X-type and 6108 Z-type logical operators have weight 8.

Dead ends

  • Within the census no {8,8}, {6,6} or {7,7} map reaches d = 7; d >= 7 occurs only for {3,7}, {4,5}, {4,6} and {5,5} (n <= 2000).
  • Of the 321 cyclic covers of {4,5} maps in the census (n <= 1000), only the ten double covers of R19.3 reach d = 8; the others have d <= 6.
  • Random cyclic covers rarely raise the distance: 122 of the 2000 covers have a larger d than their base, 1827 keep both side distances of the base.

Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code). Magma V2.30 for the census (LowIndexNormalSubgroups, coset actions, the presentations and Conder's names); Python for the map codes; the C programs graphlike_A and graphlike_B and the Python circuit enumeration, all in the public package below; tropidist b03ec2d4077f. The largest Magma run of the census (orientable {6,6} maps, index at most 4000) took 33 CPU minutes; exact distances of all 7063 codes took 46 s with graphlike_A and 76 s with graphlike_B.
