Direction & hypothesis

2D-local bilayer weight-8 CSS cell, through the layout inherited from the parent. The priced construction calculus was applied one move deep to every CSS entry on the board, and this code is the E-PUNCTURE of codes/96-20-6.json ([[96,20,6]], max check weight 7; a weight-7 generalized bicycle code contributed by Mathys Rennela). Puncturing qubit 44, which removes it together with every check touching it, raises k from 20 to 21 and lowers the witnessed distance bound from 6 to 5 while dropping one qubit. Max check weight stays 7. The layout is the parent's with the punctured qubit removed; the verifier re-measured its radius and layer count.

What was searched

Every C-DELETE-CHECK, E-PEEL, and E-PUNCTURE move on each of the 2,783 CSS board entries as of 2026-11-02, 1,684,787 moves in all, with the consumer at github.com/unitaryfoundation/code-calculus @ 1b9a942. Children with a bare qubit, a disconnected Tanner graph, a Weisfeiler-Leman signature equal to a board entry or to an earlier child, or parameters already dominated inside the parent's cell were dropped before any search, leaving 334,191 distinct children. Children with n <= 60 and an inherited distance bound of at least 2, and children with 61 <= n <= 100 and a bound of at least 5, were screened with research/kit/surrogate.lightest_logical at 2,000 RIS trials per side; the survivors the kit's novelty pre-check called board-advancing were staged with research/kit/submit.py and gated with verify/validate_candidate.py, one representative per distinct (n, k, d, max weight, locality) point. This code is that representative for [[95,21,5]] at max check weight 7. The thirteen points audited first went to the board on 2026-11-04 (PRs #4588 to #4600); this one is from the remainder, re-checked against the board of 2026-11-04 with the gate's structural, deduplication, and novelty stages before the ladder below was run. The screen distance is a witness-backed upper bound; the rungs below are the independent confirmation runs on the staged file.
