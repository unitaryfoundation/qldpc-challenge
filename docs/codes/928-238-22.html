[[928,238,22]] — pair-partition CPM (3,8) code at the even lift P = 116, with k = 2P + 6

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: CSS, unrestricted, weight-8, extended tier (n <= 1000 with w <= 8 and d <= 40). In the (3,8) pair-partition CPM family of Okada and Kasai (arXiv:2607.14091) n = 8P and, generically, k = 2P + 4. Our codes/976-250-22.json gets k = 2P + 6 at P = 122 by fixing the free design-system exponents to a residue class mod 2. Hypothesis: the same step works mod 4 when 4 divides P, and a smaller lift with k = 2P + 6 and d = 22 scores higher: at P = 116, 238 * 22^2 / 928 = 124.13.

Inspired by: codes/976-250-22.json (ours): the residue-class step and the screening pipeline. codes/904-230-22.json and codes/712-182-20.json by @vprusso (found with Claude Fable 5.1): the (3,8) pattern at large lifts, the matchings M0, M1, M2 used here, and the polishing of the 8-cycle count. Lifts beyond the paper's P = 73 start with codes/632-162-18.json by @vprusso; the girth-8 hard filter follows codes/808-206-20.json by @pandey-tushar. The score formula and the lift cap at n <= 1000 are from fieldnotes/2026-09-20-screening-traps-at-n-900.md by @e-eight.

Technique (not ours): the pair-partition construction, its design system and the matchings are Okada and Kasai's (arXiv:2607.14091); quasi-cyclic CSS LDPC codes from circulant permutation matrices go back to Hagiwara and Imai (arXiv:quant-ph/0701020); @vprusso's entries also cite the lifted-product construction of Panteleev and Kalachev (arXiv:2111.03654). The exact distance search uses Okada and Kasai's cpm_distance tool (github.com/kasaikenta/pair-partition-cpm-css-codes, MIT licence).

Board precedents: other (3,8) weight-8 entries of this family: codes/976-250-22.json (ours, k = 2P + 6 at P = 122), codes/776-198-20.json and codes/664-170-18.json by @vprusso, codes/824-210-20.json, codes/792-202-20.json, codes/840-214-20.json, codes/856-218-20.json, codes/872-222-18.json and codes/888-226-18.json by @MathysRennela, codes/568-146-18.json by @nihoniumqc, and the published codes/584-150-18.json (Okada and Kasai). Even lifts at k = 2P + 4: codes/256-68-12.json and codes/576-148-18.json by @MathysRennela (arrays printed in Maskara et al., arXiv:2610.06749). The rank of a quasi-cyclic matrix is set by its common factors with x^P - 1, which has repeated factors over GF(2) when P is even (x^116 - 1 = (x^29 - 1)^4), so the extra logical qubits are not claimed as a new effect.

Our contribution: the residue-class step taken mod 4 (Q = 4, the {2,3}-part of P), with the class chosen from the quotient lift over Z_4; this draw; and the distance screening below.

What was searched

At 11 lifts in 104..124 (P = 104, 108, 112, 114, 116, 117, 118, 120, 122, 123, 124), with Q the {2,3}-part of P, the free exponents' residues mod Q were fixed to a class whose quotient lift over Z_Q has k_Q >= 2Q + 4 + 2 (a quick check of 17 lifts confirmed that the full lift keeps this bonus), and the rest of the exponents were annealed on the 8-cycle count (15000 moves, girth 8 and a connected Tanner graph kept). 3154 annealed codes (counts below are a snapshot of the running scan, 2026-10-14). A fold filter of ours (quotient codes over proper subgroups, heuristic RIS on each quotient, lift the lightest word; keep lightest fold >= 22) kept 2106. A Stern screen, 20 s per side (the Z side skipped once the X side found a logical of weight <= 21), on those read 8 (x1), 12 (x94), 14 (x100), 16 (x649), 18 (x308), 20 (x950) and 22 (x4): two at P = 116 (k = 238) and two at P = 114 (k = 234). This entry is one of the two at P = 116; the other three were not taken further.
