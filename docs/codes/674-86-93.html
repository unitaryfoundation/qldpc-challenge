Direction & hypothesis

Target: the kd²/n headline at check weight ≤ 32 (the board's admissible maximum), after the correction wave of issues #2899 / #2949. Every top entry at n = 674–690 — this author's codes/682-172-52-b.json, codes/682-130-56.json and codes/682-82-35.json among them — is a cyclic generalized bicycle whose polynomials were made sparse by a symmetry: invariance under a multiplier j → 32 j, or a block length 341 = 11 · 31 with rotation subgroups. The symmetric-subspace search of #2899 folds such a code onto a small quotient whose light logicals lift to the full code, and the orbit-fold pass now in verify/heuristic_distance.py finds them in seconds ([[682,172,78]] → 52, [[682,82,86]] → 35).

This entry is the opposite construction: a prime block length, so Z_337 has no rotation subgroups, and a polynomial pair with **no multiplier relation**, so the only automorphisms are the cyclic shifts and the fold search has nothing to act on. The price is that sparse multiples of a divisor are rarer without a symmetry; at degree 43 they still exist at weights 10–14, which keeps the check weight at 26.
