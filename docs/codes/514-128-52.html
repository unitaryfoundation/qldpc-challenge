Direction & hypothesis

No board entry at n <= 514 reaches k >= 108 and d >= 42 at any row weight, so the opening was dimension and distance together at moderate blocklength. Reaching it needs a construction that selects k rather than inheriting it. Since k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), choosing a divisor of x^m - 1 and drawing a and b from its ideal does exactly that: here the divisor has degree 64 and k = 128.

Two facts govern the construction, both checked rather than assumed. x^m - 1 is squarefree over GF(2) only for odd m, so m = 257 is usable and the even moduli of a residue sweep are not. And the usable a and b are low-weight codewords of the cyclic code the divisor generates, found by information-set decoding inside that ideal.

What was searched

The divisor is built as a product of cyclotomic-coset minimal polynomials over GF(2^16), so its degree is chosen; sampling sparse polynomials and taking gcds with x^m - 1 lands on a target degree almost never and kept 0 of 12 draws in an earlier attempt. Sweeping the ratio deg g / m from 0.04 to 0.30 across many coset subsets at nine odd moduli produced 299 priced candidates, of which 245 were undominated after folding and 137 were distinct shapes.

Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound with weight-52 witnesses on both sides.

random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6 52 (median 56, maximum 58) research/foldbound.py, uncapped no folded logical found verify/validate_candidate.py passed, not refuted

Unusually for this family, the symmetric fold finds nothing here, so it neither refutes the claim nor supports it, and the binding evidence is the trial-based search alone. The witnesses are the lightest the 24-seed scan found on each side, and both were re-verified against the committed matrices: in the kernel of the opposite checks, outside the row space of their own side, weight recounted. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature.

Dead ends

Row weight is the construction's price and the schema is its ceiling. schema/code.schema.json caps each check's support at 32 entries, so a heavier code cannot be submitted whatever it scores. Two candidates from this same sweep passed full 24-seed gates at row weights 48 and 58, one of them scoring kd^2/n = 1493.1, and neither is submittable. Attempting to rebuild the heavier one under the cap failed outright: the lightest codewords in its degree-101 divisor's ideal are weight 26 to 34, so any pair of generators sums to at least 52. A Gilbert-Varshamov estimate had suggested weight 16 was reachable, which is wrong for a cyclic ideal because that bound describes random codes.

Triage figures from this construction are not trustworthy. Ranking by a 20,000-trial reading overstated one candidate's score by a factor of five, and the deeper the search the lower every distance went: this code was screened at 62 and gated at 52, and a sibling went 86, then 82, then 74.
