[[390,11,6]] even-d multiband dense-packed surface code (d=6, rows=3, m=6, pitch=4)

Direction & hypothesis

Target the local-2d-single / weight-4 and unrestricted / weight-4 frontiers at d = 6 via the even-d (rows = 3, pitch = d - 2) regime of research/multiband_surface.py (Open Problem 4 of fieldnotes/2026-09-01-multiband-dense-packing-method.md). At `d = 6, rows = 3, pitch = 4, the closed form n(6, m) = 66m - 6, k = 2m - 1 beats the d = 7 two-band scaling n(7, m) = 74m - 25 whenever 8m > 19 (m >= 3`).

What was searched

Evaluated d = 6, rows = 3, pitch = 4 across m in {2..15} in research/multiband_surface.py. At m = 2 ([[126,3,6]]), `66(2) - 6 = 126 > 123, which is dominated by codes/123-3-7.json; for m >= 3`, every point is undominated. Selected m = 6, giving [[390,11,6]] (kd^2/n = 1.015, max check weight 4, single-layer 2D coordinates).

Evidence trail

Confirmation ladder for the submitted code, each rung a fresh seed unless marked otherwise, reading the lightest logical found:

| budget (trials/side) | lightest logical | | ---: | ---: | | 20,000 (NumPy RIS witness) | 6 | | 200,000 (fast RIS seed 101) | 6 | | 200,000 (fast RIS seed 102) | 6 |

3 fresh-seed rung(s) agree at the best bound, the deepest of them at 200,000 trials per side. The submitted witness reproduces it: X: d <= 6 (upper_bound), Z: d <= 6 (upper_bound).

Also checked with verify/sat_certify.py (CryptoMiniSat 5.14 SAT, UNSAT below weight 6 on both sides in 0.78 s); claimed as a witness-backed upper bound.

Dead ends

  • At d = 6, rows = 3, m = 2, pitch = 4, [[126,3,6]] is dominated by

    • codes/123-3-7.json because 8m > 19 requires m >= 3.

  • At d = 6, rows = 3, pitch = 2 or pitch = 6, distance collapses below 6.
  • At d = 8, rows = 3, pitch = 6, n(8, m) = 120m - 8 < n(9, m) = 122m - 41

    • requires m >= 17 (n >= 2032 > 1000), so all d = 8 points below `n = 1000 are dominated by d = 9`.
