What was searched

Designed-common-zero bivariate bicycle sweep: for every torus Z_l x Z_m with l*m in 56..110 (n in 112..220), a point (alpha, beta) of mu_l' x mu_m' (l', m' the odd parts) with Frobenius orbit of size t >= 4 is fixed first and A, B are drawn only among the 3- and 5-term polynomials that vanish there (so k >= 2t by construction; this code's point is index [0, 5] in Z_3 x Z_31).
