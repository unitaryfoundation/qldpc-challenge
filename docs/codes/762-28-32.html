[[762,28,32]] — weight-7 cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_381 (designed divisor of degree 14, trinomial-quadrinomial pair)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8 (maximum check weight 7), tier n > 700, where the verifier admits a claim only with max check weight <= 8 and d <= 40. That tier is thin: at n = 701..1000 the weight <= 8 frontier had 57 points, and for mid k it is inherited from below 700 ([[576,32,20]] and [[672,20,32]] at weight 6, [[700,44,16]] at weight 7, [[708,24,24]], [[944,32,24]], [[948,24,28]]). A weight <= 7 code with k = 28 at n <= 762 advances once d >= 21. The hypothesis was that the designed-divisor cyclic GB family, which the board uses almost only at n <= 700, reaches d around 30 at k = 28 with one weight-3 and one weight-4 polynomial once m has irreducible factors of moderate degree (381 = 3 x 127; the multiplicative order of 2 is 7 mod 127 and 14 mod 381, so x^381 - 1 splits into factors of degrees 1, 2, 7 and 14, and the divisor here is one of the degree-14 factors).

What was searched

  • Designed-divisor cyclic GB over odd m in 301..499, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)],

    • H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T]: up to 40 random divisors g of x^m - 1 per m with deg g in 11..30, weight-3 and weight-4 multiples from 400 Prange rounds (research/kit/gf2poly.sparse_multiples), pairs in bands (3,4) and (4,4) with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 22..60 and k <= 2 deg g + 6, direct sums dropped. 1,528 pairs over 46 fertile m.

  • Screen with gf2_fast RIS (research/kit/surrogate, pair depth 8): 3,000

    • trials each; 20,000 on the 92 survivors in cells that could still advance; 300,000 on the best 18. At m = 381 the (3,4) band gave eleven k = 28 pairs, seven of which were above the bar after 20,000 trials; the best three went to 300,000.

  • Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,

    • w down; equality counts as dominated) with the n > 700 admissibility rule.

Evidence trail

The submitted pair: a(x) = x^47 + x^113 + x^332, b(x) = x^46 + x^68 + x^113 + x^300, g = gcd(a, b, x^381 - 1) of degree 14 (0x505b as a little-endian bit integer, irreducible). Row weight 7 on every check.

The d = 33 document was staged first; the gate refuted it with a weight-32 X logical, the same weight the independent 2,000,000-trial run found, and the document was re-staged at d = 32 with that witness before the deep ladder. Two 20,000,000-trial runs on fresh seeds and the packaging pass found nothing below 32; a third 20,000,000-trial run was still going when this note was written, and the submission was filed only after it finished without finding a lighter logical. Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 32, not exact. The code advances the board for any d >= 21, so the margin over the bar is large even if a later sweep tightens the bound.

Near misses: the other two k = 28 pairs at m = 381 sit at d <= 28 after 300,000 trials ([1, 19, 253] / [145, 272, 285, 297] and [185, 236, 308] / [43, 71, 149, 297]); not submitted, same cell, lower bound.
