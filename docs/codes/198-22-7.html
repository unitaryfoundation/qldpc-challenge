[[198,22,7]] — 2BGA coset code of index 99, check weight 6

Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-6 cell at n in 111..220 with check weight 6. Only board entries with check weight <= 6 can dominate a weight-6 code; at the time of the search no such entry had n' <= 198, k' >= 22 and d' >= 7, so d = 7 was enough here. The gate's novelty check reports that it advances the frontier (k); board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py, literature novelty unverified.

Hypothesis: 2BGA coset codes reach high k at small n because the right support only needs to lie in the normalizer of H; a weight-2 right support b = 1 + h with h in N_G(H) acts on the coset space G/H as a permutation, so its kernel (functions constant on the orbits of h) is large, and a weight-4 left support can share many of those zeros at check weight 6.

What was searched

2BGA coset codes with (|a|, |b|) = (2, 4) (100 random pairs per (G, H)) and (4, 2) (40 per (G, H)), b inside the normalizer, over direct products D_m x C_k, split metacyclic groups, A4/S4/A5/S5 x C_k and D_a x D_b with a cyclic non-normal subgroup H of index 56..110 and |N_G(H)| >= 6, check weight 6. Funnel for this job: 960579 candidates built, 84290 connected with k >= 8 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 1 promoted to 20,000 trials, 1 survived it, 1 survived 200,000 trials and 1 survived 2,000,000 trials with the reading still at or above the needed d (a reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).

Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 7: the lightest logical operators found are embedded in the submission (distance.X / distance.Z witnesses) and are checked by the verifier. Confirmation ladder on the box (gf2_fast, research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, 20 threads):

Reproduction

The code is the 2BGA coset code (research/kit/coset.py) on G = direct_product(dihedral(11)[0], cyclic_product(9)[0])[0] (order 198) modulo the non-normal cyclic subgroup H = [0, 63] ([G:H] = 99); a = [0, 8, 117, 121] acts on the left, b = [0, 65] (inside N_G(H)) on the right.
