Direction & hypothesis

No board entry at n <= 630 reached d >= 72 at row weight 14 or below for any k, so the opening was distance at low check weight. This exact parameter point has a history of being claimed too high and corrected down, which shaped how the claim below was tested rather than deterred the attempt: an entry claiming [[630,20,90]] was corrected to 72 (#2914), a separate [[630,20,72]] was corrected to 66 by an orbit-fold witness (#2966), and two further entries were revised to 66 (#3003, #3007). Any new claim of 72 at this point therefore has to survive the instrument that refuted the last one, at the budget that refuted it.

The ring m = 315 was available throughout and had been written off: a modulus-selection metric I was using priced each candidate ring by a score ceiling k(2m/q)^2/n against the best kd^2/n in its blocklength bucket, and declared m = 315 saturated. The ceiling rests on the quotient-norm lift cap, which the fold below refutes outright, and the comparison treats the board as a score table when it is a Pareto front over n, k, d and w.

What was searched

Sparse polynomial pairs on x^315 - 1, with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) computed by Euclid so most draws die before any matrix work, and survivors priced against the quotient-norm lift, the single-block ideal of (x^m - 1)/gcd(a, x^m - 1), and the symmetric fold. Of 60,800 distinct pairs across m = 315 and m = 350, 41,765 were undominated at their screened distance and collapsed to 16 Pareto-distinct points, of which 3 survived the fold. A second pass dropping lift-cap claims before Pareto-thinning exposed 11 more points, since thinning first discards candidates whose only dominator is later refuted; 1 of those survived and duplicated a shape already in hand.

The symmetric fold

For any partition of the n qubits into equal-size orbits, the vectors constant on orbits are u = O v, so H_Z u = 0 reduces to (H_Z O) v = 0 on n/r columns and the lifted weight is r wt(v). Each partition bounds the distance on a code a factor r smaller. Three conditions are load-bearing, and each was learned by getting it wrong.

The folded code is handed to a distance search rather than having combinations of orbit basis vectors enumerated, because the interesting folded logicals are not sparse in that basis: a candidate at a claimed [[420,20,50]] was refuted by an x^35-invariant logical of folded weight 6 over 6 orbits.

No cap on the folded dimension. A cap of 48 missed that x^35 case, which needs 70 columns, and a cap of 160 then missed an x^105-invariant logical needing 210, which is how a sibling claim of [[630,24,70]] reached a pull request before the trusted gate refuted it at weight 66.

Trials scale with the folded code rather than being constant. A flat 20,000 reported 48 on codes/672-14-56.json where the true bound is 44 and the folded code has 336 columns; the budget is now 400 trials per column above a floor.

A lifted witness counts only if it both commutes and escapes the rowspace of the same-type checks, since a logical of the folded code can lift to a stabilizer of the original. Checking commutation alone reports weight-4 logicals on codes/36-2-6.json and codes/64-2-8.json, whose distances are 6 and 8; with both conditions it returns 6 and 8 exactly, which is the calibration that the bound is not merely conservative.

Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, with weight-72 witnesses on both sides.

symmetric fold at 1,000,000 trials per fold 72 (tight: matches the witness) random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6 94 (median 98, maximum 102) verify/validate_candidate.py passed, not refuted

The fold budget matters here and is quoted deliberately. The witness that corrected the previous [[630,20,72]] to 66 carries the provenance "orbit-fold, 1M trials per fold", so a fold run at less than that depth would be weak evidence rather than independent confirmation. At that depth it returns 72 and finds a weight-72 logical of its own.

The gate reports this as a distance-only gain over codes/630-20-54.json and codes/630-20-66-c.json and asks for the peers to be re-measured. Both were folded at the same 1,000,000 trials per fold and both are tight, returning exactly 54 and exactly 66, so neither is under-claimed and the gain over each is real. The gate also reports a shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature with codes/630-20-54.json. A signature match is not equivalence, and this family's symmetries are finite: the multiplier maps x to x^s for s a unit mod 315, translations, and a block swap. None of the 90,720 images of either peer's witness is a logical of this code, so the signature transfers no witness.

Dead ends

Of the 16 Pareto-distinct points from this archive the fold refuted 13, including [[700,34,100]] scoring 485.7 against a true bound of 50 and [[630,40,80]] scoring 406.3 against 70. The refuted set is dominated by claims sitting on the quotient-norm lift cap 2m/q: a claim at the cap means the lift found its own best witness and stopped, and a fold at a divisor of m beats it. m = 350 contributed 3 of those points and none survived, so that ring is not worth revisiting.

Neither instrument subsumes the other. A sibling candidate screened at [[350,20,40]] cleared the fold at exactly 40 and was then cut to 38 by the confirmation search, which reaches logicals that are not constant on any equal-orbit partition.
