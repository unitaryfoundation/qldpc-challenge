[[785,8,27]] — a two-row shortening of the planar tile code

Direction & hypothesis

The published codes/786-8-27.json is a weight-8, single-layer 2D tile code with eight logical qubits and weight-27 X and Z logical witnesses. Its witnessed score is 7.420. We sought a smaller code in the same cell by using a local stabilizer combination as a pivot. The reported distance is a witness-backed upper bound.

What was searched

A one-row pivot scan of this 786-qubit parent found 100 structural variants and 48 connected rank-8 variants. Projection of 439 known weight-27 parent logicals gave a valid weight-26 logical in each of those 48. We then screened pairwise XORs of same-side stabilizer rows for supports of weight at most 8 and radius at most 4. After excluding the already-refuted direct targets and preserving the inherited weight-27 witnesses, one structural variant remained: the XOR of Z rows 126 and 148, with support {548,568}, pivoting qubit 548.

Evidence trail

The unchanged trusted candidate gate passed the submitted JSON with seed 1643506955 against the 2026-10-17 board. It checked CSS commutation, n=785, k=8, check weight at most 8, a connected one-layer radius-4 layout, both weight-27 logical witnesses, deduplication, and 8,000 RIS refutation trials. It found no exact or WL-equivalent board entry and marked the weight-8, local-2d-single cell as advancing by lower n.

An independent reconstruction from codes/786-8-27.json exactly matched the submitted checks, coordinates, and both witnesses. All 439 saved parent logicals projected to weight at least 27 in this child. On the exact 785-qubit matrices, accelerated RIS returned a weight-28 X logical at 10,000 trials and pair depth 8 (seed 793558000), then weight-27 X logicals at 100,000 pair-depth-512 (seed 1297648000), 100,000 pair-depth-4096 (seed 4881648000), and 500,000 pair-depth-512 (seed 1298048000), and 1,000,000 pair-depth-4096 trials (seed 4882548000), and 1,000,000 pair-depth-512 trials (seed 1298548000). Every returned support was checked on the candidate matrices.

The witnessed score is 8 × 27² / 785 = 7.429, versus 7.420 for the published parent. d≤27 remains a witness-backed upper bound. These bounded searches do not prove a minimum distance of 27.

Dead ends

Every connected direct one-row shortening of the 786-qubit parent produced a validated weight-26 logical after projection of the known parent supports. The two-row pivot above avoids those direct targets.

Tools

GPT-6 (Codex) ran as one agent for @vprusso. The unchanged repository verifier and candidate gate, exact GF(2) rank and connectivity checks, and accelerated gf2_fast RIS screened and validated candidates.

Reproduction

Start from codes/786-8-27.json. XOR Z check rows 126 and 148 to obtain {548,568}. Replace Z row 126 with this combination. XOR that row into every other Z check containing qubit 548, then delete row 126 and qubit 548. Remove qubit 548 from X checks and the coordinate list, renumbering all larger qubit indices down by one. Preserve the remaining row order. Project each published X and Z witness through the same operation; both have weight 27 and are included in the JSON.
