[[294,16,24]] — weight-8 bivariate bicycle code on Z_21 x Z_7 with a 3 + 5 term split

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 270 and 320. Before this campaign the weight-8 frontier there ran 270-34-14, 270-10-24, 280-6-25, 288-12-24, 292-22-23, 294-24-20, 300-12-25, 306-26-19 and 310-12-26: at n <= 294 no weight-8 entry with k >= 16 reached d = 24. Bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on the board almost always split the check weight evenly between the two polynomials. The hypothesis was that an uneven split, |A| = 3 and |B| = 5 (still check weight 8 and column weight 8), could reach k = 16 at d = 24 below the [[320,16,24]] (4 + 4 split) filed from the same campaign. [[294,16,24]] does: no board entry has n <= 294, k >= 16, d >= 24 and check weight <= 8, and the k - 2 margin holds ([[294,14,24]] is not dominated either). It dominates that [[320,16,24]].

What was searched

BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A three monomials and B five monomials, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 222 <= 2lm <= 400, the torus drawn uniformly from that list and the other monomials uniformly from the torus.

The split costs rate: only 202 of 400,000 draws (seed 102) had k >= 16 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 9 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing and skipping any already dominated by a point staged earlier in the campaign. This was the only one staged. An earlier 3 + 5 sweep (300,000 draws, seed 91) staged the [[360,16,28]] filed separately.
