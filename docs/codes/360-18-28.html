[[360,18,28]] — weight-8 bivariate bicycle code on Z_18 x Z_10

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted (maximum check weight 8), CSS board, n between 221 and 400. Before this code, the frontier (board plus this campaign's open PRs) at check weight <= 8, d >= 14 and n from 300 to 360 ran through 300-12-25, 300-32-15, 306-26-19, 308-10-26, 310-12-26, 330-10-27, 336-24-24, 336-28-20. The hypothesis was that random 4 + 4-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on tori with 2lm in 300..400, prefiltered for k >= 18, still reach points there that the board does not hold.

[[360,18,28]] does: no board entry has n <= 360, k >= 18, d >= 28 and check weight <= 8. Against [[360,18,27]] (same n, k and weight, and the same Weisfeiler-Leman signature) the gain is in d only, so the two codes were re-measured side by side at matched depth (see Evidence trail); it also dominates [[360,16,28]], [[378,18,27]] and [[392,18,28]], so the gate does not classify the overall gain as d-only.

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A of 4 and B of 4 monomials, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 300 <= 2lm <= 400; the torus is drawn uniformly from that list and the other monomials uniformly from the torus.

This sweep (seed 604) drew 400,020 codes in a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter; 946 had k >= 18 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 22 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and 21 were taken to 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing; 2 survived that and were staged.
