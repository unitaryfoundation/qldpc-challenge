Direction & hypothesis

Extend the d = 3, rows = 2, pitch = 2 two-band dense-packed surface-code ladder (n = 14m - 5, k = 2m - 1, asymptotic kd^2/n = 9/7 = 1.2857, research/multiband_surface.py) beyond the m <= 24 (codes/331-47-3.json) cutoff of earlier sweeps. Between k = 49 and k = 91, the board was populated by rows >= 3, pitch = 4 configurations (codes/663-91-3.json at kd^2/n = 1.2353), which pay the pitch_min(3) = 4 multi-band penalty whereas rows = 2 operates at pitch = d - 1 = 2.

What was searched

Enumerated d = 3, rows = 2, pitch = 2 across m in {25..71} in research/multiband_surface.py and compared against `local-2d-single / weight-4. Rungs m = 25..46 ([[345,49,3]] through [[639,91,3]]) and m = 58..61 ([[807,115,3]] through [[849,121,3]]`) are all undominated; selected the m = 46 endpoint [[639,91,3]] (kd^2/n = 1.2817, max check weight 4, single-layer 2D coordinates).
