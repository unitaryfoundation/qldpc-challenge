Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted weight-8 CSS cell. At submission preparation on 21 October 2026, codes/928-238-22.json by @MicheldeMessieres has the highest reported kd^2/n in this cell, 124.12931. This candidate gives 124.18421 using its submitted upper bound. It extends the Pareto frontier and dominates no existing entry: relative to that comparator it uses fewer physical qubits but encodes fewer logical qubits, at the same reported distance and weight. The score comparison is separate from Pareto dominance and could change if a lighter logical is found.

The comparator's note reports two P = 114, k = 234 draws reading 22 in a Stern screen without further investigation. The hypothesis here was that a longer anneal of the 8-cycle count could produce a P = 114 draw that retained that reading under deeper searches. This is evidence about a selected instance, not a demonstrated causal advantage of annealing.

Inspired by: codes/928-238-22.json and codes/976-250-22.json by @MicheldeMessieres for the even-lift k = 2P + 6 mechanism, anneal length and fold filter; codes/904-230-22.json by @vprusso for the matchings and 8-cycle polishing; codes/808-206-20.json by @pandey-tushar for the girth-8 filter; and fieldnotes/2026-09-20-screening-traps-at-n-900.md by @e-eight for the score and lift-cap discussion.

What was searched

At P = 114, draw solutions of the 36 paired-difference equations in the Reproduction section using a 19-dimensional integer solution basis reduced modulo 114. Require girth 8 in both Tanner graphs and compute k over GF(2). The ordinary arms retain starting draws with k >= 234; the residue-class arm selects a mod-3 class supporting k >= 236.

For subgroup orders m = 2, 3, 6, search the quotient lift over Z_(114/m) at 1,000 RIS trials per side. Lift a quotient word to the full code and check its commutation and non-membership in the same-type stabilizer row space. Discard a draw if a valid lifted logical has weight below 22. A quotient word alone is insufficient: its lift might be a stabilizer.

For the long anneals, try 15,000 single-coefficient changes in the design basis. Reject girth violations; accept an increase delta in the summed 8-cycle count with probability exp(-delta/T), with T decreasing geometrically from 64 toward 4. Recheck the starting k after every accepted move and connectivity and the fold filter every 200 accepted moves, reverting to a validated checkpoint on failure. Return the validated state with the lowest 8-cycle count. For the submitted draw, k remains 234.

The long-anneal arms screen at 2,000 RIS trials per side, then escalate readings of at least 22 through 10,000, 30,000 and 300,000 trials on fresh seeds. The greedy control instead uses 2,000 and 30,000 trials, with deep follow-up recorded separately. Counts below are completed task records, excluding metadata, reconciled on 21 October 2026.
