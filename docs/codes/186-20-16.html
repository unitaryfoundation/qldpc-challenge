Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-8 cell at n in 111..220, where the board's frontier for k >= 12 at d >= 8 is thin (frontier map of 2026-10-17). A (186, 20, 16) point at check weight 8 needed d >= 16 to clear every board entry with n' <= 186, k' >= 20, w' <= 8; the gate's novelty check reports it advances the cell on d, k, n (board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py; literature novelty unverified).

What was searched

Designed-common-zero bivariate bicycle sweep: for every torus Z_l x Z_m with l*m in 56..110 (n in 112..220), a point (alpha, beta) of mu_l' x mu_m' (l', m' the odd parts) with Frobenius orbit of size t >= 4 is fixed first and A, B are drawn only among the 3- and 5-term polynomials that vanish there (so k >= 2t by construction; this code's point is index [1, 11] in Z_3 x Z_31). Funnel for this job: 73000 candidates built, 54547 distinct with k >= 10 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 25 promoted to 20,000 trials, 21 to 200,000 trials, 7 survived with the reading still above the board's weight-8 frontier (any reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).
