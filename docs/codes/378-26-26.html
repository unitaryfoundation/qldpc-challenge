Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 330 and 400. Before this campaign the weight-8 frontier there ran 336-88-12, 336-28-20, 336-24-24, 372-14-29, 376-98-8, 378-32-19, 378-18-27 and 392-102-14: no weight-8 entry with n <= 392 and k >= 26 had d above 20, and the best k >= 24 point was 336-24-24. The hypothesis was that four-plus-four-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on tori with 2lm in 300..400, drawn at random and prefiltered for k >= 16, could reach the k >= 26, d >= 24 region. [[378,26,26]] does: no board entry has n <= 378, k >= 26, d >= 26 and check weight <= 8, and both margins hold ([[378,24,26]] and [[378,26,24]] are not dominated either). kd^2/n is about 46.5, against 41.1 for 336-24-24.

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A and B four monomials each, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 300 <= 2lm <= 400, the torus drawn uniformly from that list and the other three monomials of each polynomial uniformly from the torus.

Random supports rarely give k >= 16, so the sweep ran a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter: 300,000 draws (seed 92), of which 1,338 had k >= 16 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 33 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing and skipping any already dominated by a point staged earlier in the campaign. Three were staged: this code, a [[360,18,27]] on Z_60 x Z_3 and a [[378,18,29]] on Z_63 x Z_3.
