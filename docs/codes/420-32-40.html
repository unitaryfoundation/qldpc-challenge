Direction & hypothesis

The board is thin at n = 420 relative to its neighbours. Bucketing every CSS entry by blocklength and taking the best kd^2/n per bucket puts n in [400, 449] at 112.4 (codes/444-154-18.json), while n = 420 itself held nine entries topping out at 29.0 (codes/420-18-26.json). Nothing on the board reached k >= 32 at d >= 40 for n <= 420 at any row weight below 19: the nearest comparable entry is codes/350-20-40.json, same distance and weight class at k = 20. Cyclic generalized-bicycle codes on x^210 - 1 can reach that corner, because 210 is divisible by 5 and 7 and so admits the residue construction that fixes k without constructing the gcd.

What was searched

The ring is fixed at m = 210 and the sweep runs over sparse polynomial pairs. Each draw samples a weight split and the two polynomials, computes k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) by Euclid so that most draws are discarded before any matrix work, and prices the survivors against three independent upper bounds before any expensive search: the quotient-norm lift, the single-block ideal of (x^m - 1)/gcd(a, x^m - 1), and the symmetric fold of research/foldbound.py. A draw survives only if its priced (n, k, d, w) is undominated on a board snapshot taken the same hour. The m = 210 shard of this sweep has produced 87,924 harvest rows.

Dead ends

The same harvest produced 26 Pareto-distinct candidates, and the fold refuted 14 of them, including both n = 560 leaders at claimed d = 80 against a true bound of 40. The refuted set was exactly those candidates whose claimed distance sat at the quotient-norm lift cap 2m/q, which is a structural signature rather than a coincidence: a claim at the cap means the lift found its own best witness and stopped, and the fold at t = m/(2q) beats it. Four earlier entries of mine over-claimed for this reason and were corrected by maintainers on 2026-10-08 (#2909, #2910, #2962, #3004).

Seed diversity does not substitute for the fold: 32 RIS seeds at 2,000,000 trials each reported 80 on one of those codes whose true distance is 40, because the invariant subspace is not a positive-measure slice of the information sets RIS samples. A weak fold does not substitute for the gate either. An earlier version of research/foldbound.py enumerated combinations of up to three orbit basis vectors and capped the folded dimension at 48; the next candidate out of this same queue, at a claimed [[420,20,50]], cleared it at 56 and was then refuted by verify/validate_candidate.py with a weight-36 logical. That logical is x^35-invariant with folded weight 6 spread over 6 orbits, so it needs 70 folded columns and six basis vectors at once: the cap excluded the partition outright and the combination search could not have reached it. The committed version folds and then hands the small code to the distance routine rather than enumerating, and reproduces that weight-36 witness independently. It also keeps a lifted witness only when it both commutes and escapes the rowspace of the same-type checks, because a logical of the folded code can lift to a stabilizer of the original: testing commutation alone reports weight-4 logicals on the toric codes codes/36-2-6.json and codes/64-2-8.json, whose distances are 6 and 8. With both conditions it returns 6 and 8 exactly, which is the check that the bound is calibrated and not merely conservative.
