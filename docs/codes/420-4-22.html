Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-6 CSS cell, thin k = 4 corner at large n. Screening arXiv:2610.06623v1 left two rows whose distance the paper reports as an interval rather than an exact value, and the board holds neither.

kd^2/n is 4.61, well below the 30.5 the same cell already holds ([[672,20,32]]). This is a thin-corner record, not a strong one.

What was searched

No fresh search. All 10 CSS presentations in Table 3 of arXiv:2610.06623v1 were rebuilt with research/kit/bb.py:build_bb and their (n, k, w) measured from the matrices; all 10 reproduced their claimed parameters. Replicated components were dropped first (the paper's own connectivity audit finds 409 of 1,142 catalogue entries disconnected, and 73.1% of exact classes to be repeated components), then survivors were Pareto-screened against codes/.

Seven Table 3 rows are dominated by existing entries and two are already on the board. This row and [[432,4,23]] are the only undominated survivors.

Evidence trail

The presentation: l = 15, m = 14, A = 1 + x^3 y, `B = 1 + x^10 y^6 + x^11 y^2, so n = 2*15*14 = 420`.

    • component.

    • exact certificate. This repository's searches (research/kit/surrogate.py:distance_rand_witness, 4 seeds x 6000 RIS trials, pair_depth=16, gf2_fast backend) returned weight-22 logicals and nothing lighter on the X side.

The second (15,14) presentation

The paper prints two (15,14) presentations with these same parameters and the same interval evidence:

1. A = 1 + x^3 y, B = 1 + x^10 y^6 + x^11 y^2 — filed here. 2. A = 1 + x^4 y^3 + x^8 y^11, B = 1 + x^3 y^3.

They have different fingerprints but the same WL signature, which is the board's own "possibly equivalent" signal. A search over all 2,120 affine lattice automorphisms of Z_15 x Z_14, crossed with monomial shifts and sector exchange, found no isomorphism between them; that is evidence they are distinct, not proof, since WL equality is not a statement either way about equality.

Only one is filed. They share (n, k, d) and therefore a slug, and filing a WL-flagged pair as two independent records would overstate what is known. The second presentation is left unfiled rather than filed as a -b twin.

Dead end worth recording: the paper's [[432,4,10]] exact row is dominated eleven times over; [[180,4,14]] and [[140,6,10]] from the same table are already on this board and are themselves dominated by [[168,4,14]].

Tools

Human-directed agent work (opencode CLI) rebuilding and screening published constructions; research/kit/bb.py for construction, research/kit/surrogate.py and the gf2_fast accelerator for the witness search, verify/validate_candidate.py for the gate. Roughly 20 CPU-minutes of witness search on an n=420 code.
