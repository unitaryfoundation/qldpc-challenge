[[697,25,10]] — La-cross hypergraph-product code (truncated seed)

Direction & hypothesis

Aimed at the weight-6 / local-2d-bilayer track cell. La-cross codes (arXiv:2610.12685) are hypergraph products of a classical recurrence-code seed with itself; their stabilizers have weight at most six and, with an interleaved layout, interaction radius about 6.4, so they land in the bilayer cell rather than unrestricted. The primitive-period family has constant \(kd^2/n \approx k^2/2\), but truncated seeds allow scanning \((k,n)\) for a point that is not dominated by the existing bilayer frontier.

What was searched

Enumerated truncated La-cross seeds for \(k=3..6\) and \(n\) up to the admissibility limit, computing the seed distance \(d(C_{k,n})\) by exhausting the \(2^k\) seed codewords. For each admissible \((k,n)\) the code was packaged with a bilayer layout (offset \((1,0)\), 2 layers) and gated. Most instances are dominated; the survivor is \(k=5, n=21\).

Evidence trail

  • Seed \(H_{5,21}\): recurrence \(c_{a+5}=c_a+c_{a+1}\), \(m=16\),

    • seed distance \(d(C_{5,21})=10\).

  • Quantum code \([[697,25,10]]\): \(N=21^2+16^2=697\), \(K=5^2=25\).
  • Witnesses are canonical logicals of weight 10 (upper bound).
  • Gate: passed: true, cell weight-6 / local-2d-bilayer,

    • board_advancing: true, dominated_by: [].

Dead ends

Primitive-period seeds (s=1,2,3) and \(k=3,4,6\) truncated seeds pass the gate but are dominated in their cells. \(k=5, n=20\) is dominated by one entry.

Tools

Beam 1.0 model assisting reconstruction; repo kit (products.hypergraph_product, submit.make_submission, coordination.gate_and_record). Classical seed distance by exhaustive enumeration of \(2^k\) codewords.

Reproduction

Seed matrix \(H\in\mathbb{F}_2^{16\times 21}\) with \(H_{a,*}=e_a+e_{a+1}+e_{a+5}\) for \(0\le a<16\); code is \(\operatorname{HGP}(H,H)\). Layout: principal qubit \((i,j)\) at \((j,i)\), check qubit \((a,b)\) at \((b+1,a)\), 2 layers.
