[[722,18,24]] — open-boundary tile code on a 19x19 lattice (weight-8, bilayer)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 / local-2d-bilayer, family tile. The k=18 tile ladder on this board jumped from codes/578-18-20.json (n=578) to codes/792-18-27.json (n=792) with no instance in between. The sweep that produced this entry tested whether that hole was unvisited sizes (it was) and how d behaves across them. The working prediction — that d tracks min(Lx, Ly) rather than n, and therefore steps rather than rising smoothly — makes the 19x19 lattice the top of the gap, and this entry its most interesting member: **its distance came down twice as the search got deeper, and the claim below is the deepest number, not the first one.**

What was searched

24 lattices (Lx, Ly), Lx <= Ly, [14, 23]^2, 2 Lx Ly inside (578, 792), built with `research/local2d/boundary_engine.build_planar(Lx, Ly, S_F_TILE4, S_G_TILE4_MAIN) after calibrating the generator against codes/924-18-31.json` by X and Z check-matrix equality. Screening at 60,000 gf2_fast RIS trials per lattice, packaging at 400,000, a matched-depth confirmation of every board-advancing candidate at 1,500,000 fresh-seed fast trials, and the submission's own search at 2,000,000 fast trials. Layout from research/local2d/planar.grid_coordinates, layers=2, interaction radius 4.2426.

Evidence trail

This entry's ladder, both collapses included:

  • 60,000 fast trials: d <= 27
  • 400,000 fast trials: d <= 26 (staged and gate-passed as [[722,18,26]])
  • 1,500,000 fast trials, fresh seed 101: d <= 25 (Z-side witness of weight

    • 25; the X side reached only 38)

  • 2,000,000 fast trials, seed 0 (this submission's own search): d <= 24,

    • Z-side witness of weight 24 (the X side reached only 35 at the same depth)

So the claim shipped here is [[722,18,24]], and the two earlier numbers are recorded rather than hidden: each was the deepest measurement at its budget, and each was superseded by a deeper one. Rung 24 was otherwise empty on this family — its neighbour [[720,18,24]] came down to 23 at the confirmation depth and was not promoted — so this entry is what fills it.

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, not exact: k=18 is far outside verify/certify.py's measured envelope (certified codes today: d <= 13, k <= 12). The board's weekly fresh-seed sweep re-measures every entry at 3e8 trials; a lighter logical found there corrects or removes this entry.

Dead ends

  • Every 16x\* lattice (n=608..736) screens at d <= 20 and is dominated by

    • codes/578-18-20.json; passed the gate, not submitted.

  • [[720,18,24]] (18x20): collapsed from d <= 24 to d <= 23 at the 1.5M

    • confirmation; not promoted. The 17x\* lattices (d <= 21) and 18x18/19/21 (d <= 23) advance the cell and are submitted as their own entries.

  • No lattice in the gap ever witnessed d=22 at any budget, so rung 22 looks

    • like a step this family skips.

  • distance_slope() from research/local2d/transfer.py did not finish

    • inside 600 s for this bulk (transfer-graph state space), so the empirical ladder is the record.

  • Two annealing attempts to retrofit a 2D layout onto the large

    • pair-partition-cpm entries (632-162-18, 630-126-20) plateaued at radius 14.4-16.5 against the bilayer cap 7.0; that negative result is why this campaign went back to the 2D-native tile family.
