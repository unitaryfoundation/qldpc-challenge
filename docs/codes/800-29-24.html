[[800,29,24]] — weight-8 open-boundary tile code with a 5x5 box on a 20x20 lattice (bilayer)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 / local-2d-bilayer, family tile. The tile entries on the board use 3x3 and 4x4 boxes and carry only k in {8, 12, 18}. In the board snapshot of 2026-10-28, no code in this cell with k >= 28 and n <= 1000 had a witnessed d above 10. The high-k entries are compact periodic layouts.

Two observations pointed at larger open-boundary boxes:

  • Bicycle Race (arXiv:2610.13880). Two-block codes are

    • translation-invariant on twisted tori, and good codes concentrate in a few groups. Folding a torus onto a bilayer plane costs a factor of 2 in stretch. With the radius cap, that limits a weight-8 periodic code to a small window.

  • Bernstein–Kushnirenko bound. For a bivariate tile code, k is bounded

    • by the mixed volume of the two support polygons. In the open-boundary construction it equals it. A small window therefore caps k at about 16–24, which matches the k~20 plateau of the cell.

A 5x5 box raises the mixed volume to 28–32 while staying inside the 7.0 radius cap. Tile codes with a (D+1)x(D+1) box can reach k = 2D^2 = 32. arXiv:2504.09171 searched boxes up to 4x4 and did not search higher weights exhaustively.

What was searched

  • Genome. Two supports S_f and S_g, four monomials each, in a 5x5 box.
  • Generator program. `research/local2d/boundary_engine.build_planar(L, L,

    • S_f, S_g), then reduce_weights` on both check matrices, then removal of the all-zero rows that reduce_weights leaves.

  • Layout. research/local2d/planar.grid_coordinates, with layers = 2.
  • Admissibility. Every check, including boundary and corner checks, has

    • weight <= 8, and the layout diameter is <= 7.0.

  • Mutation search. Moves of one or two monomials, or a redraw of one

    • support. The proposal filter required mixed volume >= 28. The archive kept the best candidates in each k bin.

  • Screening.
  • An L=12 pilot. Every candidate saturated at d<=8 there, because the

    • lattice was too small.

  • An L=16 run, n=512: 45 minutes, 23,744 evaluations, 3,000 gf2_fast

    • RIS trials each.

  • Re-measurement.

    • 1. The top 5 per k bin (25 candidates) at 16x16, 18x18 and 20x20, with 20,000 fast trials at pair depth 16 (75 measurements). 2. The top 8 at 20x20, with 100,000 trials at pair depth 32 and a fresh seed. 3. The top 4 through the deep ladder.

Dead ends

  • Collapsed near-misses. Same ladder, d reported at 20,000 -> 100,000 ->

    • 7M trials:

| Code | 20,000 | 100,000 | 7M | Status | |---|---|---|---|---| | [[781,28]] | 26 | 24 | 23 | not submitted; worse kd^2/n | | [[784,28]] | 25 | 24 | 23 | not submitted | | [[800,32]] | 23 | 22 | 22 | flat at 22; a different k bin, left for a separate entry |

  • Inflated readings. An earlier 20x20 candidate with k=30 read d<=27 at

    • 4,000 trials and fell to d<=23 at 100,000 trials. Shallow readings at n~800 run 2–4 high, so nothing here was promoted from fewer than 100,000 trials.

  • Supports that break the caps. Some supports admissible in the bulk

    • break the caps only at some sizes, with boundary checks reaching weight 15 or radius 9.0. Admissibility was re-checked at every size measured.

  • Lattice size. The L=12 lattice caps d at 8 for every 5x5-box support,

    • so it cannot rank candidates.

Model: Claude Opus 5.5, driven through Claude Code. Repository tooling:

Compute was about 40 CPU core-hours: about 11 for the L=16 search, about 3 for re-measurement and about 22 for the four deep ladders.
