[[630,28,26]] — weight-7 cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_315 (designed divisor of degree 14, quadrinomial-trinomial pair)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8 (maximum check weight 7). At n <= 630 the weight <= 7 frontier with k >= 28 topped out at d = 20 ([[576,32,20]], weight 6), and the nearest weight-7 points above it were [[618,24,20]], [[708,24,24]], [[720,24,24]] and [[826,28,20]]. So a weight-7 code with k = 28 at n = 630 advances for any d >= 21. The hypothesis was that a designed-divisor cyclic GB pair with one weight-4 and one weight-3 polynomial reaches the mid 20s at k = 28 on Z_315 (315 = 3^2 x 5 x 7, so x^315 - 1 has many small irreducible factors and many degree-14 divisors), and that a moderate d at n = 630 is a claim a fresh-seed ladder can actually settle.

What was searched

  • Designed-divisor cyclic GB over odd m in 301..499, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)],

    • H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T]: up to 40 random divisors g of x^m - 1 per m with deg g in 11..30, weight-3 and weight-4 multiples of g from 400 Prange rounds (sparse_multiples in research/kit/gf2poly.py), pairs in bands (3,4) and (4,4) with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 22..60, direct sums dropped: 1,528 pairs over 46 fertile m.

  • Screen with gf2_fast RIS (research/kit/surrogate.py, pair depth 8): 3,000

    • trials each, then 20,000 on the survivors in cells that could still advance. That sweep left seven m = 315 pairs above their bars unexamined (two weight-7 k = 28 pairs and five weight-8 k = 38 pairs); all seven went to 300,000 trials here, then the most stable one to fresh-seed ladders.

  • Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,

    • w down; equality counts as dominated).

Evidence trail

The submitted pair: a(x) = x^119 + x^122 + x^245 + x^302, b(x) = x^60 + x^205 + x^275, g = gcd(a, b, x^315 - 1) of degree 14 (0x5cf1 as a little-endian bit integer). Every check has weight 7.

The d stopped moving at 20,000 trials and stayed at 26 through every deeper rung. The Z-side witness is the X-side witness mapped by (x1 | x2) -> (R x2 | R x1) with R: i -> -i mod 315, which carries X-logicals of this code to Z-logicals of the same weight; it was validated as a Z-logical before staging, so both sides carry a weight-26 witness. Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 26, not exact. The bar is 21, so the claim stays board-advancing even if a later sweep finds a logical up to five lighter.

Near misses at m = 315: the other k = 28 weight-7 pair (a = x^104 + x^174 + x^274, b = x^36 + x^57 + x^215 + x^275, same divisor) went 29 -> 26 between 20,000 and 300,000 trials. It has the same parameters and was not laddered separately. The five weight-8 k = 38 pairs did not settle: the best went 42 (20,000) -> 32 (300,000) -> 30 (3,000,000, fresh seed 31001), the next 42 -> 30, and the rest read 25, 24 and 21 at 300,000 against a bar of 19. They were not submitted because the ladder was still falling.
