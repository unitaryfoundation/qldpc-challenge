Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 360 and 400. Before this filing the weight-8 frontier there ran 360-10-28, 364-8-32, 372-14-29, 378-32-19, 378-18-27 (a lifted product over M_27 x Z_7) and 392-8-33. The hypothesis was that four-plus-four-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on long thin tori such as Z_63 x Z_3 could match the k = 18 of 378-18-27 at a larger distance. [[378,18,29]] does: no board entry has n <= 378, k >= 18, d >= 29 and check weight <= 8, and the k - 2 margin holds ([[378,16,29]] is not dominated either). Against 378-18-27 (same n, k and weight) the gain is d-only, which the gate treats as the suspect axis; it also flagged the two as possibly equivalent (same Weisfeiler-Leman signature). The matched-depth re-measurement it asks for is in the evidence trail below: at the same budget the peer reads 27 and this code reads 29, so the two are not equivalent and the gap is real at that depth. The deep ladder used 20,000,000 trials per seed because the claim is d-only.

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A and B four monomials each, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 300 <= 2lm <= 400, the torus drawn uniformly from that list and the other three monomials of each polynomial uniformly from the torus.

Random supports rarely give k >= 16, so the sweep ran a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter: 300,000 draws (seed 92), of which 1,338 had k >= 16 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 33 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing and skipping any already dominated by a point staged earlier in the campaign. Three were staged: this code, a [[378,26,26]] on Z_21 x Z_9 and a [[360,18,27]] on Z_60 x Z_3 (both filed separately).
