Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-6 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 330 and 400. Before this campaign the weight-6 frontier in the high-rate corner there was 330-68-6, 360-38-8 and 382-36-10: no weight-6 entry with n <= 380 and k >= 20 reached d = 10. The hypothesis was that two-block group-algebra (2BGA) codes on split metacyclic groups C_l1 ⋊ C_l2 of order 150 to 200 could reach d = 10 below n = 382 at k in the mid twenties. [[378,26,10]] does: no board entry has n <= 378, k >= 26, d >= 10 and check weight <= 6, and the k - 2 margin holds ([[378,24,10]] is not dominated either). It trades distance for rate against the [[378,24,16]] on a different presentation of C63 ⋊ C3 filed from the same campaign.

What was searched

Random-support 2BGA codes H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] with a, b three group elements each, both containing the identity (no loss of generality). The group menu was every non-abelian group of order 150 to 200 the kit builds from Cayley-table constructors: dihedral D_n, metacyclic C_n ⋊_r C_k (up to two actions r per (n, k)), C_m x D_k, C_m x A4, C_m x S4, D_a x D_b, C_m x (C_n ⋊ C_k) for eight small metacyclic factors, and the split metacyclic presentations ZSZ(l1, l2, q), l2 <= 8, of research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py.

A generic weight-3 element is invertible on most irreducible blocks of F_2[G], so k >= 20 is rare: the sweep ran a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter over 400,000 draws (seed 62), of which 239 had k >= 20 and a connected Tanner graph. Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 31 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8). Three different draws (on ZSZ(63,3,25), ZSZ(63,3,16) and MC(63,3,16)) gave [[378,26,10]] at screen depth. The other survivors were [[310,20,12]], [[336,20,16]], [[336,24,12]] and [[378,24,16]], filed separately, and points those dominate.
