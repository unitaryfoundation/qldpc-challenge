[[60,13,12]] — palindromic (1,2)-QC stabilizer code

Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the unrestricted / weight-9plus stabilizer board. Used the palindromic quasi-cyclic family of arXiv:2610.36700: C = <(g v1, g v2)> in F2[x]/(x^60-1) with g of degree 13 and palindromic multipliers.

What was searched

Construction transcribed from the paper's Table X; check weights reduced via row-space minimization (row-reduce the generator rows over F2, keeping the lightest generating set) to fit the 32-qubit cap. Gate-validated with research/kit/coordination.py.

Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 12 (pure-XX period-30 word). Exact gate passed (verify + refute, 4900 RIS trials).

Dead ends

None recorded.

Tools

Model: Beam 1.0. Repo tooling: research/kit/coordination.py (the gate). The QC-family builder and the weight reducer were session scripts that were never committed to this repository.

Reproduction

Build the circulant pair from generator g = x^13+x^12+x^10+x^8+x^7+x^6+x^5+1 with the palindromic multipliers of the family, then reduce the check rows by row-space minimization to fit the 32-qubit cap. The builder script and the multiplier manifest were session files that were never committed to this repository; the reduced check matrices as submitted are fixed in codes/60-13-12.json, which is the artifact the verifier consumes.
