[[972,166,34]] — irregular pair-partition CPM code at the composite lift P = 81, with a rank-deficient cocycle class giving k = 2P + 4

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: CSS, unrestricted, weight 8, extended tier (n <= 1000 with w <= 8 and d <= 40). The comparison entry is codes/996-166-32.json, introduced by @MicheldeMessieres in PR #4045. It uses the pair-partition (PP) method of Okada and Kasai (arXiv:2607.14091) on an irregular 5 x 12 template at P = 83, with n = 12P and k = 2P. Its reported distance gives an upper-bound score kd^2/n = 170.667. The full-row-rank design rate is 1/6; rank deficiency offers a way to increase k at fixed n.

Hypothesis: a composite lift can increase k at fixed n, with distance screened separately. Over F_2, x^81 - 1 has the factor x^2 + x + 1 (Phi_3). If H_X and H_Z both lose rank on that component, each side's F_2 rank drops by 2 and k rises by 4. Whether they do depends only on the exponents mod 3, so it can be designed. The extra logical classes have periodic representatives from the Z_3 quotient, whose weights multiply by 27 when lifted. This does not bound the weights of all representatives of those classes from below. For this code, k = 166 and the reported d <= 34 give the upper-bound score 166 * 34^2 / 972 = 197.424.

Technique (not ours): the PP commutation rule and CPM lift are Okada and Kasai's (arXiv:2607.14091); the design-rate count is from Lee, Okada, Maskara, Kasai and Zhou (arXiv:2609.30069); the 5 x 12 template is the one introduced by @MicheldeMessieres in codes/996-166-32.json (notes/996-166-32.md). Ours: the pairing used here, the integer-lattice exponents, and the rank-deficient class at a composite lift.

What was searched

  • Pairings: a large-neighbourhood search with exact forced-4-cycle pruning

    • around the pairing of codes/996-166-32.json found about 500 4-cycle-free pairings of the template. The one used here forces the fewest block hexagons found (6 per side; that entry's pairing forces 12), leaves 6 free exponent directions, and every block row's link is triangle-free (its square complex of rows, columns and one square per pair is nonpositively curved).

  • Exponents: the integer solution lattice of the paired-difference

    • equations, from a column Hermite reduction (27 basis vectors: 21 gauge and 6 free), reduced mod 81. Integer solutions satisfy the equations before reduction; a solution found only mod a prime need not satisfy them mod 81.

  • Rank-deficient classes: coefficient vectors mod 3 were sampled and the

    • Z_3 quotient code (n = 36) built for each. A sampled class with k = 10 instead of 6, from rank loss on Phi_3 on both sides, was fixed and the higher digits drawn at random.

  • Filter: no block 4-cycle closes (voltage 0 mod 81, nor 0 mod 27, which

    • closes after 3 turns), and only the 6 forced block hexagons per side.

  • Four filtered draws were screened by full-length Stern with a budget

    • of up to 1,000,001 iterations per side. Draws 0 and 3 completed both sides. Draws 1 and 2 found weight-14 X logicals after two iterations and stopped before the Z screen.

  • Matched control at small size, on another 4-cycle-free pairing of the

    • same template at P = 39 (n = 468), 10 draws each and 120k iterations per side: the deficient class (k = 82) read 18 on five draws and 20 on five; an ordinary class (k = 78) read 18 on all ten. This sample showed no reduction in the lightest weights found. The draws' hexagon counts also differed, so this does not isolate the effect of rank deficiency or establish that it preserves true distance.

Claim: d_X <= 34 and d_Z <= 34, from stored weight-34 logicals. No lower bound of 34 is established. For scale, the same search found weight-32 logicals in codes/996-166-32.json within about 0.6M iterations. This calibration does not exclude a sparse or harder-to-detect layer of lighter logicals in this code. Near misses: draws 1 and 2 of the same class have weight-14 X logicals on block columns 0-5 only (the pairs of the template's omitted-edge triangle), found instantly; a second deficient class read 30 on both of its draws. A restricted search on columns 0-5 exposes the collapse in seconds and should be the first filter at a composite lift.

Dead ends

  • Non-abelian two-level lifts of the (3,8) cube design: few non-abelian

    • quotients appeared among those tested for the square complex's fundamental group. Tested covers gained at most 2 cocycle dimensions. At n = 496, 30 twisted and 30 abelian draws read mean 11.6 and 11.5.

  • A rate-1/7 6 x 14 template from the Fano plane (rows are complements of 6

    • lines, k = 2P + 6): all 32,768 symmetric natural-plus-cross pairings were enumerated exactly; the best class (6 free directions, 12 forced hexagons per side) read 28 at P = 71.

  • 5 x 12 templates whose base matrix has F_2 dependencies (left-out graph

    • C4 + K2, k = 2P + 4; three quads, k = 2P + 6): at least 16 and 48 forced hexagons per side; at matched n = 492, C4 + K2 read 16 against 22 for the pairing used here.

  • Rank loss on large cyclotomic factors: none in 300 draws each for the

    • 5 x 12 designs at P = 73 and 77 or for a 4 x 10 design at P = 73 to 99.

  • Z_3 x Z_27 lifts with a class that loses rank on two of the four F_4

    • components of the Z_3 x Z_3 quotient (k = 170): the one draw screened read 30.

  • Sampled random pair-complement pairings had 0 or 1 free exponent

    • directions, motivating the structured pairing search.
