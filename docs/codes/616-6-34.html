Direction & hypothesis

Target: the weight-6 × unrestricted CSS cell, high-distance corner. The repo's quadricycle fieldnote (2026-09-19) left an open question: do rank-4 periodic generalized bicycles — four *independent* shifts on an abelian 4-torus, as opposed to the dependent-variable trivariate codes of arXiv:2406.19151 that reduce to rank-2 — reach board territory that plain bivariate-bicycle sweeps no longer find? Its pilot found a [[630,2,21]] but the planned sweep was interrupted. Hypothesis: completing the sweep at weight-3 supports per side (check weight 6) would surface undominated high-d points.

What was searched

4,000 random quadricycle candidates via research/quadricycle.py (sample_quadricycle): 2,000 at weight-2 supports (check weight 4, seed 31) and 2,000 at weight-3 supports (check weight 6, seed 32), dims in [2,11] with n = 2·l1·l2·l3·l4 ≤ 700. Screen: 1,500 fast-RIS trials, keeping candidates whose screen efficiency beat their cell bar (eff > 2.0 at w4, > 6.0 at w6). Ladder: 200k then 2M trials (fresh seeds); 66 survivors; full trusted gate (verify/validate_candidate.py, refute=True) on each.
