Z checks come from D[j][l] = -E[j][l xor 1] mod P in the same way. E must satisfy the paper's pair-partition rule: each off-diagonal cell (i < j) carries a perfect matching M on the L block columns, edge-disjoint from the matchings already placed in its row and column, and E[i,u] + E[j,u xor 1] = E[i,v] + E[j,v xor 1] mod P for every matched pair {u, v}.
