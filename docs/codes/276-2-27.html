Direction & hypothesis

Two-block group-algebra codes on non-abelian groups are not constrained to the even-distance pattern of the abelian bicycle codes; dihedral groups are the smallest family to test that at weight 8.

What was searched

Direction dihedral-w4 of the loop samples two-block group-algebra codes on dihedral groups D_m with m in [30, 80] and four group elements per block (check weight 8). Each iteration draws fresh candidates, screens them at 400 and then 20,000 RIS trials (gf2_fast), keeps the twelve best by kd^2/n that no board entry or earlier staged document dominates, and gates them with research/kit/submit.make_submission at 8,000 NumPy trials per side plus verify/validate_candidate.py. This code is iteration 609 (seed 20261626). By 29 October the direction had run 277 iterations over 33,240 candidates and staged 218; across all five directions the loop staged 1,383 documents in 390.6 CPU hours. The 26 October review kept 49 staged codes that neither the board nor another staged code dominated, and each of the 49 was re-searched on OLCF Lux (10,000,000 gf2_fast trials, two seeds) and on Perlmutter A100s (300,000,000 RIS trials per side, two seeds).
