[[31,16,5]] — cyclic XYZ stabilizer code at n=31, k=16

Direction & hypothesis

Target: stabilizer, weight-9plus × unrestricted, a high-rate non-CSS code. The cyclic XYZ construction of arXiv:2611.30654v1 (Baldelli et al.) admits a non-reversible variant at n = 31 with three paired cyclotomic orbits, one coset per Pauli type, giving k = 16 and reported d = 5.

What was searched

Implemented the construction for n = 31: factored x^31 - 1 over F_2, selected one coset from each of the three paired orbits for X, Y, Z (leaving the reciprocal cosets uncovered), formed the circulant parity checks, and assembled the stabilizer generators. One candidate was built and gated; the distance witness was found by enumerating weight-5 Pauli operators commuting with all generators.

Tools

Reproduction

n = 31. Factor x^31 - 1 over F_2 into M_0 = x+1 and six degree-5 factors forming three reciprocal pairs. Pick one factor from each pair, e.g. f_X = M_1, f_Y = M_7, f_Z = M_5, leaving their reciprocals M_15, M_3, M_11 uncovered (any such choice gives an equivalent code up to a multiplier). Then H_σ is the 31 × 31 circulant matrix whose rows are the cyclic shifts of the coefficients of h_σ = (x^31-1)/f_σ; take the first 5 rows of each. Generators: X-type rows of H_X as pure X; Z-type rows of H_Z as pure Z; Y-type rows of H_Y as Y. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30654v1 .
