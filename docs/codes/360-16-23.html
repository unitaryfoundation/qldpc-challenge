[[360,16,23]] — weight-7 bivariate bicycle code on Z_30 x Z_6

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted (maximum check weight 7), CSS board, n between 221 and 400. Before this code, the frontier (board plus this campaign's open PRs) at check weight <= 7, d >= 11 and n from 300 to 360 ran through 300-20-17, 336-12-24, 340-16-22, 360-12-25, 360-24-20. The hypothesis was that random 3 + 4-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on tori with 2lm in 300..400, prefiltered for k >= 14, still reach points there that the board does not hold.

[[360,16,23]] does: no board entry has n <= 360, k >= 16, d >= 23 and check weight <= 7. It is not a d-only gain (no board entry has the same n, k and weight with a lower d). Its maximum check weight is 7 (3 + 4 terms): weight-8 entries may reach these parameters, but no entry of check weight <= 7 does.

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A of 3 and B of 4 monomials, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 300 <= 2lm <= 400; the torus is drawn uniformly from that list and the other monomials uniformly from the torus.

This sweep (seed 721) drew 500,010 codes in a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter; 238 had k >= 14 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 4 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and 4 were taken to 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing; 1 survived that and was staged.

Dead ends

  • In this sweep 3 of 4 screen-depth survivors collapsed below the frontier by 2,000,000 trials: 400-trial readings run several units high at this n.
  • Earlier in this campaign on the same band: cyclic generalized-bicycle codes with a designed divisor at weights (3,3) and (4,4), random-support 2BGA on non-abelian groups at check weight 8, non-abelian lifted products, and weight-6 BB/2BGA at k >= 28 gave nothing that survived 20,000 trials on the frontier.
