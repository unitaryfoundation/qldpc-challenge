Direction & hypothesis

Produced by campaign cc-deletion-peel-scaled-2026-11-18: the scaled sector-targeted deletion/peel/puncture sweep of code-calculus v0.3.0 over the board's own CSS parents (n <= 120, check weight <= 8; 889 parents). Hypothesis (the campaign card's): removal moves extend the v0.1 rounds' yield to depth-3 chains at recorded cost, and each new (n, k, d, w) Pareto point in a populated cell is a frontier extension; this entry advanced its cell on k+n.

What was searched

248 children over the first 81 parents of the pool (n = 108..120), each child exported, gated, and screened before its chain leaned on it. This entry is the step-2 child of the depth-3 chain rooted at codes/120-62-2.json: [[120,62,2]] -> [[120,63,2]] -> [[120,64,2]] -> [[120,65,2]]. Evidence trail

Final claim: witness-backed upper bounds on both sides, not exact.

Dead ends

51 of the 81 parents processed produced no child the gate would pass; the refusals were mostly exact duplicates of adjacent board entries (removing one displayed check re-derives a neighbour's display) or children dominated on (n, k, d, w) by the dense weight-8 population already on the board. 14 children were refused by the gate's own refutation search for over-claimed distances. 54 gate-passed children were dominated and recorded non-frontier, not submitted.
