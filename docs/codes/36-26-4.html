[[36,26,4]] — bordered multi-circulant GF(4) Hermitian stabilizer code

Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the general stabilizer board (unrestricted / any weight) at moderate blocklengths ($33 \le n \le 49$) where Hermitian self-orthogonal multi-circulant and bordered codes over $\mathbb{F}_4$ achieve strictly higher minimum distance than pure CSS constructions. Closes the open CodeTables.de entry for [[36, 26]]_2 from 3..4 to 4 (matching the linear-programming upper bound, and [[37, 26]]_2 from 3..4 to 4 via extension). Advances the high-rate stabilizer Pareto frontier in unrestricted / any weight.

What was searched

Constructed exact Hermitian self-orthogonal $[n, m]_4$ generator matrices in $\mathbb{F}_4[x]/(x^m - 1)$ via Meet-in-the-Middle norm-table matching ($\sum_{i=1}^{\ell-1} a_i(x) a_i^\dagger(x) \equiv -1$ unbordered, or $-1 - \sum_{t=0}^{m-1} x^t$ with an all-ones border column), followed by forced-pivot minimum-weight screening on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 GPU and matroid greedy basis reduction over $\mathbb{F}_2$ to minimize maximum Pauli check weight (reducing max_weight to 24). Source archive: github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, certificates/QECC_q2_n36_k26_d4.json (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24518038).

Evidence trail

  • Witness-backed upper bound in codes/36-26-4.json: d <= 4 (upper_bound), confirmed by cli/qldpc.py with 20,000 RIS trials plus 2,000,000 gf2_fast symplectic-doubling trials finding a minimum-weight Pauli logical witness of weight 4 and nothing lighter.
  • Independent projective Meet-in-the-Middle enumeration over $\mathbb{F}_4^m$ (github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, verify_all_qecc.py) exhaustively verified zero nonzero codewords in $C^{\perp_H}$ of Hamming weight $\le 3$.
  • Verified locally with verify/qldpc_verify.py and verify/validate_candidate.py (passed: true).

Dead ends

  • Pure single-circulant ($\ell = 2$) Hermitian constructions at $(n, k) = (36, 26)$ failed to reach distance 4 due to cyclic weight traps; moving to $\ell \ge 3$ blocks (and $+1$ border where $n \equiv 1 \pmod m$) broke the bottleneck.
  • Raw Ashikhmin-Knill symplectic expansion produced higher check weights (16 to 32); enumerating all $2^{n-k} - 1$ nonzero stabilizer elements and selecting a matroid-minimum $\mathbb{F}_2$-basis lowered maximum check weight to 24 while preserving the identical stabilizer group.

Reproduction

Bordered 7-block circulant Hermitian self-orthogonal [36, 5]_4 linear code [I_5 | M(a_1) | ... | M(a_6) | 1_5] over GF(4) = GF(2)[w]/(w^2+w+1) with first columns a_1=(w,0,0,1,w), a_2=(w,0,1,1+w,1+w), a_3=(1+w,0,1+w,1,1+w), a_4=(0,w,1,0,1), a_5=(w,w,1,1+w,1), a_6=(1,0,w,0,1) plus all-ones border. Full symplectic generator supports and Pauli logical witness are recorded directly in codes/36-26-4.json (also archived at github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, certificates/QECC_q2_n36_k26_d4.json).
