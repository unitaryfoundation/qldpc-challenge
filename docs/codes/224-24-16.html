[[224,24,16]] — weight-8 bivariate bicycle code on Z_28 x Z_4

Direction & hypothesis

Cell weight-8 x unrestricted, CSS board, n between 221 and 250. Before this filing the weight-8 frontier at the bottom of that range was 224-24-10 (weight 7), 224-22-16, 224-18-15, 224-12-19, 232-62-12 and 240-28-16: at n <= 224 no entry with k >= 24 had d above 10, and the k = 22, d = 16 point 224-22-16 was the best at d = 16. The hypothesis was that four-plus-four-term bivariate bicycle (BB) codes on tori with 2lm in 222..300 could add logicals at the same length and distance. [[224,24,16]] does: no board entry has n <= 224, k >= 24, d >= 16 and check weight <= 8. It dominates 224-22-16 by two logical qubits (same n, d and weight), so the gain is on k, not a d-only gain.

What was searched

Random-support BB codes H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] with A and B four monomials each, both containing 1 (no loss of generality), on every torus Z_l x Z_m with l >= m >= 3 and 222 <= 2lm <= 300, the torus drawn uniformly from that list and the other three monomials of each polynomial uniformly from the torus.

Random supports rarely give k >= 16, so the sweep ran a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter: 300,000 draws (seed 103), of which 1,286 had k >= 16 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 4 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and were confirmed at 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing and skipping any already dominated by a point staged earlier in the campaign. This was the only one staged.

Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 16, not an exact distance. The bound was 16 at 400 trials and never moved; both sides have a weight-16 logical.

Dead ends

  • Whole-band 4 + 4 BB sweeps (222 <= n <= 400; 100,000 to 300,000

    • prefiltered draws each) spent most draws above n = 300; at n <= 250 they gave only [[240,20,18]] (filed separately).

  • A 2 + 6 split of the same check weight (200,000 draws, 1,711 with

    • k >= 16) produced nothing that advanced even at 400 screen trials.

  • Cyclic generalized-bicycle codes with a designed divisor g | x^m - 1,

    • weight-4 a and b (check weight 8): weight-4 multiples of g exist only for deg g <= ~22 at odd m from 111 to 200, and 4,000 such draws gave nothing advancing even at 400 trials.

  • Random-support 2BGA on non-abelian groups at check weight 8, 16,000

    • serial plus 150,000 prefiltered draws: every screen-depth survivor collapsed by 20,000 trials.
