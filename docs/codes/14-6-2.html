construction Twisted-torus bivariate-bicycle code from Generalized toric codes on twisted tori for quantum error correction (PRX Quantum 6, 020357, 2025). Stabilizers f(x,y)=1+x+y, g(x,y)=1+y+x on the abelian quotient group Z2/L with twist basis a_1=[0, 7], a_2=[1, 2] (n=2|det[a_1,a_2]|=2*7). Reconstructed from the published polynomials and twist; CSS form H_X=[f|g], H_Z=[gbar|fbar].

notes Reconstructed baseline from Liang, Liu, Song, Chen, Generalized toric codes on twisted tori for quantum error correction (PRX Quantum 6, 020357, 2025), arXiv:2503.03827. The [[n,k,d]] parameter set and stabilizer polynomials are published in that paper; this entry reproduces them.
