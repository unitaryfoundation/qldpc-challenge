[[408,18,32]] — weight-8 cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_204 (designed divisor, supports of weight 4 and 4)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8 (check weight 8), n band 401 to 600, where the w <= 8 frontier at k between 16 and 40 is set by generalized-bicycle codes at n 336 to 378 ([[336,28,20]], [[336,24,24]], [[378,32,19]], [[378,18,27]]) and by [[434,16,22]], [[438,22,21]] and [[464,32,12]] inside the band. The hypothesis: with k designed through the divisor structure of x^m - 1 and low-weight multiples enumerated exactly rather than sampled, m with a rich factorization (composite m, even m included) give k 18 to 30 at distances in the high twenties at n 400 to 600, above what the n ~ 340 to 380 entries reach. The target was a strict Pareto advance in the weight-8 cell by a margin, not a tie.

What was searched

  • Family: cyclic generalized-bicycle codes H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)] over Z_m with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) designed through a divisor, for every m in [201, 300] (n = 2m in [402, 600]), odd and even m alike (for even m, x^m - 1 = (x^(m/2) - 1)^2 and the prime-power factors carry multiplicities).
  • Two exact enumerators replace Prange sampling. Weight-3 words: for every prime-power divisor q of x^m - 1 the residues x^i mod q are tabulated and every trinomial 1 + x^i + x^j with q | a is read off (r[j] = r[i] + 1), which gives deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) for every trinomial pair exactly, so all weight-(3,3) pairs with k in band are seen for every m. Weight-4 words: divisors g with degree in [8, 36] are sampled (up to 100 to 150 per m, excluding multiples of x^m' + 1 for proper m' | m, whose multiples factor as (1 + x^m')(...) and have tiny distance) and every weight-3 and weight-4 multiple of g containing x^0 is listed from the residue table mod g; up to 150 to 300 random pairs per divisor give the (3,4) and (4,4) candidates.
  • Pairs are deduplicated under rotation, the multiplier automorphisms x -> x^u, swap and reciprocal; pairs whose offsets share a factor with m (direct sums) are dropped before any trial is spent.
  • Funnel: 60,117 distinct pairs screened at 600 gf2_fast RIS trials (1 thread each, 24 to 56 worker processes); a pair is promoted only while its reading still clears the board's frontier threshold for its (n, k, w) (the smallest d no entry with n' <= n, k' >= k, w' <= w reaches): 8,036 reached 20,000 trials, 196 reached 200,000 trials, 187 survived; survivors were then re-measured at 2,000,000 fresh-seed trials (60 threads) and the best two per (n, k, w) cell staged, gated and laddered.

Dead ends

  • Degenerate divisors. A divisor g that is a multiple of x^m' + 1 for a proper m' | m has plenty of weight-4

    • multiples, all of the form (1 + x^m')(1 + x^i): on Z_201 (g = x^67 + 1) they gave k = 134 at d <= 3, on Z_204 k = 102 to 136 at d 2 to 4, and the pairs with k 58 to 70 on Z_203 read d 2 to 7 at 600 trials. Every such divisor was excluded from the sweep after the smoke test; the surviving large-k cells (k 34 to 72) never read above d 13 at 600 trials, so no weight-8 cyclic GB with k >= 34 came near the d >= 15 that would advance the cell.

  • Weight-(3,3) pairs (the weight-6 cell). Enumerated exhaustively for every m in [201, 300]: pairs sharing a divisor

    • of degree >= 7 exist at m = 204, 210, 216, 217, 252, 254, 273 and a few others, but almost all of them have their offsets inside a proper subgroup of Z_m (direct sums, dropped before any trial), and the connected ones at k 14 to 16 read d 22 to 26 at 200,000 trials, which ties or trails [[420,14,22]], [[434,16,22]], [[508,14,24]] and [[510,16,26]]; nothing with k >= 18 at check weight 6 cleared its threshold. The weight-6 cell at k >= 18 stays empty above d 10 for this family.

  • Screening inflation. In the (408, 18, w8) cell 40 pairs cleared 200,000 trials at readings 28 to 35; the

    • 2,000,000-trial pass cut the best of them from 35 to 32, where three fresh 20,000,000-trial seeds then held. The (408, 20, w8) and (408, 24, w8) readings of 26 to 28 at 200,000 trials sit at or just above their thresholds (25) and were not pursued further in this PR.

  • Random 2BGA on metacyclic groups of order 201 to 300 (3+3, 3+4, 4+4 supports with the identity): k = 0 in

    • 49 percent of draws and k >= 14 in about 2 percent; the k >= 14 survivors at 200,000 trials were k = 14 to 18 codes whose readings (39 to 70) are far above anything plausible and are being re-measured; none is claimed here.
