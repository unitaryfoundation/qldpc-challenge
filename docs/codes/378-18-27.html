Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: weight-8 x unrestricted (CSS), which is where the verifier places a check weight of 8. The source is arXiv:2608.08996 Table 1, published at n = 378, k = 18, w = 10 on the estimator's upper bound of 27. Eight of that paper's other codes are already board entries citing it, but this one was missed: no entry shares (n, k) = (378, 18) and nothing dominates it at n <= 378, k >= 18, d >= 27, w <= 8, giving kd^2/n = 34.71.

What was searched

No search for the construction: it reproduces from the paper's Supplementary Information, which sits at the end of main.tex after the References and so is invisible to a text search of the article body. G = M_27 x Z_7 with K = {e} (free action) and 1x1/1x1 boundary maps, so the coset-orbit balanced product reduces to the ordinary lifted product over G, i.e. the two-block group-algebra form already in the kit. Every invariant reproduces: n = 378, k = 18 by GF(2) rank, w = 8, H_X H_Z^T = 0 over GF(2), and d = 27 on both Pauli sides. M_27 is built as metacyclic(9, 3, 4) -- C_9 rtimes C_3 with the C_3 action given by x -> x^4 -- times Z_7 on generator u, indexed (i, j, v) -> (i*3 + j)*7 + v. A sibling entry from the same table, [[390,36,<=30]], was also attempted and does NOT reproduce: its check matrices come out full rank on both sides and k = 0, which is inconsistent with the published k = 36, so it is recorded as a dead end rather than fitted to the target.
