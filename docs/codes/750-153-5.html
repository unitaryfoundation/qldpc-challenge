[[750,153,5]] — one X-check deletion from 750-152-7.json (C-DELETE-CHECK a264)

Direction & hypothesis

A high-k board region at 750 <= n reachable by deleting one check from 750-152-7.json at little distance cost. Deletion raises k by one at fixed n and fixed qubit set, so any landing point where the frontier has headroom on k is reachable in one move. The aim was the largest d the deletion could keep while still clearing the cell's floor -- here 5, against a parent claim of 7.

What was searched

All 163 CSS board entries with n > 700, not a sample of them: 88,592 essential single check deletions (rows whose removal changes the stabilizer group; dependent rows are dropped, since removing one yields an identical code). 28,913 of those -- 33% -- free a bare qubit and are d=1 by construction; a structural filter on column weights discarded them before any witness search, leaving 59,679 clean deletions across 109 distinct (n, k+1, d, w) tuples.

Witnesses were then re-measured per candidate rather than inherited: 8000 RIS trials per side, seed 0, via the challenge's own heuristic_distance search. At most 3 clean rows were sampled per tuple, so this is a floor on what the pool contains, not an exhaustive sweep.

Evidence trail

  • Parent 750-152-7.json claims d = 7.
  • Unpriced C-DELETE-CHECK carry from the parent (no child witnesses supplied):

    • interval [0, 7]. Contains the measured child distance.

  • Priced transport, d' = min(d, w_del, w_exp): [0, 7] with

    • w_del = 19, w_exp = 7 (RIS upper bounds, 20,000 trials, seed 0; the true minima are NP-hard, Theorem 24.1, so both are over-estimates).

  • Seed-0 estimate over the child: 5 per side, 8000 trials/side.
  • Gate at seed 5: passed, not refuted, no duplicate, board-advancing on no axis in particular.
  • Advancement: nothing dominates it in its cell.

Dead ends

  • Distance does not survive deletion. The parent's claim was never carried forward: on 14 of

    • the 18 filed entries the child measured strictly below the parent, so inheriting the parent's number would have been an over-claim that the refutation gate catches.

  • 28,913 of 88,592 essential deletions free a bare qubit and land at d=1; only 4 of the 18

    • filed entries are of that kind, the rest being shadowed by d=2 siblings from the same parent.

  • 22 candidates in the working set passed the gate but were dominated by a batch-mate at the

    • same (n, k, w), so 40 were reduced to 18 before filing.

  • Not attempted at this scale: C-ADD-CHECK strict improvement and E-GRAFT. The 2026-10-19

    • fieldnote reports 0 strict improvements out of 736 valid additions for n <= 10 and no graft survivors for n <= 8, so neither was worth a sweep at n = 700-1000 without a new idea.

  • C-DIRECT-SUM is unusable against the connectivity gate regardless of scale.

Tools

Models: Beam 1.0, MiMo-V2.6-Flash (matches provenance.model).

  • Structural work: the challenge's own gf2 and verify/heuristic_distance.py, plus an

    • independent nullspace test for row essentiality.

  • Verification: verify/qldpc_verify.py and verify/check_codes_format.py, unchanged, both

    • passing locally on the committed file.

  • Interval transport: the priced construction calculus at github.com/MathysRennela/code-calculus-dev @ 661d1e0, machine/priced_calculus.py. Its CodeState.delete_check

    • reproduces this entry's child state exactly from the parent, and its Independent(...) premise agreed with the independent nullspace check on all 18 pairs.

The calculus run is evidence against OBL-PRICED-INTERVAL-SOUNDNESS, not confirmation of it: containment held on 18/18 for both the unpriced carry and the priced interval, but the prices themselves are over-estimates and no lower endpoint was exercised (the ledger's lower bound stays 0 throughout).
