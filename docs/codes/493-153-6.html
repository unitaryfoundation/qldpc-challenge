Target cell: weight-9plus × unrestricted, CSS board. The paper arXiv:2610.25213 (LiftDiff) reports a [[493,153,6]] lifted-product code over ℤ₁₇ with base matrix 2×5 and target column weight 2. Its parameter point has no dominator in the current board, so reproducing it is board-advancing.

What was searched

Reimplemented the construction family: cyclic lifted product LP(M,M) over F₂[ℤ₁₇] with base matrix M of size 2×5, every cell nonzero (column weight 2), entries of polynomial weight 1 or 2. Random supports sampled uniformly over group elements; for each candidate built H_X, H_Z via research/kit/nonabelian_lp.lifted_product_base, computed k via compute_k, and screened the distance with research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand. A candidate with k=153, max check weight 9 and surrogate distance at least 6 was found after ~35 k-hits among 500+ trials. The selected candidate has one entry of polynomial weight 2 and the rest weight 1.
