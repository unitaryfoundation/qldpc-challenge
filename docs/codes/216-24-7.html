Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-6 cell at n in 111..220, where the board's frontier for k >= 12 at d >= 8 is thin (frontier map of 2026-10-17). A (216, 24, 7) point at check weight 6 needed d >= 7 to clear every board entry with n' <= 216, k' >= 24, w' <= 6; the gate's novelty check reports it advances the cell on the recorded axes (board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py; literature novelty unverified).

What was searched

Designed-common-zero bivariate bicycle sweep: for every torus Z_l x Z_m with l*m in 56..110 (n in 112..220), a point (alpha, beta) of mu_l' x mu_m' (l', m' the odd parts) with a Frobenius orbit of size t >= 2 (t >= 3 or 4 depending on the run) is fixed first and A, B are drawn only among the 2- and 4-term polynomials that vanish there (so k >= 2t by construction; this code's point is index [1, 6] in Z_3 x Z_9). Funnel for this job: 568747 candidates built, 174366 distinct with k >= 10 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 400 promoted to 20,000 trials, 400 to 200,000 trials, 22 survived with the reading still above the board's weight-6 frontier (any reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).
