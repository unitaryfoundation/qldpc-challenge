[[220,44,12]] — non-abelian lifted product over ZSZ(11,4,10)

Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the weight-9plus × unrestricted frontier at n = 220, k = 44 (20% rate, |G| = 44), using the non-abelian lifted-product ("mitten") construction of arXiv:2607.28795v2 implemented in research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py. On the current board, the existing [[220,44,10]] entry (codes/220-44-10.json, w = 9) left d >= 11 open as a frontier advance in weight-9plus × unrestricted.

What was searched

  • Sampled 200 random 1x2 x 1x2 trinomial lifted-product candidates (A_w = [[3,3]], B_w = [[3,3]], min_order = 3) over ZSZ(11,4,10) (|G| = 44, seed = 402), screened via research/kit/search.py (screen_adaptive, stages = (300, 1500), backend = "fast").
  • Ran single-element hill-climbing (50 mutation steps per seed across the top 8 seeds), mutating one non-identity group element in A or B at a time while preserving k >= 44 and min_order = 3.
  • Candidate si=4, step=28 reached d = 12 at 2,500 fast trials.

Evidence trail

  • 2,500 trials (gf2_fast): d <= 12
  • 40,000 trials (make_submission / distance_rand_witness): d <= 12 (X and Z witnesses)
  • 100,000 trials (gf2_fast): d <= 12
  • verify/validate_candidate.py: passed: true, board_advancing: true, dominated_by: [], 8,000 RIS refutation trials (seed = 2000887451) found nothing lighter.
  • Matched-depth peer audit (research/audits/leader_audit.py pair, 2,000,000 trials, seed = 51) against codes/220-44-10.json: candidate held d <= 12 (side=X) and peer held d <= 10 (side=X), decision credible: both claims held at matched depth -- the gain survives.
  • Final claim: upper_bound (witness-backed, not MILP-certified).

Dead ends

  • Pure random sampling across ZSZ and small_nonabelian_groups for |G| in {54, 56, 66, 72, 80, 84, 90} without local search topped out at d = 12 (n = 270..360), which was dominated by existing w = 8 entries ([[232,62,12]], [[280,74,12]]).
  • Lower-weight 1x2 x 1x2 (w = 7, 8) and higher-rate 1x3 x 1x3 (w = 8) random sweeps topped out at d = 6 ([[240,49,6]]) and d = 9 ([[350,70,9]]).

Tools

  • Model: Gemini Hydrogen 4
  • Kit: research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py, research/kit/search.py, verify/gf2_fast, verify/validate_candidate.py, research/audits/leader_audit.py.
