[[150,33,6]] — lifted product over a group of order 30, check weight 7

Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-8 cell at n in 111..220 with check weight 7. Only board entries with check weight <= 7 can dominate a weight-7 code; at the time of the search no such entry had n' <= 150, k' >= 33 and d' >= 6, so d = 6 was enough here. The gate's novelty check reports that it advances the frontier (k, n, w); board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py, literature novelty unverified.

Hypothesis: a lifted product of two one-row bases over a non-abelian group, A = [a1, a2] and B = [b1, b2] with entries of weight 2 and 3, has n = 5|G| and k >= |G|; because left and right supports need not commute, the weight-2 entries need not create the short cycles an abelian group forces, so d can reach 6 at rate above 1/5. Mixing a weight-3 entry into one base lifts the check weight to 7 and buys extra k over the weight-6 shape.

What was searched

Lifted products of two 1x2 base matrices with entries of weight 2 or 3 (check weight 6..8) over non-abelian groups of order 23..44, n = 5|G|, 1,500 random bases per group. Funnel for this job: 85497 candidates built, 81800 connected with k >= 8 screened at 400 gf2_fast RIS trials, 1 promoted to 20,000 trials, 1 survived it, 1 survived 200,000 trials and 1 survived 2,000,000 trials with the reading still at or above the needed d (a reading below the needed d is final, since the search returns an upper bound).

Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 6: the lightest logical operators found are embedded in the submission (distance.X / distance.Z witnesses) and are checked by the verifier. Confirmation ladder on the box (gf2_fast, research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, 20 threads):

Reproduction

The code is the lifted product over the group G = metacyclic(15,2,11)[0] of order 30 (research/kit/nonabelian_lp.lifted_product_base: entries of A act by the left regular representation, entries of B by the right one).

import sys; sys.path[:0] = ['research/kit', 'verify']
from group_algebra import metacyclic, cyclic_product
from nonabelian_lp import group_from_name

HX, HZ = lifted_product_base(mul, [[[0, 23], [0, 2, 16]]], [[[0, 5], [0, 13]]])

Witness search: research/kit/surrogate.lightest_logical (1,500 NumPy trials) tightened by gf2_fast RIS at the funnel depth; the ladder seeds are listed in the table above.
