[[930,18,36]] — weight-6 cyclic GB code over Z_465 (trinomial pair, gcd of degree 9)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6, in the n > 700 tier where the verifier admits a claim only with max check weight <= 8 and d <= 40. Above n = 700 the weight-6 column is thin and mostly inherited from below: for k = 17..20 the best entries were [[672,20,32]], [[630,20,24]] and [[682,20,22]], so a weight-6 code with k = 18 at n <= 930 advances once d >= 33. A trinomial has a gcd with x^m - 1 of degree 9 only when m has several small cyclotomic orders; 465 = 3 x 5 x 31 gives irreducible factors of degree 4 (from the fifth roots of unity) and 5 (from the 31st), and the hypothesis was that a trinomial pair sharing one factor of each has its lightest logicals above 32 at m = 465.

What was searched

Exhaustive weight-(3,3) cyclic GB enumeration over m = 361..500 with designed k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 18..80: every trinomial 1 + x^i + x^j with gcd degree >= 9, pairs canonicalized under units of Z_m and cyclic shifts, pairs whose exponents share a factor with m dropped (direct sums). 3,040 pairs in band over 140 m, subsampled to 2,026 (at most 400 per m); a companion run over m = 351..360 with k >= 12 gave 1,500 pairs. Screening used gf2_fast RIS through research/kit/surrogate.py at pair depth 8: 1,000 trials on all 3,526 pairs, 20,000 on the 282 k = 18 survivors, 300,000 on the best 16 at m = 465, then fresh-seed ladders on the leaders.
