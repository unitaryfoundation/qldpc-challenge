Direction & hypothesis

Targeted a Pareto opening in the unrestricted / weight-22 cell via the priced construction calculus (unitaryfoundation/code-calculus v0.1): E-PUNCTURE a qubit (or check) of a CSS parent, recomputing ranks, connectivity, and max check weight. The child is board-advancing when its (n,k,d,w) is nondominated in its cell.

What was searched

Single-step E-PUNCTURE moves over CSS parents, screened structurally (no bare qubit, connected Tanner graph, independent displayed rows) and by an optimistic 4-axis domination test against the board before any witness search. Survivors were packaged with research/kit/submit.py (make_submission, save_submission) and validated with research/kit/coordination.py gate (passed: true).

Evidence trail
