[[630,26,35]] — weight-8 cyclic GB code over Z_315 (designed divisor, degree 13)

Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8. At n = 630 the weight-8 column is held by [[630,34,32]] (designed-divisor two-block code on Z_315, w = 4 + 4); a code with k in 26..34 at n <= 630 needs d >= 33 to advance it. The hypothesis was that a divisor of degree 11..14 (k = 22..28) leaves enough weight-4 words in its ideal for pairs whose distance clears the k = 32..34 entries, and 315 = 3^2 x 5 x 7 gives x^315 - 1 many small irreducible factors to build divisors from.

What was searched

Designed-divisor cyclic GB codes over odd m in 301..499: up to 40 random divisors g of x^m - 1 per m with deg g in 11..30, weight-3 and weight-4 multiples of g from 400 Prange rounds (sparse_multiples in research/kit/gf2poly.py), pairs in weight bands (3,4) and (4,4) with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 22..60, direct sums dropped. 1,528 pairs over 46 fertile m; at m = 315 the (4,4) band gave 10 pairs with k = 26, 8 with k = 30, 6 with k = 32 and 5 with k = 38. Screening used gf2_fast RIS through research/kit/surrogate.py at pair depth 8: 3,000 trials each, 20,000 on 92 survivors, 300,000 on the best 18, then fresh-seed ladders on the leaders.
