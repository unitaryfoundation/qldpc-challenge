Direction & hypothesis

Dead ends

34 of the 36 screen-passers were refuted by the trusted gate, all invalid: verifier rejected — catalog MILP-incumbent distances did not survive independent witness search. Notable collapses: [[288,32,8]] w6, [[360,24,10]] w6, [[144,16,8]] w6. Lesson: MILP incumbents are optimistic upper bounds; expect the earned distance to come in lower.
