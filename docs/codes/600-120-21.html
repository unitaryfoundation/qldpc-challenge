[[600,120,21]] irregular 4x10 weight-8 pair-partition CPM lift over Z_60

Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: CSS, weight-8 x unrestricted, at rate 1/5. Campaign research/campaigns/high-rate-witness-gpu-2026-11-15/campaign.json (issue #5812) asks for high-rate codes at d >= 7 whose distances are witnessed at 300M trials. The regular pair-partition CPM family of arXiv:2607.14091 already fills this cell up to d = 18 at n <= 700 (codes/568-146-18.json and its neighbours). The hypothesis was that an irregular template, a 4 x 10 array of circulant permutation blocks with two blocks per row left empty, so that every check has weight 8 and qubits have degree 3 or 4 on each side, trades rate for distance and passes d = 18 at n = 600.

What was searched

Random lifts of one fixed 4 x 10 weight-8 template at P in {50, 55, 60}, two sweep seeds per P. Shift tables were drawn from the integer null space of the template's pair-partition commutation constraints; draws with a 4-cycle in either Tanner graph or with two equal columns were rejected. 13 instances were built and screened with the verify/gf2_fast.cpp accelerator at 2,000,000 trials (seeds 11 and 12, pair depth 32), keeping d >= 19. The whole campaign run screened 1,101 fresh constructions: 50 lifts of this template and its weight-7 variant, and 1,051 non-abelian lifted products. 30 survived the screen, in 17 distinct (n, k, w), and four were taken to the GPU.

The later fresh-seed rungs, the gate replay and the packaging were done after the search, in a separate Claude Code session (Claude Fable 5.2).

Dead ends

  • The weight-7 variant of the template gave [[600,120,13]] and [[250,50,9]] at

    • the same depth: one less check weight, much lower distance.

  • 1,051 non-abelian lifted products (1x2 base shape, check weights 6 and 7)

    • gave one GPU-measured survivor, [[540,112,8]], which has the WL signature of codes/540-112-8.json and was dropped.

  • 13 of the 17 distinct screen survivors were not GPU-measured and their

    • witnesses were not kept; nothing is claimed for them.

  • The campaign's transform stage (code-calculus moves on seeded high-rate

    • parents) and blocklengths above 600 were not run.
