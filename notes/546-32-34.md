# [[546,32,34]] — weight-8 cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_273 (designed divisor, supports of weight 4 and 4)

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8 (check weight 8), n band 401 to 600, where the w <= 8 frontier at k between 16 and 40 is set by generalized-bicycle codes at n 336 to 378 ([[336,28,20]], [[336,24,24]], [[378,32,19]], [[378,18,27]]) and a few entries inside the band. The hypothesis: with k designed through the divisor structure of x^m - 1 (or through the group algebra of a non-abelian group) and low-weight multiples enumerated exactly rather than sampled, n 400 to 600 gives k 18 to 30 at distances above what the n ~ 340 to 380 entries reach. At (n, k) = (546, 32) the smallest distance not dominated by a board entry with n' <= n, k' >= k, w' <= 8 was 20 when this was gated; the target was a strict Pareto advance by a margin, not a tie.

## What was searched

- Family: cyclic generalized-bicycle codes H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)] over Z_m with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) designed through a divisor, for every m in [201, 300] (n = 2m in [402, 600]), odd and even m alike (for even m, x^m - 1 = (x^(m/2) - 1)^2 and the prime-power factors carry multiplicities).
- Two exact enumerators replace Prange sampling. Weight-3 words: for every prime-power divisor q of x^m - 1 the residues x^i mod q are tabulated and every trinomial 1 + x^i + x^j with q | a is read off (r[j] = r[i] + 1), which gives deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) for every trinomial pair exactly, so all weight-(3,3) pairs with k in band are seen for every m. Weight-4 words: divisors g with degree in [8, 36] are sampled (up to 100 to 150 per m, excluding multiples of x^m' + 1 for proper m' | m, whose multiples factor as (1 + x^m')(...) and have tiny distance) and every weight-3 and weight-4 multiple of g containing x^0 is listed from the residue table mod g; up to 150 to 300 random pairs per divisor give the (3,4) and (4,4) candidates.
- Pairs are deduplicated under rotation, the multiplier automorphisms x -> x^u, swap and reciprocal; pairs whose offsets share a factor with m (direct sums) are dropped before any trial is spent.
- Funnel: 122,376 distinct pairs screened at 600 gf2_fast RIS trials (1 thread each, 24 to 56 worker processes); a pair is promoted only while its reading still clears the board's frontier threshold for its (n, k, w) (the smallest d no entry with n' <= n, k' >= k, w' <= w reaches): 11,500 reached 20,000 trials, 549 reached 200,000 trials, 474 survived; survivors were then re-measured at 1,000,000 and 20,000,000 fresh-seed trials (26 to 60 threads) and the best per (n, k, w) cell staged, gated and laddered.

## Dead ends

- Screening inflation at n >= 500. The 200,000-trial readings of the sweep survivors are far from converged at
  these lengths: a [[584,22]] weight-8 cyclic pair read 85 at 600 trials and 30 at 20,000; a [[504,18]] pair read
  52 at 200,000, 42 at 1,000,000 and 2,000,000, and 40 at 20,000,000; a [[600,14]] metacyclic 2BGA read 62 at
  2,000,000 and the gate's own pass found 50; a [[546,26]] weight-8 cyclic pair read 43 at 1,000,000, 39 at
  20,000,000, and one of three further 20,000,000-trial seeds found 37. Every survivor was therefore re-measured
  at 1,000,000 and then 10,000,000 (weight 6) or 20,000,000 (weight 8) fresh-seed trials before it was staged.
- Degenerate divisors (cyclic sweep). A divisor g that is a multiple of x^m' + 1 for a proper m' | m has many
  weight-4 multiples of the form (1 + x^m')(1 + x^i), and every such pair had d <= 7 at 600 trials (k = 102 to
  136 at d 2 to 4 on Z_204, k = 134 at d <= 3 on Z_201); these divisors were excluded from the sweep.
- Weight-(3,3) cyclic pairs. Enumerated exhaustively for every m in [201, 300]: the connected pairs with k 14 to 16
  read d 22 to 26 at 200,000 trials, tying or trailing [[420,14,22]], [[434,16,22]], [[508,14,24]] and
  [[510,16,26]]; no cyclic weight-6 pair with k >= 18 cleared its threshold, so the cyclic family does not reach
  the empty k 20 to 60 region of the weight-6 cell.
- Random 2BGA on metacyclic groups of order 201 to 300: k = 0 in 49 percent of 400,000 draws (3+3, 3+4, 4+4
  supports) and k >= 14 in about 2 percent. Weight-6 draws with k = 24 to 32 at n = 504 to 560 (C_p x|_r C_q of
  order 252 to 280) read only d 12 at 200,000 trials, one above the threshold, and were not pursued; the
  weight-8 metacyclic survivors read 39 to 74 at 200,000 trials, readings the deeper passes cut sharply
  wherever they were re-measured (first item).
