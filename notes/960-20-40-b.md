\# \[\[960,20,40]] coset lifted product code, check weight 6



\*\*Source.\*\* R. Rajpurohit, V. Lalitha, S. G. Srinivasa, \*Coset Lifted Product Codes\*, \[arXiv:2610.12782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12782). This entry is one of the large-block codes of that paper's extended search (m = 240 family, Section V); the construction, the search procedure and the GAP scripts are the paper's. Data and scripts: https://github.com/rajendrarajpurohit/CLP\_QLDPC.



\## Construction



A coset lifted product code (arXiv:2610.12782, Definition III.1). Let G = SmallGroup(1200,642) in GAP's SmallGroups library, with polycyclic generating sequence f1, ..., f7, and H = <f6\*f7>, a cyclic subgroup of order 5, so m = \[G:H] = 240. The protograph a is an element of F\_2\[G] acting on the cosets G/H by the left action; the protograph B is a 2x2 matrix over F\_2\[N\_G(H)] acting by the right action. With



\- a = 1 + f3\*f4\*f5\*f6^4\*f7^3 + f2\*f3\*f4\*f5^2\*f6^2,

\- B = \[\[1, 1 + f1\*f2\*f3\*f4\*f5\*f7], \[1 + f1\*f2\*f3\*f4, f1\*f2\*f3\*f4\*f5\*f7^4]],



the 1x1 over 2x2 layout gives n = 4m = 960 qubits, k = 20, every check of weight 6 and every qubit in six checks. The matrices in the entry are the ones gap/rebuild.g of the repository above produces from these data.



\## Distance



| search | budget | result |

|---|---|---|

| QDistRnd, both sides (paper's screen) | 2 x 10^5 rounds per side | d <= 56 |

| this repository, fast RIS (`verify/gate\_changed.py`) | 8 x 10^6 trials, seed 6980315 | no logical lighter than 48 on either side |

| this repository, orbit-fold with pynauty automorphisms and SAT stage (`verify/heuristic\_distance.py`) | 3 x 10^3 trials, seeds 1-4; 3 x 10^4 trials | weight-40 logicals on both sides, carried as the witnesses; nothing lighter |



The entry claims d <= 40 with a weight-40 X-type and a weight-40 Z-type witness, both checked by the verifier. The distance is not certified exact. Q = 20 \* 40^2 / 960 = 33.3.



\## Relation to the board



At d = 40 and check weight 6, no entry with n <= 960 and k >= 20 reaches this distance. The code is distinct from the \[\[960,20]] entry built on SmallGroup(480,1005) (a different group and protographs, not a walker relative); it is filed because the random-information-set search leaves it at 48 after 8 x 10^6 trials, where the earlier code read 42, which suggests its lightest logicals are harder for that search to reach.



\## Provenance



Classical construction: a coset lifted product code in the framework of arXiv:2610.12782, obtained by the search procedure described in that paper over the GAP SmallGroups library (GAP 4.15.1 with the SmallGroups and QDistRnd packages). No AI model was used in the search or the construction. Authors of the construction: Rajendra Rajpurohit, V. Lalitha, Shayan Garani Srinivasa (arXiv:2610.12782).



\## Reproduction



gap/rebuild.g in https://github.com/rajendrarajpurohit/CLP\_QLDPC, with G, H, a and B as given above, rebuilds HX and HZ; `verify/qldpc\_verify.py` on the entry checks the matrices, the weights and both witnesses.
