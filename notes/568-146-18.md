## What was searched

First reproduce the matrices of codes/904-230-22.json exactly from its
exponents. Then solve the 36 paired-difference equations in 48 variables
over F_P. Their nullity is 19. Draw null-space coefficients with NumPy's
seeded generator and mutate one coefficient at a time, modulo P.

Enumerate signed exponent forms of closed non-backtracking base walks of
length 4, 6 and 8 on the 3-by-8 protograph. Identify equal forms and forms
related by an overall sign; discard the identically zero form. There are
84, 336 and 4830 forms per side. Minimize lexicographically the total number
of vanishing length-4/6 forms, then vanishing length-8 forms. Reject an
increasing move; accept ties. Retain only draws with no length-4/6 zero.
This objective counts distinct exponent forms, not all rooted walks.

Pilot: 32 draws, seed 202610041, cyclic lift schedule 41,43,47,53,67,71,
2000 proposed coefficient moves per restart. Focused follow-up: 64 draws,
seed 202610042, schedule 53,67,71,41, 6000 moves per restart. Each draw
received exact rank, CSS and weight checks, 16 NumPy witness trials per
side, and 2000 accelerated RIS trials per side at pair depth 8, four
threads. Preserve returned witnesses and maintain best-distance bins by
(n,k). All screening values are upper bounds.

The selected matrix is pilot index 17, P=71, with 136 vanishing length-8
forms summed over both sides. Its ranks are 211 and 211, n=568, k=146,
all check rows have weight 8, and all columns have weight 3 on each side.

## Reproduction

The following independent recipe reconstructs the parity checks, in the
same ordering as the submitted JSON:

```python
import numpy as np
P = 71
EX = [[61,36,7,60,1,43,13,29],
      [26,57,18,70,8,26,66,6],
      [22,65,41,34,66,63,9,12]]
EZ = [[1,59,20,3,12,57,26,52],
      [15,56,32,36,68,63,9,5],
      [63,13,55,24,36,53,23,31]]
def lift(E):
    H = np.zeros((3*P,8*P), dtype=np.int8)
    r = np.arange(P)
    for i in range(3):
        for j in range(8):
            H[i*P+r,j*P+(r-E[i][j])%P] = 1
    return H
HX,HZ = lift(EX),lift(EZ)
assert not np.any((HX @ HZ.T) % 2)
```

The matching triple is M0=(0,4)(1,7)(2,6)(3,5),
M1=(0,7)(1,2)(3,4)(5,6), M2=(0,2)(1,5)(3,7)(4,6).
For block-row pair (i,j), use M[(i-j) mod 3]. Each pair (a,b) obeys
EX[i,a]-EX[i,b]-EZ[j,a]+EZ[j,b]=0 modulo 71, giving even mixed overlap.
