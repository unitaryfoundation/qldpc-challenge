# [[674,168,76]] - Frobenius-paired cyclic generalized-bicycle code

## Direction & hypothesis

The search targeted the unrestricted operational frontier near blocklength 674.
The prior entry in this parameter cell was [[674,168,74]], so a witnessed
distance above 74 would advance the frontier even if it did not exceed the
global operational score. The hypothesis was that pairing a sparse cyclic word
with a Frobenius-related partner could retain the degree-84 common factor needed
for k=168 while suppressing the short logicals common in random Z_337 bicycle
codes.

The submitted claim is the witness-backed upper bound d <= 76. It is not an
exact-distance claim. The code has maximum check weight 30 and score
168*76^2/674 = 1439.715134, improving the previous [[674,168,74]] score of
1364.937685.

## What was searched

A private cyclic-ideal miner sampled 1,195,273 Frobenius-paired Z_337 proposals
before stopping at its survivor quota. Four profiles used weight-15 or weight-16
cyclic words, exponent multiplier 128, and partners a(x^4) or a(x^8), with exact
polynomial-gcd filtering before distance work. This instance appeared at sample
1,120,660 under the weight-15, partner-4 profile. The campaign produced eight
shallow survivors after 500,000 RIS trials per selected code.

Separate control sweeps searched 10,000,000 Z_341 proposals and 5,000,000
Z_331 proposals. Z_341 produced 41 algebraic hits, all rejected by a quotient
trap; Z_331 produced three hits and no survivor.

The submitted X witness is the weight-76 winner from the deeper matched audit.
The submitted Z witness is its generalized-bicycle block-swap and reversal
mirror; both were checked directly against the original parity-check matrices.
That audit measured the candidate and its same-(n,k,w) board peer at identical
budget, depth, thread count, and seed. At the adjusted claim, the candidate
reached 76 and the peer independently reached its filed value 74. This evidence
supports an upper-bound submission only and does not prove that 76 is the exact
distance.

## Dead ends

The strongest shallow candidate in the same Z_337 batch claimed
[[674,170,83]] with score 1737.582, but an eight-million-trial audit found a
weight-77 logical and reduced its score below the global record. A distinct
[[674,168,82]] candidate fell to weight 76. The present code itself screened at
82, survived two independent general audits at 78, and then fell to 76 under
the depth-64 matched audit. These collapses are why it is submitted at the
observed weight 76 rather than its shallow estimate.

The Z_341 quotient failures also showed that common-factor dimension alone was
not predictive: every algebraically promising hit carried a logical of weight
at most 31 in the cheap quotient screen.

## Tools

The search was developed with OpenAI GPT-5 (Codex) and run on an Apple M3 Ultra
Mac Studio. Exact binary-polynomial gcd and GF(2) rank computations screened the
construction; the challenge repository's `gf2_fast` structural and general RIS
backends produced and checked the logical witnesses. No GPU was used. The
discovery campaign consumed approximately 0.44 CPU core-hours before this
candidate was selected; the later confirmation runs are reported separately in
the evidence ladder.

## Reproduction

Work in F_2[x]/(x^337+1). Let

```text
A = [0, 2, 9, 19, 45, 68, 98, 163, 183, 206, 223, 234, 264, 280, 295]
B = [0, 8, 36, 45, 55, 58, 76, 109, 150, 169, 180, 218, 262, 272, 315]
```

Here B is a cyclic shift of the support obtained from A by exponent multiplier
4, and A is invariant under exponent multiplier 128 modulo 337. With C(a) the
337 by 337 binary circulant generated by A, define

```text
H_X = [C(a) | C(b)]
H_Z = [C(b)^T | C(a)^T].
```

The matrices commute, each side has rank 253, and therefore
k=674-253-253=168. Each check has weight 30. The full check matrices and both
logical witnesses are serialized in the accompanying code JSON.
