# [[630,26,35]] — weight-8 cyclic GB code over Z_315 (designed divisor, degree 13)

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8. At n = 630 the weight-8 column is held by
[[630,34,32]] (designed-divisor two-block code on Z_315, w = 4 + 4); a code
with k in 26..34 at n <= 630 needs d >= 33 to advance it. The hypothesis was
that a divisor of degree 11..14 (k = 22..28) leaves enough weight-4 words in
its ideal for pairs whose distance clears the k = 32..34 entries, and 315 =
3^2 x 5 x 7 gives x^315 - 1 many small irreducible factors to build divisors
from.

## What was searched

Designed-divisor cyclic GB codes over odd m in 301..499: up to 40 random
divisors g of x^m - 1 per m with deg g in 11..30, weight-3 and weight-4
multiples of g from 400 Prange rounds (`sparse_multiples` in
research/kit/gf2poly.py), pairs in weight bands (3,4) and (4,4) with k = 2 deg
gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 22..60, direct sums dropped. 1,528 pairs over 46 fertile
m; at m = 315 the (4,4) band gave 10 pairs with k = 26, 8 with k = 30, 6 with
k = 32 and 5 with k = 38. Screening used gf2_fast RIS through
research/kit/surrogate.py at pair depth 8: 3,000 trials each, 20,000 on 92
survivors, 300,000 on the best 18, then fresh-seed ladders on the leaders.

This code is the cautionary case of its sweep: the screens and the
2,000,000-trial rung stopped in the mid 50s, and the first 20,000,000-trial
rung found a weight-35 Z logical. The document was re-staged at d = 35 with
that witness, and the next two 20,000,000-trial rungs on fresh seeds stopped
at exactly 35 again, once on each side. `qldpc submit`'s own 10,000,000-trial
pass (seed 0) only reached 43, which is why this entry was packaged with
research/kit/promote.py from the validated witness instead. The margin over
the [[630,34,32]] bar is 2: a later logical of weight <= 32 would put this
code below that entry.

## Dead ends

- Degree 7..10 divisors (k = 14..20) at m = 301..350: 178 pairs, nothing above
  the [[630,34,32]] bar after 20,000 trials.
- k >= 36 at weight 8 across m = 315..495: about 400 pairs, d <= 18 at 3,000
  trials, except five k = 38 pairs at m = 315 (d <= 42 at 20,000 trials, not
  deepened).
- Near misses at m = 315 and 300,000 trials, not filed given this code's
  collapse from 54 to 35: a second k = 26 pair (a = [26, 126, 132, 174], b =
  [3, 164, 166, 280]) at d <= 54 and the k = 30 pair (a = [10, 46, 178, 181],
  b = [150, 181, 223, 225]) at d <= 34.
- In this family at n ~ 600..700, screens at 300,000 trials overstate the
  distance badly: here by 19, and a k = 22 pair on Z_341 from the same sweep
  went 86 -> 76 -> 67 between 300,000 and 20,000,000 trials.
