# [[144,6,15]] — genon code (stabilizer quotient of a bivariate-bicycle code)

## Direction & hypothesis

Aim: a non-CSS stabilizer entry on the `weight-6 × unrestricted` board.
The paper "Twisted Compactification, Quotient Quantum Codes and Logical Gates"
(arXiv:2611.09538v1) constructs genon codes as quotients of bivariate-bicycle
codes by a ZX-duality. The parent `[[288,12,18]]` BB code has check weight 6;
a free quotient by an involution halves `n` and `k`, giving `[[144,6,?]]`.
If the quotient keeps weight 6 and distance close to the parent's, it should
advance the weight-6 unrestricted stabilizer frontier, which has entries at
`[[144,6,15]]`/`17`/`18` but at higher check weight.

## What was searched

Parent: `l=m=12`, `A=[(3,0),(0,2),(0,7)]`, `B=[(0,3),(1,0),(2,0)]`
(`[[288,12,18]]` BB code). Quotient by
`D_{(0,1)}: g -> (0,1)-g`, which swaps the two blocks and the Pauli letters.
Each X-check orbit gives one stabilizer generator on 144 qubits; the 288
generators have rank 138, so `k=6`, with maximum check weight 6.

Distance: a Pauli logical of weight 15 was found by the gate's refutation
search (seed 1113693782); the candidate's original weight-18 claim was
tightened to 15. No lighter logical was found in 8000 RIS trials on the
final document (seed 335356710) and 2,000,000 accelerator trials.

## Evidence trail

- Gate verdict: `passed: true`, `refuted: false` on the final document.
- `n=144, k=6, d<=15`, Pauli weight, `w=6`, `unrestricted`.
- Witness: `X=[18,27,39,70,79,91,110,131,143]`,
  `Z=[10,39,44,50,84,91,102,136,143]`.
- Board novelty: `board_advancing: true`, `dominated_by: []`,
  `advances_by: [d, k, n]`.

## Dead ends

- A first construction attempt claimed `d<=18`; the gate's refutation found a
  weight-15 logical (seed 1113693782), so the claim was revised to 15.
- Pure X-type and pure Z-type logical searches on the quotient found minimum
  weight 18; the weight-15 logical is a mixed Pauli operator.

## Tools

Model: Beam 1.0. Repository tooling: `research/kit/bb.py` for the parent,
manual quotient construction, `verify/qldpc_verify.py`, and the gate's RIS
refutation.

## Reproduction

Build the parent with `build_bb(12, 12, [(3,0),(0,2),(0,7)], [(0,3),(1,0),(2,0)])`
from `research/kit/bb.py`. Quotient by `D_{(0,1)}: g -> (0,1)-g`, pairing
each X-check with its image Z-check; the resulting 288 generators on 144 qubits
are the `checks.S` of `codes/144-6-15-b.json`. Parameters and witness are as
recorded in that file.
