# [[640,16,88]] — 2BGA lifted product on metacyclic Z_40 ×|₁₃ Z_8

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the **weight-9plus × unrestricted** track cell, where the existing LP
codes have low k and the Pareto frontier is thin. The hypothesis was that
metacyclic 2BGA codes (non-abelian, order ~320) with weight-5 supports could
reach k ≈ 16 and d ≈ 88, filling a gap between the weight-8 codes (k ≤ 14,
d ≤ 81) and the high-weight codes (k > 80).

The starting point was the polynomial-domain LP framework of arXiv:2609.01305,
which shows that conjugate-compatible lifting guarantees CSS orthogonality for
any base matrix over F_2[D]/(D^L + 1). The 2BGA construction generalizes this
to non-abelian groups, and metacyclic groups Z_n ×|_r Z_k provide a structured
search space with provable group-theoretic properties.

## What was searched

**Phase 1 — Group parameter sweep.** Enumerated all valid metacyclic parameters
(n, k, r) with n × k ∈ [300, 350] (giving n_code = 2 × order ∈ [600, 700]):
- 8 groups tested: meta(40,8,r), meta(56,6,r), meta(80,4,r), meta(42,8,r),
  meta(49,7,r), meta(32,10,r) with all valid r values.

**Phase 2 — Support search.** For each group, tried 10-30 random support sets
of weight 4 and weight 5 (a, b ⊂ G, |a| = |b| = w). Screened at 500-1000 RIS
trials via gf2_fast. Kept codes with k ≥ 8, d ≥ 30.

**Phase 3 — Weight sweep.** Tested weight = 3, 4, 5. Weight 3 produced no
competitive codes (too few terms). Weight 4 gave the best k/d balance. Weight 5
(k=16, w=10) produced the submitted code.

**Phase 4 — Deep verification.** The top ~20 candidates from screening were
re-verified with `lightest_logical` (5000 trials/side, Python NumPy backend).
Screen distances were typically inflated 2-6×. Only [[640,16,...]] survived
with d ≥ 100.

## Evidence trail

Final claim: **d ≤ 88** (witness-backed upper bound, not exact).

The X witness has weight 88 (found by RIS-fast during CI refutation) and the Z
witness has weight 102 (found by RIS during CI refutation). Both were verified
by the gate. The original claimed d=104 was refuted by these lower-weight
logicals, and d=92 was further refuted by the weight-88 witness.

## Dead ends

- **Cyclic group LP (abelian Z_L):** Systematically swept Z_L for L ∈ [150, 350].
  Best deep-verified result: [[602,8,86]] eff=98 — not competitive. The abelian
  structure limits code diversity.

- **Metacyclic with weight-4 supports:** Many codes screened at d ≥ 90 but
  collapsed to d ≤ 30 under deep verification. The screen-to-true distance
  inflation ratio was 2-6× for metacyclic 2BGA codes.

- **meta(56,6,11) with weight-4:** Screened at d=99 (k=19, eff=277), but deep
  verification found d=24 — a 4× inflation. This was the most dramatic collapse.

- **meta(56,6,19) with weight-4:** Screened at d=106 (k=12), collapsed to d=16
  under 200k total RIS trials. Completely dominated by the screen estimate.
