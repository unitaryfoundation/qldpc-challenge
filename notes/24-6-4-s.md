# [[24,6,4]] — weight-5 Z6-invariant non-CSS stabilizer code

## Direction & hypothesis

Following the group-invariant lazy-SAT search that established `codes/21-7-4.json`, we targeted whether non-CSS stabilizer codes with exact distance `d = 4` can be realized with maximum generator weight strictly bounded by `w <= 5` under a single-layer 2D integer-grid layout (`local-2d-single`, interaction radius `<= 4.0`). We explored cyclic symmetry under `Z6` with 4 qubit blocks (`n = 24`, `18` generators, `k = 6`, rate 1/4, `kd^2/n = 4.0`).

## What was searched

- Shape `(b, c) = (4, 3)` under cyclic group `Z6` (`n = 24`, 18 generators, `k = 6`, check weights `<= 5`).
- Encoding: Incremental CaDiCaL 1.9.5 (via PySAT) enforcing symplectic commutation (`A B^T + B A^T = 0` over GF(2)), symmetry breaking, and upfront distinct nonzero syndrome constraints for all single-qubit Paulis and all weight-2 Pauli pairs modulo `Z6`.
- Lazy cuts: Weight-3 nontrivial logicals were detected via meet-in-the-middle syndrome hashing and cut by group-invariant constraints.
- Convergence: Found in 6.62 s on a single CPU thread. Check weights consist of 12 generators of weight 5 and 6 generators of weight 4 (maximum weight `w = 5`).
- 2D layout: Simulated annealing over integer coordinates on a 5x5 grid placed all 24 qubits into a single physical layer (`layers = 1`) with maximum interaction radius `4.0` (`local-2d-single`).

## Evidence trail

1. **Independent distance verification:**
   - Exhaustive Pauli enumeration across all single-qubit, 2-qubit, and 3-qubit Pauli operators proves 0 nontrivial logicals of weight `<= 3`.
   - Stored logical witness has weight 4: `X7 X21 Z1 Z9` (acting on 4 distinct qubits).
   - SciPy HiGHS MILP across logical sectors independently confirms exact minimum distance `d = 4`.
2. **Refutation gate:**
   - Tested by `verify/validate_candidate.py` through 3,460 randomized information-set (RIS) trials (seed 1793998649) without refutation.
3. **Locality & commutation:**
   - All 18 stabilizer generators pairwise commute under the standard symplectic inner product.
   - Non-CSS structure verified: no local Clifford frame reduces the generator set to pure CSS form.
   - Single-layer 2D layout satisfies `layers = 1` and `interaction_radius = 4.0`.

## Dead ends

- Attempting to compress the blocklength to `n = 20` (`b=4, c=3` under `Z5`) at `w <= 5` timed out without reaching `d >= 4`.
- Tightening interaction radius below `4.0` on a 5x5 single-layer grid for this code resulted in check diameter expansion.

## Tools

- Software: Python 3.12, NumPy, SciPy (HiGHS MILP), PySAT (`cadical195`), and the repository's `verify/qldpc_verify.py` and `verify/validate_candidate.py`.
- Compute: ~6.6 s CPU for SAT synthesis, < 5 s for layout annealing and exact distance verification.

## Reproduction

Index 24 qubits as `6*b + g` (`b in {0, 1, 2, 3}`, `g in Z_6`). The 18 generators in `codes/24-6-4-s.json` are generated by cyclic shifts of 3 orbit representatives across the 6-element cyclic group. Coordinates and full matrices are self-contained in `codes/24-6-4-s.json`.
