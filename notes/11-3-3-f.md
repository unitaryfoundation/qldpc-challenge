# [[11,3,3]] — priced-calculus deletion/peel chain from a 12-qubit CSS parent

## Direction & hypothesis

Weight-6 local-2d board cell; aim at a board-advancing point by deforming a
small high-distance CSS parent with the priced construction calculus (CodeState,
apply_operation, DistanceLedger, planner.plan/plan_chain). Hypothesis: a
depth-2 chain of single-check deletion (C-DELETE-CHECK) and peel (E-PEEL)
moves on a d>=4 parent can land a d>=3 survivor at n=11 that the
depth-1 sweep (fieldnote 2026-10-24-priced-calculus-board-sweep.md) did not
reach, because the second move exploits the changed support of the first.

## What was searched

Parents: CSS entries with n<=12, d>=4 (12-2-4.json and 12-2-4-b.json).
Moves: C-DELETE-CHECK and E-PEEL, chained to depth 2, via planner.plan with
structural screening (bare-qubit screen, Tanner connectivity, domination
pruning). Witness search up to weight 4 per side; gate validation with
verify/validate_candidate.py (refute=True). Two distinct parents produced
this survivor; permutation-equivalent duplicates were collapsed before
submission.

## Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound: d_X <= 3, d_Z <= 3 (witnesses in
codes/11-3-3-f.json). Full distance d = 3 (upper bound). The calculus
propagated the parent interval through C-DELETE-CHECK/E-PEEL and the child
witnesses tightened the upper bound; the gate's refutation search did not find
a lighter logical. Confidence: upper_bound.

## Dead ends

Depth-1 deletion/peel on the same parents mostly yielded d=1 or dominated
points (10-5-1, 10-4-2, 11-7-1 were gate-passing but not board-advancing).
Addition (C-ADD-CHECK), graft (E-GRAFT), Hadamard (E-LOCAL-HADAMARD),
rebase (P-REBASE), and extend (E-EXTEND) found no survivors in the ranges
tried (see fieldnote 2026-10-24-priced-calculus-board-sweep.md).
