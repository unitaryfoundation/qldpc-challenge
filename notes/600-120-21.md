# [[600,120,21]] irregular 4x10 weight-8 pair-partition CPM lift over Z_60

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: CSS, weight-8 x unrestricted, at rate 1/5. Campaign
`research/campaigns/high-rate-witness-gpu-2026-11-15/campaign.json` (issue
#5812) asks for high-rate codes at d >= 7 whose distances are witnessed at
300M trials. The regular pair-partition CPM family of arXiv:2607.14091 already
fills this cell up to d = 18 at n <= 700 (`codes/568-146-18.json` and its
neighbours). The hypothesis was that an irregular template, a 4 x 10 array of
circulant permutation blocks with two blocks per row left empty, so that every
check has weight 8 and qubits have degree 3 or 4 on each side, trades rate for
distance and passes d = 18 at n = 600.

## What was searched

Random lifts of one fixed 4 x 10 weight-8 template at P in {50, 55, 60}, two
sweep seeds per P. Shift tables were drawn from the integer null space of the
template's pair-partition commutation constraints; draws with a 4-cycle in
either Tanner graph or with two equal columns were rejected. 13 instances were
built and screened with the `verify/gf2_fast.cpp` accelerator at 2,000,000
trials (seeds 11 and 12, pair depth 32), keeping d >= 19. The whole campaign
run screened 1,101 fresh constructions: 50 lifts of this template and its
weight-7 variant, and 1,051 non-abelian lifted products. 30 survived the
screen, in 17 distinct (n, k, w), and four were taken to the GPU.

6 fresh-seed rung(s) agree at the best bound, the deepest of them at
300,000,000 trials per side. The submitted witness reproduces it: X: d <= 21
(upper_bound), Z: d <= 21 (upper_bound).

Every rung used pair depth 32. The 300M rung is `verify/ris_gpu.py`, whose
operators are re-verified on CPU. With seeds 9101 to 9104 the reading is
already 21 at 50,000 trials on all four. `verify/gate_changed.py` was also
replayed on this file as a new entry against the board at e289cf5 (seed
1827679645): 11 orbit-fold partitions at 50,000 trials, one 97,000-trial
Python RIS seed and the 8,000,000-trial fast pass found nothing lighter than
21. The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, not an exact distance. The
later fresh-seed rungs, the gate replay and the packaging were done after the
search, in a separate Claude Code session (Claude Fable 5.2).

## Dead ends

- The weight-7 variant of the template gave [[600,120,13]] and [[250,50,9]] at
  the same depth: one less check weight, much lower distance.
- 1,051 non-abelian lifted products (1x2 base shape, check weights 6 and 7)
  gave one GPU-measured survivor, [[540,112,8]], which has the WL signature of
  `codes/540-112-8.json` and was dropped.
- 13 of the 17 distinct screen survivors were not GPU-measured and their
  witnesses were not kept; nothing is claimed for them.
- The campaign's transform stage (code-calculus moves on seeded high-rate
  parents) and blocklengths above 600 were not run.

## Reproduction

H_X and H_Z are 4 x 10 arrays of 60 x 60 circulant permutation blocks. For
each non-empty entry S[i][c], H[60*i + r, 60*c + ((r + S[i][c]) mod 60)] = 1
for r = 0..59; `-` marks an empty block. S_X = [[4, 20, 38, 9, 59, 8, -, -,
42, 51], [42, 12, 53, 28, -, -, 15, 17, 10, 54], [49, 7, -, -, 59, 44, 51, 20,
21, 14], [-, -, 18, 37, 36, 34, 45, 32, 21, 18]]. S_Z = [[29, 32, 50, 25, 24,
24, 35, 4, -, -], [22, 49, 46, 5, 28, 26, -, -, 50, 47], [32, 48, 43, 14, -,
-, 16, 3, 46, 55], [-, -, 26, 10, 44, 56, 3, 5, 43, 36]]. Both matrices have
240 rows of weight 8 and full rank, so k = 600 - 480 = 120. Rebuilding from
these tables reproduces the committed checks row for row.
