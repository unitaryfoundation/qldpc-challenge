## Direction & hypothesis

Target the `local-2d-single / weight-4` and `unrestricted / weight-4`
frontiers at `d = 6` via the even-`d` (`rows = 3, pitch = d - 2`) regime of
`research/multiband_surface.py` (Open Problem 4 of
`fieldnotes/2026-09-01-multiband-dense-packing-method.md`). At `d = 6, rows =
3, pitch = 4`, the closed form `n(6, m) = 66m - 6, k = 2m - 1` beats the `d =
7` two-band scaling `n(7, m) = 74m - 25` whenever `8m > 19` (`m >= 3`).

## What was searched

`research/multiband_surface.py`. At `m = 2` (`[[126,3,6]]`), `66(2) - 6 = 126
> 123`, which is dominated by `codes/123-3-7.json`; for `m >= 3`, every point
is undominated. Selected `m = 6`, giving `[[390,11,6]]` (`kd^2/n = 1.015`, max
check weight 4, single-layer 2D coordinates).

## Dead ends

- At `d = 6, rows = 3, m = 2, pitch = 4`, `[[126,3,6]]` is dominated by
  `codes/123-3-7.json` because `8m > 19` requires `m >= 3`.
- At `d = 6, rows = 3, pitch = 2` or `pitch = 6`, distance collapses below 6.
