## Construction

Schedule: interleaved; two-block interleaved (A: 4 terms, B: 4 terms), 9 CX layers per round; terms by left/right regular terms recorded by the generator; X-check term slots [2, 7, 6, 1, 3, 5, 8, 4], Z-check term slots [6, 1, 2, 7, 4, 3, 0, 5] over terms A_0..A_3, B_0..B_3; RIS screen at 2 rounds: {'Z': 464, 'X': 455}
