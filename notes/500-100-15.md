# [[500,100,15]] — mirror fold (symplectic halving) of a [[1000,200]] pair-partition CPM code

## Direction & hypothesis

Fold a strong X<->Z symmetric CSS code along its mirror to get a genuinely non-CSS stabilizer code with half the qubits and half the logical qubits. Hypothesis: the fold keeps most of the parent's distance (the parent's light logicals rarely contain many mirror pairs), so k d^2/n stays high. Score of this entry: 45.0. Derived, not a new construction.

**Derived from:** an unpublished [[1000,200]] CSS code of ours (irregular 4 x 10 pair-partition CPM template, check weight 7, lift P = 100; the same irregular template A as our board entry codes/990-198-17.json, with a different couple solution; the parent itself is not on the board; lightest logical found 16 per side, upper bound), by @MicheldeMessieres. The parent's check matrices are not needed to use this entry (the generators below are complete); the parent is rebuilt by symplectic doubling H'_X = (A|B), H'_Z = (B|A), which is the parent up to the qubit relabelling tau.
**Fold technique (not ours):** symplectic halving and the CPM halving map of arXiv:2609.30069 (Prop. 4; board fieldnote fieldnotes/2026-09-28-symplectic-doubling-and-halving.md, @MathysRennela); mirror codes of Khesin and Lu (arXiv:2603.05496); the reverse-fold shape of @MinboGaoTHU's codes/485-97-25.json, and his parent-symmetry witness transfer (as in his distance revision of @jacksongquera's codes/480-12-34.json), which our shift-scored search also uses.
**Our contribution:** the parent code, the mirror choice, and the distance search below.

## What was searched

- Mirrors: all maps (l,t) -> (sigma(l), u t + b_l) sending X-check rows to Z-check rows; exactly one fixed-point-free involution exists (u = -1), up to conjugation by the cyclic shift.
- Distance: fold logicals correspond to parent logicals v (in ker H_X, outside row H_Z) with Pauli weight |v union tau(v)|/2, so every parent logical found was scored under all 100 cyclic shifts. Information sets were drawn pair by pair (a Y enters or leaves as one unit).
- W7_Qb412P100_0 (parent Stern, pair-aware, shift-scored): 300 s, 23023 iterations, lightest 15
- D7_Qb412P100_0 (parent Stern, pair-aware, shift-scored): 3600 s, 279268 iterations, lightest 15
- N_W500_pisd (Pauli Stern on the fold): 1500 s, 17018 iterations, lightest 15

Claim: d <= 15, witness-backed upper bound. Calibration of the same tool on codes/485-97-25.json (parent rebuilt by doubling): 10 min of search read 28 against the published 25, so these readings are upper bounds of limited power.

## Dead ends

- A Stern search directly on the fold, without the parent's shift symmetry, is several times weaker at this size (33 after 5 min on codes/485-97-25.json).
- Pure-type logicals (all X, all Z or all Y) are far heavier than d on these folds.

## Reproduction

The generators in this entry are exactly the folded parent X-checks in row order (construction field). Doubling them gives the parent back.
