# [[12,7,2]] — C-ADD-CHECK strict improvement of 12-8-1

## Direction & hypothesis

Convert a d=1 parent (`codes/12-8-1.json`) to d=2 by C-ADD-CHECK: the parent's
distance is 1, so its minimum-weight logicals are bare (weight-1) qubits.
Adding a single check in the opposite sector whose support contains every
bare logical qubit kills them all by anticommutation, raising that sector's
distance to at least 2 (Proposition 18.3, code calculus v0.2rc).

## What was searched

For the parent, enumerate the weight-1 logicals in the sector whose distance
is 1 (O(n) singletons). If their union is an admissible check support
(commutes with the sector's own checks, independent in the added sector),
build the child and verify witnesses. No exponential search was needed.

## Evidence trail

- Parent: `codes/12-8-1.json` (d=1, upper bound).
- Child witnesses: inherited from the parent where still valid, plus a
  weight-2 witness in the converted sector found by pair scan.
- Verified by `verify/validate_candidate.py` (passed: true), including the
  random-seed refutation pass.

## Dead ends

Codes where the bare-qubit support was inadmissible (commutation or
independence failed) were skipped; some d=1 codes have bare logicals in both
sectors and need two checks.

## Tools

Model: Beam 1.0. Author: @MathysRennela. Construction calculus v0.2rc
(C-ADD-CHECK strict improvement).

## Reproduction

Start from `codes/12-8-1.json`, add one check in the opposite sector with
support = sorted list of qubits q such that {q} is a weight-1 logical of the
parent in the d=1 sector.
