# [[672,28,24]]: weight-6 two-block group algebra code over C28 x Dic3

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: check weight 6, unrestricted locality (no layout submitted). Two-block group algebra (2BGA) codes C(G;a,b) (Lin and Pryadko, arXiv:2306.16400) over non-abelian groups with a large centre hold many points of this cell between n = 480 and n = 960, among them 480-16-26, 672-20-32 and 960-24-38. Hypothesis: on groups whose group algebra F_2[G] can carry k >= 16 (selected by its representation theory: an abelian normal subgroup of index 2 with central 2-part, and character orbits of large capacity), random pairs (a, b) of check weight 6 (types 3+3 and 2+4; 3+5 and 4+4 for weight 8) screened by information-set decoding (ISD) give certifiable codes beyond the board's frontier at n <= 1000. The target of the search, a certified kd^2/n >= 30, was not reached (see Dead ends); this code (kd^2/n = 24.0) is the best certified new code of the search that no board entry dominates or ties.

## What was searched

One search job (seed 20261110): pairs (a, b) with 1 in a and b, of types 3+3, 3+5, 4+4 and 2+4, on 30 non-abelian groups of order 60 to 480 (20 groups of a census ranking, the groups of five board entries, among them `C12 x|_5 C28` of 672-20-32, and five groups of a norm design). Per pair: k by GF(2) ranks after a pre-filter through central quotients (k(G) <= |K| k(G/K) for a central 2-subgroup K); kept if k >= 16 and the Tanner graph is connected; deduplicated by a canonical key under Aut(G), two-sided translations and the X/Z exchange. 10778 codes with distinct keys were screened by chained ISD (5e4 iterations per side, plus words invariant under central subgroups of order <= 4), 107 refined with 1e6 iterations per side, 27 with 8e6, and six finalists with d <= 30 were certified by two exact programs. On `C12 x|_5 C28` at type 3+3: 154000 draws, 240 accepted pairs, 199 codes with distinct keys; this is the only one with k >= 28 and an upper bound above 20. About 32 CPU-hours in all (screening 19.2, design files 1.7, refinement 10.2, exact searches 0.8).

## Evidence trail

- Construction: provenance.construction. rank H_X = rank H_Z = 322, k = 28 = 14 + 14, the dimensions of R/(aR + Rb) and R/(Ra + bR) for R = F_2[G]; check weight 6, every qubit in 3 X-checks and 3 Z-checks, Tanner graph connected, Tanner girth 8. Rebuilt independently twice (Python from the product rule, Magma from the presentation), equal to the entry row for row; GAP identifies the presentation as SmallGroup(336,81), isomorphic to C28 x Dic3.
- Screening ladder (upper bounds): 5e4 ISD iterations per side gave 24 on both sides (10 and 12 distinct words); 1e6 per side gave 24 again (44 and 43 distinct words of weight 24) and nothing lighter; words invariant under central subgroups of order 2 to 8 had weight 24 to 48.
- Lower bound: every column of H_X and H_Z has weight 3, so the all-one vector lies in both row spaces and every logical has even weight. Two independently written exhaustive programs, exactA and exactB, searched W = 23 (weights <= 22), anchored at the four qubit orbits of the Tanner-graph automorphism group (order 168, SmallGroup(168,32), semiregular, two orbits per block; Magma, confirmed with nauty), earlier orbits excluded. Neither found a nontrivial logical on either side: Z side 7345978 and 7507311 nodes in 15.3 and 17.4 s, X side 7199307 and 7370526 nodes in 14.9 and 17.0 s (exactA and exactB, one thread). A rerun of both programs on a second machine reproduced every node count.
- Third program: dist-m4ri 0.16.1 (github.com/QEC-pages/dist-m4ri @ 4cc9c12, connected-cluster search, one thread). With the same anchors it gives d_Z = d_X = 24 (279 s and 94 s). Without any symmetry (all start columns) it finds no nontrivial logical of weight <= 22 on either side (weights up to 20: 184 s and 199 s; weight 22: 1105 s and 1207 s), so the conclusion does not rest on the automorphism group.
- Upper bound: the witnesses in the entry, weight 24 on each side; dist-m4ri returned another word of weight 24 on each side.
- Final claim: d_X = d_Z = 24 exactly; certs/672-28-24.json records it at level solver.
- Board placement at commit 82a52ee51 (our reimplementation of the site's cell and Pareto rules): on the frontier of the unrestricted cells of weight 6, 8 and any, with no entry dominating or equal to it. The largest d of a CSS entry with n <= 672, k >= 28 and check weight at most 6 is 20 (576-32-20); every CSS entry with n <= 672, k >= 28 and d >= 24 has check weight at least 7 (e.g. 630-28-26). It dominates 30 entries, 25 of them in the weight-6 cell. Four have no layout: 682-20-22 (on n, k and d), 708-24-24 and 720-24-24 (on n, k and w), 826-28-20 (on n, d and w); the other 26 carry layouts of the local-2d classes and keep their places in those cells. Among certified entries it dominates 697-25-10.
- Related work and novelty: the construction is the 2BGA code of Lin and Pryadko (arXiv:2306.16400, Phys. Rev. A 109, 022407 (2024)), and the group `C12 x|_5 C28` is that of the board entry 672-20-32 (by vprusso). Its group C28 x Dic3 (isomorphic to `Z84 x|_29 Z4`) is also the host group of Qian and Li, Multi-agent discovery of practical quantum LDPC codes, arXiv:2608.08996, who build coset codes over it, among them [[336,12,20]] (w = 6, A = e + xs + x^3, B = s^3 + x^2 + x^4, K = <x^42>). Our code is not the double cover (K trivial) of their [[336,12,20]]: the two-block code of their A and B over the whole group has k = 12, with A acting from the left or from the right (its quotient by K has k = 12, as they report), and a double cover of a two-block code with k = 12 has k <= 24 (Lemma 8 of the package README); the quotients of our code by the three central subgroups of order 2 have k = 20 (computed, GF(2) ranks; `pair-partition-and-two-block-codes/672-28-24/related/` in the repository cited below). A targeted literature search on 2026-11-11 found no published code with the parameters [[672,28,24]]; the only board entry with n = 672 and k = 28 is the stabilizer code 672-28-30, a different code (it has stabilizer elements of Pauli weight 4, this code none below 6).

## Dead ends

- The same group gave two inequivalent [[672,24,26]] codes with exact d = 26 (two programs; Tanner groups C4 x C28 with three qubit orbits per block). They tie with the board entry 672-24-26, a different code (its Tanner graph is isomorphic to neither), and would be dominated by our 600-24-26 once #5271 is merged, so they are not submitted.
- The certified target kd^2/n >= 30 was not reached: the best certified new codes are [[672,24,26]] (24.14) and this one (24.00). The weight-8 lead [[504,28,<=31]] and the weight-6 leads [[600,16,<=32]] are uncertified ISD bounds with few hits.
- At n = 960 the ISD bounds did not converge: the board's 960-24-38 reached 38 on one side only, once in 8e6 iterations per side, and 40 on the other.
- Census groups SmallGroup(160,9) and (400,97) gave no pair with k >= 16 at the odd types, and on (112,8) and (224,8) every 3+3 pair with k >= 16 had a disconnected Tanner graph.
- Choosing the pairs by the norms of a and b in F_2[G] (all of k in one block of the group algebra) did not raise d at equal k: on C24 x Dic5 at k = 16, 1.7 % of the designed codes and 0.9 % of the random ones reached the largest bound.
- A [[960,20,24]] code over SmallGroup(480,80) (type 2+4) is exact but dominated.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code); Python (standard library) for the driver, the group tables and the checks; C programs for k, for the ISD and for the two exact searches exactA and exactB (github.com/TimoKellerMath/quantum-error-correction @ aaa18674c9a1, `two-block-and-amplified-codes/src/`, with their completeness proofs in exactA_NOTES.md and exactB_NOTES.md there); Magma for the Tanner-graph automorphism group, nauty and GAP in the checks; dist-m4ri 0.16.1 as third program. Compute: about 32 CPU-hours for the search, with 32 workers on our compute server; about 1 CPU-minute for the exact searches of this code; about 55 CPU-minutes for the independent checks, mostly dist-m4ri without symmetry.

## Reproduction

In `pair-partition-and-two-block-codes/` of github.com/TimoKellerMath/quantum-error-correction @ 2a15e549f6f4: `python3 src/rebuild.py` rebuilds H_X and H_Z from the group law and a, b and checks the matrices, k, the witnesses and the records; the subdirectory `672-28-24/` holds the definition, the matrices, the witnesses, the logs and JSON records of both exact searches (`records/`) and the dist-m4ri runs (`third_tools/`). By hand: G = <x, y | x^12 = y^28 = 1, y x y^-1 = x^5>, element x^i y^j has index 168 (j mod 2) + 14 i + floor(j/2), a = 1 + x^2 y^9 + x^10 y^11, b = 1 + x^10 y^22 + x^7 y^27, and H_X, H_Z as in provenance.construction. exactA and exactB, run with W = 23 and generators of the Tanner-graph automorphism group, rerun the lower bound in about 16 s per side and program; dist-m4ri needs no group (21.5 and 23.4 CPU-minutes for sides Z and X).
