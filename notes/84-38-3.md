# [[84,38,3]] [[84,38,3]], a self-dual PSL(2,7) {7,7} hyperbolic surface code, opens the high-rate corner of the weight-8/unrestricted cell: 38 logical qubits on 84 physical at check weight 7, a rate of 0.45 that no weight-8 entry on the board approaches at this blocklength.

## Direction & hypothesis

weight-8 x unrestricted, family `topological`. Chosen because the paper's
headline instances are all far over the n<=700/1000 blocklength cap, leaving
only its small-q members admissible, and because high-rate
low-weight-per-qubit entries are thin in this cell: the existing frontier tops
out around kd2/n 123 but at k<=230 with n>=700, so nothing occupies small n at
high k.

## What was searched

The construction is algebraic, so the search is over generator classes rather
than over code families: for each (p, q) the triangle-group relations
ord(x)=ord(y)=p and ord(xy)=2 with y=JxJ^-1 for a projective involution J in
PGL(2,q) admit many (x, y) pairs, deduplicated on the check-support signature.
For p=7, q=7 that enumeration yields 84 distinct classes, all giving n=84,
k=38; every one measured d=3 on both sides. n=84 and k=38 match the paper's
Table 2 exactly. Also swept p=5 q=5 (30 distinct classes, all [[30,8,3]],
already on the board as codes/30-8-3.json and therefore not filed).

## Dead ends

- The paper's headline codes -- [[51330,17112,10]], [[51330,10268,12]],
  [[17220,7382,8]], [[25944,12974,8]], [[7440,1490,10]] -- are 1.7x to 51x
  over the board's blocklength cap (n<=700, extended to 1000 only for w<=8 and
  d<=40) and cannot be filed at any confidence tier. This is the whole reason
  the admissible slice of the paper is small.
- p=5 q=5 gives [[30,8,3]], already on the board from a qecdb reconstruction;
  re-filing it would be a duplicate, so it was dropped.
- The paper's modular-compiler half (Secs. IV-VI: module partitions, R/K
  routing metrics, Stim/PyMatching thresholds) is not rankable under TRACKS.md
  -- routing cost and gate scheduling are explicitly out of scope, and a
  module assignment earns at most the Layer-3 `modular` flag. Roughly 60% of
  the paper yields nothing for the board.

--class 0 writes HX/HZ as .npy. The script recomputes the matrices from the

data-availability statement offers its matrices on request only. Verified that
this script regenerates the submitted HX/HZ row for row.
