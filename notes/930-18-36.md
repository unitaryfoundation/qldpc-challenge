# [[930,18,36]] — weight-6 cyclic GB code over Z_465 (trinomial pair, gcd of degree 9)

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6, in the n > 700 tier where the verifier admits a
claim only with max check weight <= 8 and d <= 40. Above n = 700 the weight-6
column is thin and mostly inherited from below: for k = 17..20 the best
entries were [[672,20,32]], [[630,20,24]] and [[682,20,22]], so a weight-6
code with k = 18 at n <= 930 advances once d >= 33. A trinomial has a gcd with
x^m - 1 of degree 9 only when m has several small cyclotomic orders; 465 = 3 x
5 x 31 gives irreducible factors of degree 4 (from the fifth roots of unity)
and 5 (from the 31st), and the hypothesis was that a trinomial pair sharing
one factor of each has its lightest logicals above 32 at m = 465.

## What was searched

Exhaustive weight-(3,3) cyclic GB enumeration over m = 361..500 with designed
k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 18..80: every trinomial 1 + x^i + x^j with gcd
degree >= 9, pairs canonicalized under units of Z_m and cyclic shifts, pairs
whose exponents share a factor with m dropped (direct sums). 3,040 pairs in
band over 140 m, subsampled to 2,026 (at most 400 per m); a companion run over
m = 351..360 with k >= 12 gave 1,500 pairs. Screening used gf2_fast RIS
through research/kit/surrogate.py at pair depth 8: 1,000 trials on all 3,526
pairs, 20,000 on the 282 k = 18 survivors, 300,000 on the best 16 at m = 465,
then fresh-seed ladders on the leaders.

2 fresh-seed rung(s) agree at the best bound, the deepest of them at

(upper_bound), Z: d <= 40 (upper_bound).

The claim descended with depth: the screens read 60, 46 and 40, the 2,000,000-
and 10,000,000-trial rungs held at 38, and the first 20,000,000-trial rung
found a weight-36 X logical. The document was re-staged at d = 36 with that
witness. Of the 20,000,000-trial rungs that followed on fresh seeds, all but
one stopped at exactly 36 (on both sides) and one stopped at 38; nothing below
36 was found. `qldpc submit`'s own 10,000,000-trial pass does not reach 36
reliably, which is why this entry was packaged with research/kit/promote.py
from the validated witness. The bar for this cell is d >= 33, so the margin is
3, and the descent from 40 to 36 over two decades of trials says a deeper
sweep may lower it further.

## Dead ends

- k = 18 at m = 381 and 438: best 38 and 28 at 20,000 trials; two m = 381
  pairs (a = [0, 1, 14] with b = [0, 88, 362] and b = [0, 19, 107]) reached d
  <= 34 at 300,000 trials (bar 33) and were not deepened.
- Three more m = 465 pairs at d <= 40 after 300,000 trials (a = [0, 1, 12]
  with b = [0, 110, 190], [0, 80, 355], [0, 221, 389]) fell to 36..38 by
  2,000,000 to 10,000,000 trials; the pair [0, 1, 19] / [0, 97, 324] screened
  at d <= 42, which the n > 700 cap (d <= 40) makes inadmissible as stated.
- k >= 20 trinomial cells (m = 372, 420, 434, 438, 465): every pair at d <= 24
  after 1,000 trials; the k = 28 pairs at m = 381 have d <= 2.
- m = 351..360 with k >= 12: 1,500 pairs; the k = 12 cell topped out at d <=
  40 after 20,000 trials, not pursued.
