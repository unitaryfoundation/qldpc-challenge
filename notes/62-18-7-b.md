# [[62,18,7]] — code-calculus v0.1 E-PEEL of a CSS parent

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted a Pareto opening in the unrestricted / weight-8 cell via the priced
construction calculus (`unitaryfoundation/code-calculus` v0.1): E-PEEL a qubit
(or check) of a CSS parent, recomputing ranks, connectivity, and max check
weight. The child is board-advancing when its (n,k,d,w) is nondominated in
its cell.

## What was searched

Single-step E-PEEL moves over CSS parents, screened structurally (no bare
qubit, connected Tanner graph, independent displayed rows) and by an
optimistic 4-axis domination test against the board before any witness
search. Survivors were packaged with `research/kit/submit.py`
(`make_submission`, `save_submission`) and validated with
`research/kit/coordination.py gate` (`passed: true`).

## Evidence trail

Distance is a witness-backed upper bound from a 300-trial RIS screen per side
(confidence `upper_bound`); no lower bound is claimed. Move: E-PEEL of parent
`63-18-7`.

## Dead ends
