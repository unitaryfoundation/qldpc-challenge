# [[11,3,3]] — E-GRAFT reduction of [[12,3,3]]

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the `weight-6 × local-2d-single` cell: remove one qubit from the
board's `[[12,3,3]]` entry via the `E-GRAFT` rule of the priced construction
calculus, preserving `k` and `d` while lowering `n` (and hence raising `kd²/n`).

## What was searched

Single-qubit grafts (`E-GRAFT`) on parents up to `n≤60`, trying every qubit and
every spliced sector, with all permutations of the chain order for rows touching
the grafted qubit. Parents' witnesses were reused when they did not contain the
grafted qubit; candidates were screened with `verify/validate_candidate.py`
(refute=False) and then the full gate.

## Evidence trail

- Parent: `codes/12-3-3.json` (n=12, k=3, d=3).
- Graft removes 1 qubit → n=11, k=3, d=3.
- Distance is a witness-backed upper bound (both sides), refuted at 200k+ RIS
  trials with no lighter logical found.
- `verify/validate_candidate.py` passes: `board_advancing: true`, no duplicate
  or WL-equivalent entry.

## Dead ends

Grafts on parents where the grafted qubit appeared in a distance witness were
invalid (witness destroyed). Most permutations failed the coverage/CSS checks.
Chains of two grafts collapsed `k` or failed connectivity.

## Tools

Model: Beam 1.0. Agent harness: OpenCode. Calculus: E-GRAFT (local staging tooling, not committed). Board tooling: `verify/validate_candidate.py`, `research/kit/coordination.py`.

## Reproduction

Apply `E-GRAFT` with sector `X`, grafted qubit `2`, ordered row ids as in
`codes/12-3-3.json`, using the E-GRAFT operation. Export via
`export_challenge_entry` with the surviving witnesses.
