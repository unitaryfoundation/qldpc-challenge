# [[682,62,98]] weight-24 cyclic generalized-bicycle from a designed divisor

## Direction & hypothesis

Nothing on the board at n <= 682 reaches k >= 62 with d >= 98 at any row weight, so the opening was distance at moderate dimension. A residue construction cannot aim there: it fixes k between 20 and 34 whatever the ring. Since k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), choosing a divisor of x^m - 1 and drawing a and b from its ideal selects the dimension instead, and here a degree-31 divisor gives k = 62.

Two facts govern the construction, both checked rather than assumed. x^m - 1 is squarefree over GF(2) only for odd m, which makes m = 341 usable and the even moduli of a residue sweep unusable. And the generators are low-weight codewords of the cyclic code the divisor generates, found by information-set decoding inside that ideal.

## What was searched

The divisor is a product of cyclotomic-coset minimal polynomials over GF(2^10), so its degree is chosen; sampling sparse polynomials and taking gcds with x^m - 1 lands on a target degree almost never and kept 0 of 12 draws in an earlier attempt. Sweeping deg g / m from 0.04 to 0.30 over many coset subsets at nine odd moduli produced 299 priced candidates, 245 undominated after folding, 137 distinct shapes. This one sits at ratio 0.09.

## Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, with a weight-98 X witness and a weight-101 Z witness, so the distance is the minimum of the two.

    random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6      98   (median 102, maximum 104)
    research/foldbound.py, uncapped            121   (2 blocks, t = 31, orbit 11)
    verify/validate_candidate.py               passed, not refuted

The fold sits above the claim rather than below it, so it clears the code without being the binding bound: the trial-based search is what establishes 98 here. Both witnesses were re-verified against the committed matrices, in the kernel of the opposite checks and outside the row space of their own side. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature.

## Dead ends

Row weight is this construction's price and the schema is its ceiling. `schema/code.schema.json` caps each check's support at 32 entries, so a heavier code cannot be submitted whatever it scores. Two candidates from this sweep passed full 24-seed gates at weights 48 and 58, one scoring kd^2/n = 1493.1, and neither is submittable. Rebuilding the heavier one under the cap is impossible: the lightest codewords in its degree-101 divisor's ideal are weight 26 to 34, so any pair of generators sums to at least 52. A Gilbert-Varshamov estimate suggesting weight 16 was reachable is simply wrong for a cyclic ideal, which is structured rather than random.

Every distance in this family falls as search depth rises, so triage figures are not findings. This code was screened at 108 by a 20,000-trial reading and gated at 98; a sibling went 86, then 82, then 74 across three budgets; and one candidate's score fell from 571.4 to 115.7 once folded.
