# [[560,20,56]] weight-14 cyclic generalized-bicycle

## Hypothesis

Cyclic generalized-bicycle codes cover a wide blocklength range, and the
board's coverage of that range is uneven. Bucketing every CSS entry by
blocklength and taking the best kd^2/n in each bucket shows n in [550, 599]
topping out at 163.0 while n in [600, 649] reaches 617.1, and n = 560 itself
held only four entries with a best of 87.5. The family can reach that band:
the ring size has to be divisible by 5 and 7 for the residue construction
that fixes k, which admits m = 280, and the quotient-norm lift at q = 7 caps
the distance at 2m/q = 80. A sweep aimed there should beat the band.

## Construction

    ring   x^280 - 1
    a(x) = x^85 + x^128 + x^179 + x^199 + x^229 + x^266 + x^272
    b(x) = x^45 + x^77 + x^142 + x^232 + x^244 + x^256 + x^278
    H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)]
    H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T]

n = 2m, and k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), computed by Euclid before any matrix
work so that most draws are discarded for free.

## What was swept

The ring is fixed at m = 280 and the sweep runs over the sparse polynomial
pairs. Each draw samples a weight split and the two polynomials, computes k by
Euclid so most draws are discarded before any matrix work, and runs a cheap
60k-trial distance screen. A draw survives only if the resulting (n, k, d, w)
is undominated on the live board snapshot. Surviving draws go through an
escalating ladder of 400k, 2M and 8M random information-set trials, with the
board comparison repeated at each rung so a candidate that collapses is
dropped as soon as it stops being interesting. 3617 draws produced 80 that
were undominated at the screened distance and 5 that were Pareto-distinct.

## The single-block check

General RIS over all 2m columns is close to blind to a logical supported
entirely on one block. Such a vector u satisfies a(x) u(x) = 0 mod x^m - 1,
which puts u in the ideal generated by h = (x^m - 1) / gcd(a, x^m - 1), so it
can be searched directly in a space of dimension deg h rather than 2m. This
matters: a previous submission of mine survived 150M general RIS trials at
weight 74 and was then refuted by a weight-64 logical sitting entirely in one
block, which the ideal search finds in seconds. Every candidate here is checked
that way before the distance is believed, and any candidate whose single-block
bound comes in below the ladder is dropped rather than submitted.

## Distance ladder

Witness search runs on each side separately, so a run that happens to find one
side cannot leave the other without a witness. Both witnesses are re-checked
directly against H: in the kernel of the opposite matrix, and outside the row
space of its own.

## What it advances

Score kd^2/n = 146.286 at check weight 14, against 163.0 for the best entry
anywhere in n in [550, 599] and 87.5 for the best at n = 560, both at
submission time. Undominated in its cell.

## Boundary

The distance is an upper bound from witness search, not a proof. Literature
novelty is unverified: cyclic GB codes are a classical family and these
parameters may appear in the 2BGA literature.
