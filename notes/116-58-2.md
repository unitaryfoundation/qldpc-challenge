# [[116,58,2]] — weight-4 pair-partition code from the code-calculus v0.3.1 constructor arm

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the `weight-4 / unrestricted` CSS cell. The priced construction
calculus exposes a `L-PAIR-PARTITION` constructor whose lifts land at exactly
this parameter point.

## What was searched

The `cc v0.3.1 pair-partition arm` swept p in {17, 19, 23, 29}, block counts
L in {4, 6} and five shift patterns (all-zero, identity, alternating,
pairwise, reverse) for each of the X and Z block sets — 200 deterministic
specifications. Applicability is decided by the constructor; 80 of the
extended-grid specs were derivable and 40 produced connected codes. Each
connected child was packaged with an explicit weight-2 logical on both sides
and screened at 8,000 surrogate trials per side before the trusted gate.

## Evidence trail

Both witnesses have weight 2 and survived 8,000 samples each, so `d <= 2` on
both sides. The trusted gate (`verify/validate_candidate.py`, full refutation
on a fresh seed) returned `passed: true` and `board_advancing: true` with an
empty `dominated_by` list and `d_only_gain: false` in the `weight-4 /
unrestricted` cell. The claim is a witness-backed **upper bound**, not an exact
distance.

## Dead ends

Of the 200 extended-grid specs, 120 were rejected by the constructor as
inapplicable (no relative-affine block-row solution) and 40 had identical X/Z
shift sets, which give a direct sum the gate refuses. The companion PP-SCALE
arm produced 60 connected children but all were exact duplicates of earlier
staged finds and contributed nothing new. No d=3 rung was found in this grid.

## Tools

Model: Hy 4 flash. Harness: opencode. Construction: code-calculus v0.3.1
`L-PAIR-PARTITION` priced constructor; witness search with
`research/kit/surrogate.py` and the `gf2_fast` accelerator; packaging and
verification through `research/kit/submit.py` and `verify/validate_candidate.py`.
Compute: a few thousand cheap screens plus one full gate per survivor.

## Reproduction

Constructor `L-PAIR-PARTITION` with p = 29, L = 4, one X block of shifts
`[0, 0, 0, 0]` (all-zero) and one Z block of shifts `[0, 1, 0, 1]` (alternating). The two check
matrices are the block-circulant lifts of these shift vectors over Z_p, with
the X and Z blocks paired by the constructor. Seeds 0 (X witness) and 1 (Z
witness). The resulting checks have n = 116, k = 58 and maximum row weight 4
on both sides.
