## Direction & hypothesis

Advance the unrestricted weight-9plus cell's frontier at n=684 on the
k axis with two-block group-algebra codes over the affine group
Aff(F_19): our merged [[684,18,106]] (kd^2/n 295.7) holds the cell
leader, and the k=16 corner had no witnessed point between the old
k=14 anchor (now dominated by this document) and the k=18 leader. The
hypothesis: the affine-2BGA family carries a k=16 interior point that
dominates the k=14 anchor outright (both k and d strictly higher at
the same n), giving the family two adjacent frontier points.
