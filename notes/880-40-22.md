# [[880,40,22]]: [[4,2,2]] amplification of 180-20-11

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: check weight 8, so the weight-8 class, unrestricted locality (no layout submitted), CSS board. One step of the distance amplification of Liang, Gu, Chen, Eisert and Wang (arXiv:2609.37231) with the [[N,N-2,2]] amplifier turns a CSS code [[n,k,d]] whose retained checks are linearly independent into a [[(N+1)n - k, (N-2)k, 2d]] code, at check weight max(w_X + 1, w_Z + 1, N + q_Z, N + q_X) with q the largest column weights. The distance doubles exactly, for X and Z separately (Lemma 4 there), so every board base with a certified exact distance gives an amplified code with a proved exact distance. Hypothesis: some of these are new points of the frontier, in particular where the board's amplified entries keep dependent base rows (which only lengthen the code) or use bases without a certificate. Here the base is codes/180-20-11.json ([[180,20,11]] two-block code over C15 x S3, board entry of G. Lee), certified exact in certs/180-20-11.json.

## What was searched

All CSS entries of board commit 8042f9da with an exact certificate were amplified on paper with the [[N,N-2,2]] amplifiers for N = 4, 6, 8, 10 (935, 784, 664 and 567 bases with (N+1)n - k <= 1000). Of the admissible candidates with check weight at most 12, 89 are not dominated by a CSS entry in (n, k, d, w) and 57 of these also not by each other; most have d = 4 (high-rate bases of distance 2). The eight with d >= 12 were built and verified, with linearly independent base rows spanning the row spaces chosen to minimize the check weight (randomized greedy; a counting bound shows that no choice of rows gives a lower weight). This code is one of them.

## Evidence trail

- Construction: Definition 1 of the README of the package cited below, with the base rows listed in provenance.construction left out (80 of 90 X-checks and 80 of 90 Z-checks kept); n = 880 = 5 x 180 - 20, k = 40 by GF(2) ranks, check weight 8, qubit degree at most 11 (7 X-checks, 7 Z-checks).
- Distance (proved): d_X = 2 d_X(base) = 22 and d_Z = 2 d_Z(base) = 22 by Lemma 4 of arXiv:2609.37231 (written out in the coordinates of this entry as Proposition 4 of the README), since d_X(base) = d_Z(base) = 11 is certified in certs/180-20-11.json (tropidist b03ec2d4077f, level solver). Witnesses: u (x) (e_0 + e_1) for the witnesses u of codes/180-20-11.json.
- Consistency check (computed, heuristic): an information-set search with 1.1e5 trials per side found nothing lighter than 22; the 450 and 443 distinct words of weight 22 it found (X, Z) all have the form l (x) (e_a + e_b) with l a weight-11 logical of the base, as the proof predicts (Lemma 5 of the README; with the 90 such logicals of the base found by a saturated search there are 6 x 90 = 540 of them per side).
- Board placement (computed at 8042f9da): no CSS entry dominates or equals it in (n, k, d, w); it dominates no entry, so it is a new point of the frontier.
- Final claim: d = 22 exactly, by proof. Filed as upper_bound with the witnesses, since the board earns the exact tier only through certs/ and its certifiers do not reach d = 22 at k = 40.

## Dead ends

- Most amplifications of certified bases are on the board already or dominated: by our audit, 70 of the 80 amplified entries of 8042f9da are amplifications of certified bases (so their distances are exact by the same lemma), and for example [[90,8,10]] and [[108,8,10]] give [[442,16,20]] and [[532,16,20]] at check weight 7, dominated by the board's [[252,16,d<=20]].
- The [[4,2,2]] step costs more than one unit of check weight when a column weight of the base exceeds w - 3: [[48,4,7]] and [[60,4,8]] (w = 5, column weight 3) give [[236,8,14]] and [[296,8,16]] at check weight 7, dominated by [[140,8,14]] and [[180,12,d<=16]].

## Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code); Python (standard library) for the construction, ranks and checks; an information-set search in C; tropidist (Cheng, Guo, Pan, arXiv:2611.08247) at the board's pinned commit b03ec2d4077f for the direct runs.
