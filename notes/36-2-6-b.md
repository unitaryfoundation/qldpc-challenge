# [[36,2,6]] — quantum Tanner code over PSL(2,5)

## Direction & hypothesis

Aimed at the weight-4 unrestricted CSS cell with a small quantum Tanner code.
PSL(2,5) with a length-2 repetition seed code yields n=36, d=6 at weight 4.

## What was searched

Random quantum Tanner codes over PSL(2,5) with RepetitionCode(2), seeds 0..11,
bipartite and non-bipartite variants, screened at 300 trials per side and
re-screened at 10,000 trials per side.

## Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d<=6 on both X and Z sides. Full gate refutation
passed on a fresh seed.

## Dead ends

Rep-3 instances over the same group landed at w=6 with d around 3-6 and did
not advance their cell. Non-bipartite variants did not improve d.

## Tools

qLDPC library (QTCode.random), challenge research kit, gf2_fast. Model: Beam 1.0.

## Reproduction

Group PSL(2,5), seed code repetition length 2, random seed 9, bipartite False.
