# [[854,158,2]] — one Z-check deletion from 854-157-3.json (C-DELETE-CHECK b237)

## Direction & hypothesis

A high-k board region at 854 <= n reachable by deleting one check from 854-157-3.json at
little distance cost. Deletion raises k by one at fixed n and fixed qubit set, so any landing
point where the frontier has headroom on k is reachable in one move. The aim was the largest
d the deletion could keep while still clearing the cell's floor -- here 2, against a parent
claim of 3.

## What was searched

All 163 CSS board entries with n > 700, not a sample of them: 88,592 essential single check
deletions (rows whose removal changes the stabilizer group; dependent rows are dropped, since
removing one yields an identical code). 28,913 of those -- 33% -- free a bare qubit and are
d=1 by construction; a structural filter on column weights discarded them before any witness
search, leaving 59,679 clean deletions across 109 distinct (n, k+1, d, w) tuples.

Witnesses were then re-measured per candidate rather than inherited: 8000 RIS trials per side,
seed 0, via the challenge's own `heuristic_distance` search. At most 3 clean rows were sampled
per tuple, so this is a floor on what the pool contains, not an exhaustive sweep.

## Evidence trail

- Parent `854-157-3.json` claims d = 3.
- Unpriced `C-DELETE-CHECK` carry from the parent (no child witnesses supplied):
  interval [0, 3]. Contains the measured child distance.
- Priced transport, `d' = min(d, w_del, w_exp)`: **[0, 2]** with
  w_del = 2, w_exp = 5 (RIS upper bounds, 20,000 trials, seed 0; the
  true minima are NP-hard, Theorem 24.1, so both are over-estimates).
- Seed-0 estimate over the child: 2 per side, 8000 trials/side.
- Gate at seed 5: passed, not refuted, no duplicate, board-advancing on no axis in particular.
- Advancement: nothing dominates it in its cell.

## Dead ends

- Distance does not survive deletion. The parent's claim was never carried forward: on 14 of
  the 18 filed entries the child measured strictly below the parent, so inheriting the parent's
  number would have been an over-claim that the refutation gate catches.
- 28,913 of 88,592 essential deletions free a bare qubit and land at d=1; only 4 of the 18
  filed entries are of that kind, the rest being shadowed by d=2 siblings from the same parent.
- 22 candidates in the working set passed the gate but were dominated by a batch-mate at the
  same (n, k, w), so 40 were reduced to 18 before filing.
- Not attempted at this scale: `C-ADD-CHECK` strict improvement and `E-GRAFT`. The 2026-10-19
  fieldnote reports 0 strict improvements out of 736 valid additions for n <= 10 and no graft
  survivors for n <= 8, so neither was worth a sweep at n = 700-1000 without a new idea.
- `C-DIRECT-SUM` is unusable against the connectivity gate regardless of scale.

## Tools

Models: Beam 1.0, MiMo-V2.6-Flash (matches `provenance.model`).

- Structural work: the challenge's own `gf2` and `verify/heuristic_distance.py`, plus an
  independent nullspace test for row essentiality.
- Verification: `verify/qldpc_verify.py` and `verify/check_codes_format.py`, unchanged, both
  passing locally on the committed file.
- Interval transport: the priced construction calculus at github.com/MathysRennela/code-calculus-dev @ 661d1e0, `machine/priced_calculus.py`. Its `CodeState.delete_check`
  reproduces this entry's child state exactly from the parent, and its `Independent(...)` premise
  agreed with the independent nullspace check on all 18 pairs.

The calculus run is evidence against `OBL-PRICED-INTERVAL-SOUNDNESS`, not confirmation of it:
containment held on 18/18 for both the unpriced carry and the priced interval, but the prices
themselves are over-estimates and no lower endpoint was exercised (the ledger's lower bound
stays 0 throughout).

## Reproduction

Take `854-157-3.json` from `codes/`, remove the Z-check whose support is
[590, 591, 621, 622], and set k = 158. The result is `codes/854-158-2.json`. Then:

```
python verify/qldpc_verify.py codes/854-158-2.json
python verify/check_codes_format.py --files codes/854-158-2.json
```

Both exit 0. The distance witnesses in `codes/854-158-2.json` are the acceptance argument; they are
upper bounds only.
