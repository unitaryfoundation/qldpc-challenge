k=16/18 pair ([[684,16,110]] kd^2/n 283.0, [[684,18,106]] kd^2/n
295.7, both merged from this campaign): no board entry at n<=684
carried k>=20, and a k=20 point at d>=101 would lead the cell outright.
The hypothesis: the affine-2BGA family's exponent-vector search carries
high-k interior points once the high-k screen phantoms are filtered by
full-budget ladders.
