# [[810,166,8]] — weight-6 lifted product over F_2[Z_54 x|_19 Z_3] with 1x2 base matrices of binomial entries

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6, high-rate corner. For k >= 166 at n <= 810 the
weight <= 6 frontier had d = 6 at best ([[546,184,6]], [[768,258,6]]), and the
d = 8 points nearby were [[830,146,8]] and [[864,146,8]]. So a weight-6 code
with k >= 166 at n <= 810 advances for any d >= 7. The board's [[675,139,8]] is a
lifted product over a metacyclic group with 1x2 base matrices of weight-2
entries (n = 5|G|, k >= |G|, check weight 6). The hypothesis was that the same
shape over larger groups (|G| = 121..200) keeps d = 8 while k grows with |G|,
and that d = 8 at n ~ 800 is a claim a fresh-seed ladder settles quickly.

## What was searched

- Lifted products from `lifted_product_base` in research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py with
  A = [a1, a2], B = [b1, b2], every entry of the form 1 + g (identity plus one
  element of order >= 15). Groups of order 121..200: every ZSZ(l1, l2, q) with
  l2 <= 12 (abelian q = 1 included) and the kit's small non-abelian groups
  (C_m x A4, C_m x S4, C_m x D_k). 400 groups drawn, 30 support draws each:
  4,889 connected candidates (disconnected ones dropped by
  `block_structure` in research/kit/prescreen.py).
- Screen with gf2_fast RIS (pair depth 8): 300 trials each. d = 4 or 6 almost
  everywhere; d = 8 only at n = 810, 900, 945 and 960. 219 candidates within
  one of their bar went to 5,000 trials. d = 8 held at [[810,166,8]],
  [[900,184,8]], [[945,193,8]] and [[960,196,8]], and d = 7 at [[625,129,7]],
  [[640,132,7]], [[700,144,7]] and [[825,169,7]]. No candidate reached 9.
- Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,
  w down; equality counts as dominated).

## Evidence trail

The submitted code: G = Z_54 x|_19 Z_3 (x^54 = y^3 = 1, y x = x^19 y; element
x^a y^b at index 3a + b), A = [1 + x^29 y^2, 1 + x^26] (indices [0, 89] and
[0, 78]) acting on the left, B = [1 + x y^2, 1 + x^34 y] (indices [0, 5] and
[0, 103]) acting on the right. n = 5 x 162 = 810, k = 166 = |G| + 4, every X
check has weight 4 + 2 and every Z check 2 + 4. Six different support choices
at |G| = 162 (over ZSZ(54,3,19), ZSZ(54,3,37) and ZSZ(27,6,10)) screened at
d = 8. This is one of them.

| screen stage 1 | 300 | 8 |
| screen stage 2 | 5,000 | 8 |

Both sides carry a weight-8 witness. Claim: witness-backed upper bound
d <= 8, not exact. With d = 8 the code dominates [[830,146,8]] and
[[864,146,8]] outright (smaller n, larger k, same d and weight).

## Dead ends

- Shape A = [g1, 1 + g2], B = [1 + g3, g4] (check weight 5, n = 5|G|) over the
  same groups: d = 3 in all 2,848 candidates at 300 trials. A monomial entry leaves
  weight-2 classical codewords that lift to light logicals.
- Larger |G| in this shape did not lift d. Every n from 900 to 1000 stayed at
  d <= 8, so the k gain at fixed d is all this family buys.
- Abelian groups (q = 1): d <= 4 in all 2,643 abelian candidates at 300
  trials. Every d >= 5 came from a non-abelian group (d histogram of the
  2,246 non-abelian ones: 184 at 5, 537 at 6, 26 at 7, 25 at 8).


the four |G|-blocks (i, j) of sector 1 and 648..809 the single block of
sector 2, in the layout of nonabelian_lp.lifted_product_base.
