# [[360,25,4]] even-d multiband dense-packed surface code (d=4, rows=3, m=13, pitch=2)

## Direction & hypothesis

Target the `local-2d-single / weight-4` frontier at `d = 4, k = 25` via the
even-`d` (`rows = 3, pitch = d - 2`) regime of `research/multiband_surface.py`
(Open Problem 4 of `fieldnotes/2026-09-01-multiband-dense-packing-method.md`).
Because the even-`d` law `n(4, m) = 28m - 4, k = 2m - 1` saves `10m - 8`
qubits relative to punctured `d = 5` two-band codes (`38m - 14`), the qubit
savings grow linearly with `m`.

## What was searched

Evaluated the `d = 4, rows = 3, pitch = 2` ladder across `m in {2..33}` in
`research/multiband_surface.py` and compared against the verified board in
`local-2d-single / weight-4`. At `m = 13`, the construction yields
`[[360,25,4]]` (`kd^2/n = 1.111`, max check weight 4, single-layer 2D
coordinates).
