# [[693,6,64]] — cyclic mirror stabilizer code on Z_693, weight 6

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the weight-6, unrestricted cell of the stabilizer board. At the board commit this entry was prepared against
(7ee74980), its best entry by kd^2/n scored 32.4. If the witnessed bound held, this code would score 35.5. That is a scalar comparison, not a dominance claim: the gate reports
this code as board-advancing without dominating any entry. Mirror codes, the symplectic halvings of cyclic two-block
CSS codes (Khesin and Lu, arXiv:2603.05496; see also arXiv:2609.30069), put a weight-3 X part and a weight-3 Z part on
one cyclic group. That gives weight <= 6 with a large search space at fixed n. We do not claim the family as new.

## What was searched

Cyclic mirror codes on Z_n: generator i has X on i + a and Z on -i - b for 3-element sets a, b. We grouped trinomial
pairs by their common binary-polynomial divisor with x^n + 1, which sets k. Candidates were screened for isotropy and
rank, then by a shallow search on the symplectic double, then by GPU Pauli-weight information-set search. Finalists got
the long runs below. Mirrors of strong CSS parents under Tanner-graph involutions and a weight-4 cyclic family were
searched in the same round and did not beat their targets.

## Evidence trail

Board claim: **d <= 64, witnessed upper bound only.** Proven lower bound: **d >= 24** (single run, not independently checked). So 24 <= d <= 64, and the gap is
large. Read 64 as the lightest logical our searches found, not as the distance.

Lower bound: an exhaustive Pauli-weight branch and bound of the Dumer-Kovalev-Pryadko irreducible-cluster type (the
family of Lee et al., arXiv:2609.30069, App. B.2), our own unpublished tooling, ran at cap 23. It found no nontrivial
logical of weight <= 23 (8,195,946,752,668 nodes, 192 CPU threads, 29,382 s, single executor). The cap-24 run timed out.

Upper-bound ladder, lightest logical returned by each run:

- GPU Pauli-weight information-set search with correlated steps: 74 (20 s), 67 (120 s), 68 (1800 s, 8.4e9 steps);
- fresh-elimination GPU run without correlated steps, 1200 s, 4.8e7 eliminations: 69;
- an independent audit's search of the symplectic double's Hamming sector, with logicals mapped back through the
  mirror's coordinate alignments (1200 s): **64**. This replaced our earlier claimed bound of 65.
- a second independent generic Pauli search with correlated steps, 1800 s, 8.4e9 steps: 64 again;
- a wider-cutoff rerun of the Hamming-sector audit, 1200 s in four fresh-seed chunks: 64;
- short CPU runs (repository-style Pauli RIS, fast and fold passes): 112 to 191, which we read as inconclusive;
- The repository's own gate, run locally as CI runs it (`verify/gate_changed.py` with the base-branch verifier, seed 693006064): orbit-fold passes over 12 detected symmetries, one Python RIS seed of 108,160 trials, and the accelerated pass over 2,710,000 trials before its 90-minute cap: no logical lighter than 64. This says little here: the accelerated pass's lightest proposal had Hamming weight 83 on the doubled matrices, heavier than our witness's 72 (64 qubits, 8 of them Y), so the gate's search did not reach the claimed bound.

The pattern is a warning sign. Our own generic searches stopped at 67 to 69, the structured search found 64, and the
claim came down once (65 to 64). A lighter logical may well exist. The weekly board sweep is welcome to find it.

Equivalence checks. The verifier's single-qubit Clifford test finds no CSS image. No board entry of either type has
(n, k) = (693, 6), so this cannot be a block-Clifford relabeling of a board entry. Coordinate pairings were checked
against the full Tanner-graph automorphism group of the doubled parent: conjugating the mirror involution gives exactly
693 distinct pairings, all covered by the alignments searched above.

## Dead ends

- The claimed bound came down from 65 to 64 under an independent structured search. Our own generic searches stopped at
  67 to 69; one independent 1800 s generic run hit 64 once.
- Perturbing the central cyclic factor of a non-abelian parent gave no stronger survivor in the sampled classes.
- The weight-4 cyclic screen did not beat its target.

## Tools

Found with the Hyra 2.0 research agent. Our own GPU Pauli-weight information-set searches ran on NVIDIA H20 GPUs, and
our own branch-and-bound prover gave the lower bound. pynauty computed the graph automorphisms. The repository's
submission kit and verifier were used unchanged.

## Reproduction

n = 693. Generator i, i in Z_693, has X on qubits i, i + 96, i + 605 and Z on qubits -i, -i - 83, -i - 372, all mod 693.
A qubit in both carries Y. That gives 693 generators of rank 687, so k = 6. Score Pauli weight with Y counted once.
