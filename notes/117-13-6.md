# [[117,13,6]] weight-5 rectangular lifted-product code

## Direction & hypothesis

Use a rectangular lifted product to realize distance 6 with weight 5 checks.
Deleting redundant checks reduces static resources while preserving the two
CSS spaces; no new family or decoding-performance claim is made.

Resource comparison: for k >= 13, recorded d >= 6, maximum check weight <= 5 and degree <= 6, the audited catalog direct-sum baseline requires at least 133 data qubits, versus 117 here. Allowing check weight 6 admits [[30,4,6]] plus [[62,10,6]], with n = 92 and k = 14. The data-qubit benefit therefore depends on the weight-5 constraint. These catalog comparisons use recorded distances, not a uniform exact-distance certification.

## What was searched

This promotion confirms one fixed period 9 pair of 2-by-3 polynomial matrices,
rather than a new parameter sweep. Four redundant rows were deleted from
the 108-row construction, leaving 104 checks and reducing incidences 540 to 520.
Two new independent seeds each ran 1,000,000 fast RIS trials on each CSS side
on this exact final basis.

## Dead ends

- The unreduced 108-check representation has the same stabilizer group and
  distance, with extra incidences. Larger repeated copies do not retain the
  resource advantage; for example [[150,32,6]] fits a target of 26 logical
  qubits more cheaply than two copies of this code.
