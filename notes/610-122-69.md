# [[610,122,69]] cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_305 with k designed from a divisor of x^305 - 1

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: weight-9plus x unrestricted. The board's most efficient entries are
cyclic generalized-bicycle (GB) codes, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)] over Z_m
with m near 340, and each of their construction strings names a designed
divisor ideal of x^m - 1. For this family k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), so
random supports give k = 0 or 2 and the frontier cannot be reached by
rejection sampling at any check weight (issue #2778, measured on Z_315,
Z_330, and Z_341: 150 of 180 random weight-12 draws had k = 2, none above
12). The sampler added in PR #2790 does the opposite: pick a divisor g
whose degree sets k, then draw a and b as sparse multiples of g. This entry
is one of the two Z_305 draws from its first six default draws, filed after
a deeper distance measurement than the sampler's own screen. At n <= 610 the
board had no weight-9plus code with both k >= 122 and d >= 73; the k >= 120
codes at n <= 610 top out at d = 36 ([[482,146,36]]) and the d >= 73 codes
at k = 26 ([[560,26,80]]).

## What was searched

- research/kit/search.py sample_cyclic_gb(6, seed=7) at its defaults: odd
  m in 300 to 345, a divisor g of x^m - 1 with 2 deg g in 120 to 200, a and
  b each a sparse multiple of g at weight 12 to 16 (Prange search in the
  kernel of the circulant of the reciprocal check polynomial), pairs whose
  gcd exceeds g discarded. Six draws in 15 s: n 610 to 666, k 120 to 162,
  check weight 27 to 32.
- Screen (research/kit/surrogate.py, fast backend): 2,000 trials read 82 to
  91 on five of the six; at 200,000 trials four fell to 24, 25, 24, and 9,
  and the two Z_305 draws read 77 and 79. At 2,000,000 trials with pair
  depth 32 they read 75 (this code) and 76 ([[610,120]], the other draw).
- This code: g = 0x3dfd0c90c24c2fef (degree 61), a = x^{22, 52, 54, 62,
  87, 115, 117, 119, 152, 168, 171, 196, 213, 220, 263, 271} (16 terms),
  b = x^{52, 73, 79, 132, 150, 165, 196, 211, 259, 266, 280, 281, 285, 304}
  (14 terms), check weight 30, each qubit in one circulant of H_X and one
  of H_Z.

## Evidence trail

Each reported operator was re-verified in Python before it was recorded
(zero syndrome against the opposite checks, outside the row space of its
own checks, weight recounted); verify/qldpc_verify.py re-checks both
witnesses.

| instrument | side | trials | seed | lightest logical |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| surrogate.lightest_logical, pair depth 10 | X | 20,000 | 11 | 87 |
| surrogate.lightest_logical, pair depth 10 | Z | 20,000 | 12 | 86 |
| surrogate.distance_rand_witness, fast, pair depth 32 | both | 2,000,000 | 7 | 75 |
| annihilator.attack (single-block subspaces, all four) | both | 200,000 | 11 | 82 on each side |
| surrogate.distance_rand_witness, fast, pair depth 32 | both | 10,000,000 | 51 | 73 (Z) |
| the gate's own refutation (validate_candidate.py) | both | 8,000 | gate | nothing below 73 |

The 10,000,000-trial search was run twice with the same seed by a driver
that meant to run the two sides separately; distance_rand_witness searches
both sides on every call, so the second run returned the identical
operator and added nothing. X side: the GB code has the automorphism
(left, i) -> (right, -i mod 305), (right, j) -> (left, -j mod 305), which
maps H_X onto H_Z (circ(a)^T = R circ(a) R with R the index reversal), so
d_X = d_Z exactly and the X witness filed is the image of the Z witness.
Final claim: d <= 73 on both sides, a witnessed upper bound at 10,000,000
CPU trials per side. The cell's leaders have been attacked at 300,000,000
GPU trials per side; this entry has not, and its reading fell from 86 to 75
to 73 as the budget grew by 500x, so a further drop under a matched-depth
attack would not be surprising. kd^2/n = 1066 at d = 73; the cell headline
is [[682,172,78]] at 1534. The sibling draw [[610,120,73]] has the same n
and d bound with k = 120, so this entry dominates it and the sibling was
not filed.

## Dead ends

- Random supports at check weight 12 to 32 on Z_315, Z_330, Z_341, and
  prime orders 251 to 347 (fieldnotes/2026-08-15-dead-ends-and-leads.md):
  k <= 2 on 150 of 180 draws and never above 12, zero survivors at k >= 4
  and d >= 20. Raising weight= on sample_dihedral or sample_kasai_affine buys
  check weight only.
- Four of the six designed-divisor draws at other m collapsed to d 9 to 25
  by 200,000 trials; a shared divisor fixes k and says nothing about d.
- The single-block annihilator attack, which refuted or tightened 87 board
  leaders in the 2026-09-29 and 2026-10-04 sweeps, reached 82 on this code
  from each of the four block subspaces, above the RIS reading; its
  structural shortcut does not beat plain information-set search here.

## Tools

Claude Fable 5.1 (Claude Code) driving the repo kit: research/kit/gf2poly.py
and search.sample_cyclic_gb for the construction, research/kit/surrogate.py
(fast backend, gf2_fast built with make fast) for the RIS ladder,
research/kit/annihilator.py for the structural attack,
verify/validate_candidate.py for the gate. The 10,000,000-trial search took
1,457 s wall on a laptop; the rest under 10 minutes.

## Reproduction

```
from search import sample_cyclic_gb
draws = list(sample_cyclic_gb(6, seed=7))   # the two m = 305 draws
```
or directly: m = 305, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)], H_Z = [circ(b)^T |
circ(a)^T] with the exponent sets of a and b above (circ(a)_{i,j} = 1 iff
j - i mod 305 is an exponent of a). k = 122 by construction, recomputed by
the verifier. Witnesses: distance_rand_witness(HX, HZ, trials=10_000_000,
seed=51, backend="fast", pair_depth=32) returns the Z operator; map it
through i -> 305 + (-i mod 305), 305 + j -> (-j mod 305) for X.
