# [[49,33,5]] — bordered multi-circulant GF(4) Hermitian stabilizer code

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the general stabilizer board (`unrestricted` / `any weight`) at moderate blocklengths ($33 \le n \le 49$) where Hermitian self-orthogonal multi-circulant and bordered codes over $\mathbb{F}_4$ achieve strictly higher minimum distance than pure CSS constructions. Strictly improves the online CodeTables.de minimum-distance lower bound for [[49, 33]]_2 from 4..6 to 5..6. Advances the high-rate stabilizer Pareto frontier in unrestricted / any weight.

## What was searched

Constructed exact Hermitian self-orthogonal $[n, m]_4$ generator matrices in $\mathbb{F}_4[x]/(x^m - 1)$ via Meet-in-the-Middle norm-table matching ($\sum_{i=1}^{\ell-1} a_i(x) a_i^\dagger(x) \equiv -1$ unbordered, or $-1 - \sum_{t=0}^{m-1} x^t$ with an all-ones border column), followed by forced-pivot minimum-weight screening on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 GPU and matroid greedy basis reduction over $\mathbb{F}_2$ to minimize maximum Pauli check weight (reducing `max_weight` to 28). Source archive: github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, `certificates/QECC_q2_n49_k33_d5.json` (Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24518038`).

## Evidence trail

- Witness-backed upper bound in `codes/49-33-5.json`: `d <= 5` (`upper_bound`), confirmed by `cli/qldpc.py` with 20,000 RIS trials plus 2,000,000 `gf2_fast` symplectic-doubling trials finding a minimum-weight Pauli logical witness of weight 5 and nothing lighter.
- Independent projective Meet-in-the-Middle enumeration over $\mathbb{F}_4^m$ (github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, `verify_all_qecc.py`) exhaustively verified zero nonzero codewords in $C^{\perp_H}$ of Hamming weight $\le 4$.
- Verified locally with `verify/qldpc_verify.py` and `verify/validate_candidate.py` (`passed: true`).

## Dead ends

- Pure single-circulant ($\ell = 2$) Hermitian constructions at $(n, k) = (49, 33)$ failed to reach distance 5 due to cyclic weight traps; moving to $\ell \ge 3$ blocks (and $+1$ border where $n \equiv 1 \pmod m$) broke the bottleneck.
- Raw Ashikhmin-Knill symplectic expansion produced higher check weights (16 to 32); enumerating all $2^{n-k} - 1$ nonzero stabilizer elements and selecting a matroid-minimum $\mathbb{F}_2$-basis lowered maximum check weight to 28 while preserving the identical stabilizer group.

## Reproduction

Bordered 6-block circulant Hermitian self-orthogonal [49, 8]_4 linear code [I_8 | M(a_1) | ... | M(a_5) | 1_8] over GF(4) = GF(2)[w]/(w^2+w+1) with first columns a_1=(1+w,w,1,1,1+w,1+w,1+w,1), a_2=(0,1+w,1+w,1,1,0,w,0), a_3=(1,w,0,0,1,0,1,w), a_4=(w,w,1+w,w,0,0,1,w), a_5=(w,1,w,w,w,w,0,0) plus all-ones border, basis-reduced over GF(2) from check weight 32 to max check weight 28. Full symplectic generator supports and Pauli logical witness are recorded directly in `codes/49-33-5.json` (also archived at github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, `certificates/QECC_q2_n49_k33_d5.json`).
