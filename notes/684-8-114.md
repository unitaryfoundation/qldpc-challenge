# [[684,8,114]] seed-weight-9 affine 2BGA at k=8, d<=114 frontier extension

## Direction & hypothesis

Advance the unrestricted weight-9plus cell's frontier at n<=684 with
generalized-bicycle codes over the affine group: Seed weight 9 was an untested
neighbor of the weight-8 sweet spot; its first lane draw landed this corner,
and weight-6/7/8 draws land nearby, so the corner is robust to the seed
weight.

## What was searched

One lane of an 11-lane unattended sweep over affine 2BGA seed vectors at
n<=684 (q in {17,19}, seed weights 6-20, ~300 draws per lane). Each draw was
screened by a 400-trial numpy RIS reading behind a board prepass (CSS,
connectivity, exact-fingerprint and WL dedup); the lane's top band was re-read
at fresh-seed 20,000-trial rungs and packaged by the kit's submit path. The
lane first claimed d<=122; the four independent-seed confirmation rungs below,
run for this submission, read lighter logicals, and the entry was repackaged
at the lowest reading with the witness that set it. CI's independent RIS-fast refutation search (8,000,000-trial class, PR CI, seed 1858076355) then found a weight-114 X logical the local ladder had missed, and the entry was corrected to the CI reading with CI's witness carried. This candidate comes from
the |a|=|b|=9 band at q=19.

## Dead ends

- k=10 draws at this cell repeat one WL signature (the board's 684-10-90
  class); the k=10 corner was abandoned as a sampler trap.
- Unrestricted bivariate-bicycle trinomial/quadnomial pairs on 100 < n <= 240
  are closed by five lanes (~5,600 draws, no gate pass; 93% die in prepass as
  direct sums); non-abelian BBGA and metacyclic 2BGA at n <= 320 closed with
  no gate pass.
- A [[544,12,16]]-class draw is dominated (the gate lists 170 dominators); it
  recurred twice independently and was recorded non-frontier, not staged for
  submission.
- The lane's own deep readings did not survive independent seeds: every
  candidate on this sweep read 1-6 units lighter under the four fresh-seed
  confirmation rungs, and all were repackaged at the lower reading before
  submission. Screen readings sit higher still.
