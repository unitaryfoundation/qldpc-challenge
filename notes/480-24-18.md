# [[480,24,18]] — weight-6 2BGA code on the metacyclic group C_60 x|_17 C_4

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6 (check weight 6), n band 401 to 600. The weight-6 frontier there is thin at k >= 16: the entries are low-rate (k <= 16, e.g. [[434,16,22]], [[510,16,26]], [[576,12,30]]) or high-rate at small distance ([[540,112,8]], [[600,40,9]]), and k 20 to 60 between d 12 and 28 was empty. The hypothesis: two-block group-algebra codes on non-abelian groups reach k well above what cyclic weight-6 codes give at these lengths without the distance collapse of the high-rate entries. At (n, k) = (480, 24) the smallest distance that is not dominated by a board entry with n' <= n, k' >= k, w' <= 6 was 11 when this was gated.

## What was searched

- Family: two-block group-algebra codes H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] on the non-abelian metacyclic groups C_p x|_r C_q (r^q = 1 mod p, r != 1) of order 201 to 300 (n = 402 to 600); supports a, b of 3 or 4 elements containing the identity (a -> ga and b -> bg give equivalent codes), drawn at random; k computed exactly in the worker and only k >= 14 screened (about 2 percent of draws).
- Funnel as for the cyclic sweep: 600 gf2_fast RIS trials (1 thread), promotion to 20,000 and 200,000 trials only while the reading clears the frontier threshold for (n, k, w), then 1,000,000 and 20,000,000 fresh-seed trials and the ladder. A second, weight-6-only run (3+3 supports, k >= 16) used the same funnel.

## Dead ends

- Screening inflation at n >= 500. The 200,000-trial readings of the sweep survivors are far from converged at
  these lengths: a [[584,22]] weight-8 cyclic pair read 85 at 600 trials and 30 at 20,000; a [[504,18]] pair read
  52 at 200,000, 42 at 1,000,000 and 2,000,000, and 40 at 20,000,000; a [[600,14]] metacyclic 2BGA read 62 at
  2,000,000 and the gate's own pass found 50. Every survivor was therefore re-measured at 1,000,000 and then
  20,000,000 fresh-seed trials before it was staged, and only the 20,000,000-trial reading was claimed.
- Degenerate divisors (cyclic sweep). A divisor g that is a multiple of x^m' + 1 for a proper m' | m has many
  weight-4 multiples of the form (1 + x^m')(1 + x^i), and every such pair had d <= 7 at 600 trials (k = 102 to
  136 at d 2 to 4 on Z_204, k = 134 at d <= 3 on Z_201); these divisors were excluded from the sweep.
- Weight-(3,3) cyclic pairs. Enumerated exhaustively for every m in [201, 300]: the connected pairs with k 14 to 16
  read d 22 to 26 at 200,000 trials, tying or trailing [[420,14,22]], [[434,16,22]], [[508,14,24]] and
  [[510,16,26]]; no cyclic weight-6 pair with k >= 18 cleared its threshold, so the cyclic family does not reach
  the empty k 20 to 60 region of the weight-6 cell.
- Random 2BGA on metacyclic groups of order 201 to 300: k = 0 in 49 percent of 400,000 draws (3+3, 3+4, 4+4
  supports) and k >= 14 in about 2 percent. Weight-6 draws with k = 24 to 32 at n = 504 to 560 (C_p x|_r C_q of
  order 252 to 280) read only d 12 at 200,000 trials, one above the threshold, and were not pursued; the
  weight-8 metacyclic survivors read 39 to 74 at 200,000 trials, readings the deeper passes cut sharply
  wherever they were re-measured (first item).

## Reproduction

Construction (also in provenance.construction): Two-block group algebra (2BGA) code over the non-abelian metacyclic group C_60 semidirect_17 C_4 of order 240 (research/kit/group_algebra.py metacyclic(60, 4, 17) and build_2bga): element (i, j) = x^i y^j at index i*4 + j, product (i1, j1)(i2, j2) = (i1 + 17^j1 * i2 mod 60, j1 + j2 mod 4); H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] with L, R the left and right regular representations; a = [0, 173, 202], b = [0, 130, 187] (element indices). n = 2|G| = 480, k = 24, check weight 6 = 3+3.

```python
import sys; sys.path[:0] = ['research/kit', 'verify']
from group_algebra import metacyclic, build_2bga
from css import compute_k
HX, HZ = build_2bga(metacyclic(60, 4, 17)[0], [0, 173, 202], [0, 130, 187])
assert compute_k(HX, HZ) == 24
```
