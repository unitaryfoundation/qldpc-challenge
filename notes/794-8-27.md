# [[794,8,27]] fourth row-space graft of the planar tile code

## Direction and hypothesis

The weight-8, local-2d-single CSS cell contains `[[795,8,d≤27]]` in
`codes/795-8-27.json`, with witnessed efficiency `8 × 27² / 795 = 7.336`.
A fourth local row-space graft might remove one more physical qubit while
retaining `k=8`, maximum check weight 8, the one-layer radius-4 layout,
and weight-27 logicals on both CSS sides. That gives `8 × 27² / 794 = 7.345`.

## What was searched

I mapped the four-check stabilizer moves of the source tile code
`codes/798-8-27.json` through the three existing grafts, excluding supports
that touch any previously deleted qubit. For each distinct surviving support,
I used a row-space representation in the 795-qubit parent to choose a valid
pivot, cleared the target qubit, and deleted it. A cheap screen retained 65
variants with maximum check weight 8, interaction radius 4, and inherited
X-27 and Z-27 logicals. This screen did not estimate minimum distance.

Five target and side groups, covering 47 of the 65 variants, were ruled out
without another random search. Earlier searches on the 796-qubit parent had
found weight-26 logicals after grafts at X targets 19, 38, 775, and 795 and
at Z target 396. Lifting those logicals to weight-27 parent operators and
transporting them through the third graft in `codes/795-8-27.json` left valid
weight-27 logicals containing the corresponding targets 19, 38, 774, 794,
and 396. Deleting any such target on the opposite check side would produce
a weight-26 logical regardless of the graft support.

I ran accelerated random-information-set searches at pair depth 8 on all
18 remaining variants: 10,000 and 100,000 trials each. None found a
logical below weight 27. Deeper searches refuted the sole X358 variant
at 500,000 pair-depth-256 trials (Z-26) and one Z395 variant at 500,000
pair-depth-512 trials (X-26). Both operators lifted to valid weight-27
logicals containing their targets in `codes/795-8-27.json`, which rules out
all nine Z395 variants as well as X358. Eight structural variants remained:
seven at Z target 414 and one at Z target 413.

## Evidence trail

The submitted Z414 move survived 10,000 and 100,000 pair-depth-8 trials
(seeds 424060089 and 514060089; best weight 27), 500,000 pair-depth-256
trials (seed 916540089; best X-27), and 500,000 pair-depth-512 trials
(seed 919100089; best X-27). A representative Z413 move also survived
500,000 pair-depth-256 trials. These searches look for counterexamples;
their failure to find one is not a proof of exact distance.

The unchanged `verify/validate_candidate.py` gate passed with seed
654547610. It recomputed `n=794`, `k=8`, CSS commutation, maximum check
weight 8, one layer, radius 4, and both nontrivial weight-27 logical
witnesses. Its independent 8,000-trial refutation search found no lighter
logical. It found no exact or Weisfeiler–Leman-equivalent board entry and
marked the code as advancing the weight-8, local-2d-single CSS cell by
lower `n`. The submitted X-27 and Z-27 witnesses are transported from
`codes/795-8-27.json` and validate on the final matrices. **`d≤27` is a
witness-backed upper bound, not an exact-distance claim.**

## Dead ends

The 47 variants eliminated by transported logicals were structurally valid
but could not retain distance 27. Their target groups were X19 (8
variants), X38 (9), X774 (8), X794 (9), and Z396 (13). Deeper searches
then eliminated X358 (1) and Z395 (9). Those two lighter logicals lift to
the 795-qubit parent, so changing support at the same targets cannot fix
the distance loss.

## Tools and reproduction

screened with exact GF(2) row-space arithmetic; distance screens used
`verify/gf2_fast.cpp`. The trusted candidate gate ran unchanged.

20, 64, 107, and 130. Their support is
`S={19,57,137,158,414,434,493,534}`. Use row 20 as pivot; clear qubit

qubit. Preserve the order of all other checks and coordinates. The JSON
contains the full matrices, layout, and both logical witnesses.
