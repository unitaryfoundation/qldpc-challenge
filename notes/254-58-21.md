# [[254,58,21]] — GB divisor code on Z_127, weight-16

## Direction & hypothesis

Generalized bicycle (two-block) code on the cyclic group Z_127: H_X = [A|B], H_Z = [B^T|A^T] with A, B circulants; a(x) support {6,37,39,64,68,100,105,106} (weight 8), b(x) support {4,9,40,81,93,95,115,118} (weight 8), max check weight 16. Divisor trick: both a and b are sparse multiples of a degree-29 divisor g of x^127-1 (x^127-1 factors over GF(2) as (x+1) times eighteen degree-7 irreducibles; g here is (x+1) times four of the degree-7 factors, deg g = deg gcd(a,b,x^127-1) = 29, machine-verified), locking k = 2*29 = 58 before any linear algebra. Distance evidence: rising RIS ladder 20k/100k/400k/1.6M/2M trials (fresh seed per rung) flat at d<=21 at every rung; refutation barrage 8M trials x 3 fresh seeds (777/999/424242) at pair_depth 16 plus 3M trials x same seeds at pair_depth 24 (33M further trials) all bottoming at exactly d<=21; the 2M-trial witness search returned the embedded weight-21 X logical, and the GB transpose symmetry (block swap + index reversal i -> -i mod 127) maps it to the embedded weight-21 Z logical, both machine-verified against ker/rowspace.
