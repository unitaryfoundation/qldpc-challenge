# [[936,76,10]] — weight-5 lifted product over F_2[Z_36 x|_19 Z_2] with 2x3 single-element base matrices

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6 (maximum check weight 5). The board's
[[624,52,9]] is a lifted product with 2x3 base matrices whose entries are
single group elements, so n = 13|G|, k = |G| + 4 and every check has weight
5 (three left-acting entries plus two right-acting ones). The hypothesis was
that the same shape over slightly larger non-abelian groups keeps the
distance at or above 9 while k grows with |G|; when this code was gated, the
smallest distance that advanced the board at this (n, k, w) was 9.

## What was searched

- Lifted products from `lifted_product_base` in research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py
  with 2x3 base matrices A (left regular representation) and B (right regular
  representation), every entry a single group element, over groups of order
  47..76: ZSZ(l1, l2, q) metacyclic groups from `zsz` in the same module and
  the kit's small non-abelian groups. 6,744 connected candidates screened at
  300 gf2_fast RIS trials (pair depth 8).
- d = 10 appeared only at n = 780, 832 and 936. All seven candidates in these
  three cells held d = 10 at 300, 5,000 and 300,000 trials; the best one per
  cell went to the gate and to fresh-seed ladders.
- Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,
  w down; equality counts as dominated).

## Evidence trail

The submitted code: G = Z_36 x|_19 Z_2 of order 72 (x^36 = y^2 = 1,
y x = x^19 y; element x^a y^b at index 2a + b), A = [[[39], [8], [45]], [[71], [21], [27]]] and
B = [[[53], [28], [31]], [[46], [57], [55]]] as element indices. n = 13 x 72 = 936, k = 76 = |G| + 4, every
check has weight 5.

| instrument (gf2_fast RIS, pair depth 8) | trials per side | lightest logical |
|---|---:|---:|
| screen stage 1 | 300 | 10 |
| screen stage 2 | 5,000 | 10 |
| screen stage 3 | 300,000 | 10 |
| gate refutation pass (verify/validate_candidate.py) | gate's own | nothing lighter |
| deep ladder, fresh seed 1400001 | 20,000,000 | 10 |
| deep ladder, fresh seed 1407920 | 20,000,000 | 10 |
| deep ladder, fresh seed 1415839 | 20,000,000 | 10 |

The reading never moved from 10 between 300 and 20,000,000 trials, and no
rung found anything lighter. Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 10, not
exact.

## Dead ends

- Shape A = [g1, 1 + g2], B = [1 + g3, g4] (check weight 5, n = 5|G|) over
  groups of order 121..200: d = 3 in all 2,848 candidates at 300 trials.
- In this 2x3 shape, d stayed at or below 9 for every other order in 47..76;
  d = 10 needs the three orders above.
- Abelian groups never exceeded d = 4 in the companion weight-6 sweep of the
  same family (2,643 candidates), so the search kept to non-abelian groups.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 (provenance.model) on a Brev n2d-highcpu-64 box; gf2_fast
(verify/gf2_fast.cpp) through research/kit/surrogate.py;
research/kit/nonabelian_lp.py for the groups and the lifted product;
research/kit/prescreen.py for the direct-sum check; research/kit/submit.py
for packaging; verify/validate_candidate.py as the gate. About 2 CPU-hours
of screening and about 25 CPU-hours of ladders for this code.
