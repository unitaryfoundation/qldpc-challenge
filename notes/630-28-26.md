# [[630,28,26]] — weight-7 cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_315 (designed divisor of degree 14, quadrinomial-trinomial pair)

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8 (maximum check weight 7). At n <= 630 the
weight <= 7 frontier with k >= 28 topped out at d = 20 ([[576,32,20]], weight 6),
and the nearest weight-7 points above it were [[618,24,20]], [[708,24,24]],
[[720,24,24]] and [[826,28,20]]. So a weight-7 code with k = 28 at n = 630
advances for any d >= 21. The hypothesis was that a designed-divisor cyclic
GB pair with one weight-4 and one weight-3 polynomial reaches the mid 20s at
k = 28 on Z_315 (315 = 3^2 x 5 x 7, so x^315 - 1 has many small irreducible
factors and many degree-14 divisors), and that a moderate d at n = 630 is a
claim a fresh-seed ladder can actually settle.

## What was searched

- Designed-divisor cyclic GB over odd m in 301..499, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)],
  H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T]: up to 40 random divisors g of x^m - 1 per m
  with deg g in 11..30, weight-3 and weight-4 multiples of g from 400 Prange
  rounds (`sparse_multiples` in research/kit/gf2poly.py), pairs in bands (3,4) and
  (4,4) with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 22..60, direct sums dropped:
  1,528 pairs over 46 fertile m.
- Screen with gf2_fast RIS (research/kit/surrogate.py, pair depth 8): 3,000
  trials each, then 20,000 on the survivors in cells that could still
  advance. That sweep left seven m = 315 pairs above their bars unexamined
  (two weight-7 k = 28 pairs and five weight-8 k = 38 pairs); all seven went
  to 300,000 trials here, then the most stable one to fresh-seed ladders.
- Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,
  w down; equality counts as dominated).

## Evidence trail

The submitted pair: a(x) = x^119 + x^122 + x^245 + x^302,
b(x) = x^60 + x^205 + x^275, g = gcd(a, b, x^315 - 1) of degree 14
(0x5cf1 as a little-endian bit integer). Every check has weight 7.

| instrument (gf2_fast RIS, pair depth 8) | trials per side | lightest logical |
|---|---:|---:|
| screen stage 1 | 3,000 | 30 |
| screen stage 2 | 20,000 | 26 |
| screen stage 3, seed 3300026 | 300,000 | 26 (X) |
| fresh seed 32001 | 3,000,000 | 26 (X) |
| gate refutation pass (verify/validate_candidate.py) | gate's own | nothing lighter |
| deep ladder, fresh seed 41001 | 20,000,000 | 26 (X) |
| deep ladder, fresh seed 42001 | 20,000,000 | 26 (X) |
| deep ladder, fresh seed 43001 | 20,000,000 | 26 (X) |
| qldpc submit fast pass (packaging) | 10,000,000 | nothing lighter |

The d stopped moving at 20,000 trials and stayed at 26 through every deeper
rung. The Z-side witness is the X-side witness mapped by
(x1 | x2) -> (R x2 | R x1) with R: i -> -i mod 315, which carries X-logicals
of this code to Z-logicals of the same weight; it was validated as a
Z-logical before staging, so both sides carry a weight-26 witness.
Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 26, not exact. The bar is 21, so the
claim stays board-advancing even if a later sweep finds a logical up to five
lighter.

Near misses at m = 315: the other k = 28 weight-7 pair
(a = x^104 + x^174 + x^274, b = x^36 + x^57 + x^215 + x^275, same divisor)
went 29 -> 26 between 20,000 and 300,000 trials. It has the same parameters
and was not laddered separately. The five weight-8 k = 38 pairs did not settle: the
best went 42 (20,000) -> 32 (300,000) -> 30 (3,000,000, fresh seed 31001),
the next 42 -> 30, and the rest read 25, 24 and 21 at 300,000 against a bar of
19. They were not submitted because the ladder was still falling.

## Dead ends

- Weight-(3,3) trinomial pairs over m = 351..500 with k >= 20: d <= 24 at
  1,000 trials in every cell, d <= 8 for k >= 30.
- Weight-7 pairs at n = 700..930 with k = 36..60: d <= 16 at 3,000 trials
  against bars of 13..17 (most cells at d <= 9).
- Weight-6 bivariate bicycle codes on non-coprime tori Z_l x Z_m with
  l*m in 301..392 (about 100 shapes, 400 draws each, exponents uniform or
  planted on multiples of a divisor of l or m): no k above 24, so no
  candidate reached the weight-6 k >= 36 cells, where the bar is 11..13.

## Tools

The pair came out of a designed-divisor sweep run by Claude Fable 5.1
(provenance.model). The 300,000-trial re-screen, the fresh-seed ladders and
the packaging were run by Claude Opus 5.5 after a model switch. Brev
n2d-highcpu-64 box; gf2_fast (verify/gf2_fast.cpp) through
research/kit/surrogate.py; research/kit/gf2poly.py for factoring, divisors, Prange
sparse multiples and the circulant builder; research/kit/prescreen.py for the
direct-sum check; research/kit/submit.py for packaging;
verify/validate_candidate.py as the gate. About 25 CPU-hours of screening,
shared with the rest of that sweep, and about 35 CPU-hours of ladders for
this code.

## Reproduction

```python
import sys; sys.path[:0] = ["research/kit", "verify"]
import gf2poly
HX, HZ = gf2poly.cyclic_gb(315, [119, 122, 245, 302], [60, 205, 275])
assert gf2poly.designed_k(315, [119, 122, 245, 302], [60, 205, 275]) == 28
```

circ(v)[i, (i + j) mod 315] = v[j]; qubits 0..314 form the left block and
315..629 the right block. Unit multiples of the exponents mod 315, common
shifts, and swapping a with b give the same code up to qubit permutation.
Literature novelty unverified; board-relative novelty from
verify/validate_candidate.py.
