# [[762,28,32]] — weight-7 cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_381 (designed divisor of degree 14, trinomial-quadrinomial pair)

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-8 (maximum check weight 7), tier n > 700,
where the verifier admits a claim only with max check weight <= 8 and
d <= 40. That tier is thin: at n = 701..1000 the weight <= 8 frontier had 57
points, and for mid k it is inherited from below 700 ([[576,32,20]] and
[[672,20,32]] at weight 6, [[700,44,16]] at weight 7, [[708,24,24]],
[[944,32,24]], [[948,24,28]]). A weight <= 7 code with k = 28 at n <= 762
advances once d >= 21. The hypothesis was that the designed-divisor
cyclic GB family, which the board uses almost only at n <= 700, reaches
d around 30 at k = 28 with one weight-3 and one weight-4 polynomial once
m has irreducible factors of moderate degree (381 = 3 x 127; the multiplicative
order of 2 is 7 mod 127 and 14 mod 381, so x^381 - 1 splits into factors of
degrees 1, 2, 7 and 14, and the divisor here is one of the degree-14 factors).

## What was searched

- Designed-divisor cyclic GB over odd m in 301..499, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)],
  H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T]: up to 40 random divisors g of x^m - 1 per m
  with deg g in 11..30, weight-3 and weight-4 multiples from 400 Prange
  rounds (research/kit/gf2poly.sparse_multiples), pairs in bands (3,4) and
  (4,4) with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) in 22..60 and k <= 2 deg g + 6,
  direct sums dropped. 1,528 pairs over 46 fertile m.
- Screen with gf2_fast RIS (research/kit/surrogate, pair depth 8): 3,000
  trials each; 20,000 on the 92 survivors in cells that could still advance;
  300,000 on the best 18. At m = 381 the (3,4) band gave eleven k = 28 pairs,
  seven of which were above the bar after 20,000 trials; the best three went
  to 300,000.
- Dominance against the board checkout at run time (n down, k up, d up,
  w down; equality counts as dominated) with the n > 700 admissibility rule.

## Evidence trail

The submitted pair: a(x) = x^47 + x^113 + x^332, b(x) = x^46 + x^68 + x^113 +
x^300, g = gcd(a, b, x^381 - 1) of degree 14 (0x505b as a little-endian bit
integer, irreducible). Row weight 7 on every check.

The d = 33 document was staged first; the gate refuted it with a weight-32
X logical, the same weight the independent 2,000,000-trial run found, and
the document was re-staged at d = 32 with that witness before the deep
ladder. Two 20,000,000-trial runs on fresh seeds and the packaging pass
found nothing below 32; a third 20,000,000-trial run was still going when
this note was written, and the submission was filed only after it finished
without finding a lighter logical. Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 32, not
exact. The code advances the board for any d >= 21, so the margin over the
bar is large even if a later sweep tightens the bound.

Near misses: the other two k = 28 pairs at m = 381 sit at d <= 28 after
300,000 trials ([1, 19, 253] / [145, 272, 285, 297] and [185, 236, 308] /
[43, 71, 149, 297]); not submitted, same cell, lower bound.

## Dead ends

- Weight-(3,3) trinomial pairs, exhaustive over m = 351..500 with k >= 18:
  the only cells were k = 18 (m = 365, 381, 438, 465), 20 (372, 465), 22 (438),
  24 (420), 28 (381), 30 (434), 36 (438), 40 (465); everything with k >= 20 was
  at d <= 24 after 1,000 trials (the k = 28 trinomial pairs at m = 381 have
  d <= 2: a = 1 + x^127 + x^254 type words); the k = 18 cells (bar d >= 33
  from [[672,20,32]]) gave the separately filed weight-6 [[930,18,36]].
- (4,4) pairs at the same m = 381: k = 34 only, all d <= 3 at 3,000 trials.
- n > 720 with k = 36..62 at w = 8 (m = 385, 399, 403, 429, 455, 475, 483, 495):
  d <= 5..20 at 3,000 trials against bars of 21..23; one [[910,46,35]] at 1,000
  trials fell to 25 at 3,000 and below the bar after 20,000.
- BB and metacyclic 2BGA samplers with random supports were prepared but not
  run for lack of box time; the designed-divisor route produced every
  survivor.

## Reproduction

```python
import sys; sys.path[:0] = ["research/kit", "verify"]
import gf2poly

```

circ(v)[i, (i + j) mod 381] = v[j]; qubits 0..380 form the left block and
381..761 the right block. Unit multiples of the exponents mod 381, common
shifts, and swapping a with b give the same code up to qubit permutation.
Literature novelty unverified; board-relative novelty from
verify/validate_candidate.py.
