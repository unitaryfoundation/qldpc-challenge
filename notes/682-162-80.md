# [[682,162,80]] cyclic generalized-bicycle CSS code

## Direction & hypothesis

The CSS weight-9plus, unrestricted cell has a witnessed-bound leader
`[[682,142,82]]` in `codes/682-142-82.json`, with `kd²/n = 1400.01`.
At the same block length, an increase in `k` could outweigh a modest decrease
in the best known distance bound. This search used sparse checks from a cyclic
ideal related to that entry. The submitted `[[682,162,d≤80]]` has maximum check
weight 32 and witnessed-bound score `162 × 80² / 682 = 1520.23`.

## What was searched

The length-341 cyclic ideal was recovered from the published pair of check
polynomials. Its common divisor has degree 71. Permuted-kernel RREF searches
of 20,000, 200,000, and 250,000 trials collected 181 distinct check words of
weight at most 16, including the published pair. Pairwise shifted XORs of
those words produced 15 sparse cyclic-orbit representatives. Among the
admissible pairs containing a closure word, 21 had `k ≥ 152` and check weight
at most 32. The submitted pair has a common divisor of degree 81, hence
`k = 2 × 81 = 162`.

The unchanged `verify/validate_candidate.py` gate passed this corrected

Weisfeiler–Leman-equivalent entry. The claim is **`d ≤ 80`, a witness-backed
upper bound**, not an exact distance or a lower-bound certificate. An
additional GPU refutation pass was queued when this draft was prepared; its
result is not included here.

## Dead ends

All four weight-30, `k = 162` pairs from the first closure batch fell to
weight 62–70 under the full symmetry-fold search. A weight-30, `k = 152`
pair from the next closure batch looked promising at 100,000 native trials,
then produced a weight-79 logical at 300,000 trials, below its required 80.
Later high-dimension pairs with `k = 172`, `182`, and `202` yielded folded
logicals as light as weight 2–70, ruling those pairs out.

## Tools

GPT-6 (Codex), run as a single Codex agent, generated and screened the
construction for `@vprusso`. The search used the repository's
`research/kit/gf2poly.py`, the accelerated kernels in
`verify/gf2_fast.cpp`, and the matrix-derived symmetry search in
`verify/heuristic_distance.py`. Search counts are sampling evidence; they
do not certify that a lighter logical does not exist.

## Reproduction

Let `C(S)` be the 341 by 341 binary circulant whose row `i` has ones in
columns `(i+s) mod 341` for `s ∈ S`. Set

```
a = [0, 1, 5, 32, 77, 81, 99, 110, 154, 160, 198, 205, 232, 263, 275, 286]
b = [7, 12, 48, 64, 94, 95, 117, 196, 197, 241, 253, 283, 301, 314, 335, 339]
H_X = [C(a) | C(b)]
H_Z = [C(b)^T | C(a)^T]
```

These formulas reproduce the checks in `codes/682-162-80.json` exactly.
