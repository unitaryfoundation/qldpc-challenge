# [[33,11,7]] — multi-circulant GF(4) Hermitian stabilizer code

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the general stabilizer board (`unrestricted` / `any weight`) at moderate blocklengths ($33 \le n \le 49$) where Hermitian self-orthogonal multi-circulant and bordered codes over $\mathbb{F}_4$ achieve strictly higher minimum distance than pure CSS constructions. Strictly improves the online CodeTables.de minimum-distance lower bound for [[33, 11]]_2 from 6..8 to 7..8 (and [[34, 11]]_2 from 6..9 to 7..9 via extension). On the stabilizer board (unrestricted / any weight), strictly dominates [[44,10,6]] (codes/44-10-6.json, w=20) on all four axes simultaneously (33 < 44, 11 > 10, 7 > 6, 14 < 20).

## What was searched

Constructed exact Hermitian self-orthogonal $[n, m]_4$ generator matrices in $\mathbb{F}_4[x]/(x^m - 1)$ via Meet-in-the-Middle norm-table matching ($\sum_{i=1}^{\ell-1} a_i(x) a_i^\dagger(x) \equiv -1$ unbordered, or $-1 - \sum_{t=0}^{m-1} x^t$ with an all-ones border column), followed by forced-pivot minimum-weight screening on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 GPU and matroid greedy basis reduction over $\mathbb{F}_2$ to minimize maximum Pauli check weight (reducing `max_weight` to 14). Source archive: github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, `certificates/QECC_q2_n33_k11_d7.json` (Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24518038`).

## Evidence trail

- Witness-backed upper bound in `codes/33-11-7.json`: `d <= 7` (`upper_bound`), confirmed by `cli/qldpc.py` with 20,000 RIS trials plus 2,000,000 `gf2_fast` symplectic-doubling trials finding a minimum-weight Pauli logical witness of weight 7 and nothing lighter.
- Independent projective Meet-in-the-Middle enumeration over $\mathbb{F}_4^m$ (github.com/ramuza0113-sketch/qecc-codetables-2026 @ aa4649d, `verify_all_qecc.py`) exhaustively verified zero nonzero codewords in $C^{\perp_H}$ of Hamming weight $\le 6$.
- Verified locally with `verify/qldpc_verify.py` and `verify/validate_candidate.py` (`passed: true`).

## Dead ends

- Pure single-circulant ($\ell = 2$) Hermitian constructions at $(n, k) = (33, 11)$ failed to reach distance 7 due to cyclic weight traps; moving to $\ell \ge 3$ blocks (and $+1$ border where $n \equiv 1 \pmod m$) broke the bottleneck.
- Raw Ashikhmin-Knill symplectic expansion produced higher check weights (16 to 32); enumerating all $2^{n-k} - 1$ nonzero stabilizer elements and selecting a matroid-minimum $\mathbb{F}_2$-basis lowered maximum check weight to 14 while preserving the identical stabilizer group.

## Reproduction

3-block circulant Hermitian self-orthogonal [33, 11]_4 linear code [I_11 | M(a_1) | M(a_2)] over GF(4) = GF(2)[w]/(w^2+w+1) with first columns a_1 = (1+w, w, 1, 1+w, 1, 0, 1, w, w, 1+w, 1) and a_2 = (1, 1+w, 0, 1, w, 0, w, w, 0, 0, 1), basis-reduced over GF(2) from check weight 18 to max check weight 14.
