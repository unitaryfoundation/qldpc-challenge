# [[128,8,9]] two-block group algebra code over SmallGroup(64,47), a 2BGA archive baseline

Primary reference: [arXiv:2306.16400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16400) (Lin and
Pryadko, Quantum two-block group algebra codes), companion enumeration archive
github.com/QEC-pages/2BGA-codes @ 403d194c3f98, nonabelian row `64 47 8 9 [ 3 ] [ 2, 15, 26 ]`.

This is a reproduction, not a search. The code is the archive's; the entry is
seeded as a literature baseline attributed to the paper's authors. What is new
here is the check that the archive row rebuilds to the published n, k, and
check weight, and a witness-backed distance measurement of our own.

## Direction & hypothesis

Issue #1204's literature cross-check found that the board's `codes/128-8-6.json` ([[128,8,6]], weight 6, a Liang-Eberhardt-Chen baseline) is strictly
dominated at the same n and k by an unseeded 2BGA archive row of equal check
weight, and that a surrogate distance search left the archive's distance
standing. Issue #3658 asks for those rows to be seeded through the gate as
baselines, so the frontier shows the published code rather than the entry it
dominates. This is one of them.

## What was searched

No parameter search was run. The archive row was rebuilt from its published
data alone:

1. The group table of `SmallGroup(64,47)` = C16 : C4 was exported with
   GAP 4.16.1 and the SmallGroups library 1.7.0 through the repository's own
   `research/kit/groups/cayley_table.g`, elements in GAP's `Elements(G)` order
   with the identity first, into `research/kit/groups/smallgroup_64_47.json`.
2. `a` and `b` are the archive's element index lists (the identity is implicit in the archive's lists),
   converted to 0-based indices `[0, 2]` and `[0, 1, 14, 25]`, and the code is
   `research/kit/group_algebra.build_2bga_small_group(64, 47, [0, 2], [0, 1, 14, 25])`:
   H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T].
3. n = 128, k = 8, and check weight 6 match the row (the filename of the
   archive file records total row weight 6 with a of weight 2), and
   H_X H_Z^T = 0.

The reconstruction convention (1-based GAP element indices, identity implicit
unless listed, left action for a and right action for b) was checked before
trusting it: the archive rows that `research/provenance/derived.json` matches
to five board entries (`96-4-10`, `60-4-8`, `72-8-8`, `192-12-14`, `180-4-15`)
were rebuilt the same way, and each came out permutation-equivalent to its
board entry under nauty's canonical form with the permutation verified on the
row spaces.

## Evidence trail

The archive's distance 9 is a QDistRnd probabilistic upper bound. This
entry carries its own witnesses from the kit's RIS search, 40,000 trials per
side on the NumPy backend at seed 20261017:

| side | witness weight | depth | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 9 | 40,000 RIS trials, seed 20261017 | upper bound |
| Z | 9 | 40,000 RIS trials, seed 20261017 | upper bound |

Each witness was checked against the verifier's criteria before writing (in the
kernel of the opposite checks, outside the row space of its own, weight equal
to the claimed value). A deeper search with the `gf2_fast` accelerator, two
seeds at 2,000,000 trials each, found nothing lighter than 9. The gate's
own refutation pass found nothing lighter either. So the archive says
d <= 9 probabilistically and this entry says d <= 9 witnessed; the
`confidence` field records the upper bound, and nothing here is exact until
`verify/certify.py` closes it.

## Dead ends

None in the reproduction itself. Two facts about the archive worth knowing for
anyone rebuilding rows from it: about 2,000 of its 72,637 rows list the
identity explicitly as index 1 and the rest leave it implicit, and the filename
weights (`wt`, `wtL`) are the way to tell, since the archive's README describes
only the implicit form; and several rows are duplicated verbatim across files.

## Tools

GAP 4.16.1 with the SmallGroups library 1.7.0 (through the passagemath-gap
distribution) for the group table; `research/kit/group_algebra.py` for the
construction; `research/kit/surrogate.py` for the witness search (NumPy backend
for the recorded witnesses, `gf2_fast` for the deeper check);
`research/kit/submit.py` for packaging; `research/provenance/iso_common.py`
(pynauty) for the convention check; and the trusted gate
`verify/validate_candidate.py`. Model: Claude Fable 5.1 (Claude Code), as
`provenance.model` records; it ran the reproduction from the archive data under
the submitter's direction.

## Reproduction

`./qldpc reproduce 128-8-9 --construction` replays `repro/128-8-9.json`, which
runs `research/kit/rebuild.py --constructor group_algebra.build_2bga_small_group`
with `{"order": 64, "gap_id": 47, "a": [0, 2], "b": [0, 1, 14, 25]}` and
compares the verifier's fingerprint with the committed entry.
