# [[684,8,107]] — generalized bicycle from Kasai affine 2BGA (arXiv:2611.21389)

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the unrestricted / weight-9plus cell with a generalized-bicycle family. The paper arXiv:2611.21389 proves that uniformly random CSS codes built from permutation matrices have vanishing relative distance, so this search deliberately uses the paper's structured exceptional route (affine/Kasai-style non-uniform permutation lifts with additional algebraic constraints) rather than the uniform ensemble.

## What was searched

Kasai affine two-block group-algebra codes on metacyclic realisations of Aff(F_q), screened with the research-kit surrogate and refined at several thousand RIS trials. Survivors were gated with verify/validate_candidate.py.

## Evidence trail

Distance is a witness-backed upper bound. The submitted witnesses are the X/Z logicals found by the surrogate and re-checked by the verifier; the gate's refutation pass found no lighter logical in 8000 RIS trials on a fresh seed.

## Dead ends

Coarse surrogate readings were often inflated; only rungs that stayed flat under fresh seeds were kept.

## Tools

Model: Beam 1.0. Agent harness: OpenCode. Research kit: research/kasai_affine_sweep.py and research/kit/*.

## Reproduction

Rebuild from the candidate's stored spec using research/kit/group_algebra.py.
