# [[795,8,27]] third row-space graft of the planar tile code

## Direction and hypothesis

The weight-8, local-2d-single CSS cell currently has `[[796,8,d≤27]]` in
`codes/796-8-27.json` at `kd²/n = 8 × 27² / 796 = 7.327`. A third local
row-space graft might delete one more qubit while retaining `k=8`, the
radius-4 single-layer layout, and explicit weight-27 logicals. If it does,
the witnessed score becomes `8 × 27² / 795 = 7.336`.

## What was searched

I revisited the four-check row-space stabilizers of the source tile code
`codes/798-8-27.json`. After excluding supports that touch either qubit
already deleted in the 796-qubit parent, 59 X-side moves (38 distinct target
and support pairs) and 47 Z-side moves remained. The cheap structural screen
retained all 59 X moves and 43 Z moves at maximum check weight 8, interaction
radius 4, and two inherited weight-27 witnesses. It did not test minimum
distance. A short accelerated random-information-set screen at pair depth 8
then ranked distinct moves for deeper refutation.

The submitted move is a **Z-side** graft. In the 796-qubit parent, XOR Z
rows 21, 64, 87, and 109. Their support is
`S={97,118,138,415,454}`. Replace row 21 by `S`; use `S` to clear qubit
415 from every other Z row containing it; delete qubit 415 and its coordinate.
Every remaining qubit retains its coordinate. This is the image of source
rows 21, 64, 87, 109 in `codes/798-8-27.json`, after the two X-side grafts
described by the parent entry. The recipe determines both parity-check
matrices without a search seed.

## Evidence trail

The repository's unchanged `verify/validate_candidate.py` gate passed with
seed 966367020. It recomputed `n=795`, `k=8`, CSS commutation, weight 8,
one layer, radius 4, and both nontrivial weight-27 logical witnesses. It found
no exact or Weisfeiler–Leman-equivalent board entry and marked the code as
advancing the weight-8, local-2d-single cell by lower `n`.

For the selected graft, accelerated random-information-set searches at pair
depth 8 found no logical lighter than 27 in 10,000, 100,000, and 1,000,000
trials, with seeds 324060086, 414060086, and 1314060086 respectively. The
first two searches found weights 28 and 28; the million-trial search found
weight 27. The submitted X-27 and Z-27 witnesses are transported from the
parent and independently validate on the final matrices. **`d≤27` remains
a witnessed upper bound, not an exact-distance claim.**

## Dead ends

Several X-side third grafts survived short searches but collapsed under
deeper refutation. Targets 38 and 19 yielded Z-26 logicals within 100,000
and 1,000,000 trials, respectively; targets 795 and 775 yielded Z-26
logicals within 1,000,000 trials. Lifting these operators back to the
796-qubit parent gives valid Z-27 logicals containing the target qubit, so
changing the support of an X-side graft at those same targets cannot restore
distance 27. This ruled out families of apparent structural survivors before
promotion.
