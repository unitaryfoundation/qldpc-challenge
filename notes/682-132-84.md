# [[682,132,84]] — weight-24 Frobenius-paired cyclic generalized bicycle on Z_341

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the best kd²/n among codes of check weight at most 27, i.e. below
the w = 28–32 band where the board's top cyclic generalized-bicycle entries
sit. The leader in that range was `codes/682-122-87.json` ([[682,122,87]],
w = 20, kd²/n = 1354.0), itself a weight-10 word of the Z_341 Frobenius
family; above it the next entries are [[682,142,79]] (w = 24, 1299.4) and
[[682,182,64]] (w = 24, 1093.1). The question was whether weight-11/12/13
words (w = 22/24/26) of the same family reach the distances the w = 28
entries have (76–78) while keeping k in the 120–190 range, where any d near
the constituent code's Gilbert–Varshamov bound beats 1354.

The family: a(x) over Z_341 invariant under j → 32 j (mod 341), so its
Fourier values lie in GF(32) and each full-order decic of x^341 − 1 divides a
with probability 2⁻⁵ instead of 2⁻¹⁰; b = a(x⁴) or a(x⁸), a Frobenius image
with the same zero set, so the pair shares the whole divisor and
k = 2 deg gcd(a, x^341 − 1). See `notes/682-172-78.md` for the derivation.
What is new here is the check-weight band: the earlier campaigns used
weight-14 and weight-16 words (w = 28, 32).

## What was searched

Four samplers, each 3 threads, on weight 12 (fixed points 6/4/2), weight 13
(5/3/7) and weight 11 (5/3/7) symmetric words, ~1,000 words per batch,
zero sets read off exactly in GF(1024). Kept: shared degree ≥ 61 (k ≥ 122)
with at least six vanishing full-order decics. Per hit: the constituent cap
(`gf2_fast.circulant_gb_witness`, 60k then 400k trials, single-block words
of C_g); hits with cap below the d needed for their k were dropped (most:
caps 16–64). Survivors of the cap entered an RIS ladder of
1k → 20k → 500k → 2M trials per partner, discarding as soon as the bound
fell below the target.

About 137,000 words sampled over the four samplers (≈ 4 h wall clock
each, ladders included): 22 hits with shared degree ≥ 61, six of them past
the cap — k = 122 (cap 88), 122 (cap 96), 130 (92), 132 (92), 140 (84) and
160 (76, not laddered). Every laddered word survived both partners to the
2M rung: nine codes, listed below.

## Dead ends

- Most cap-passing structure is in the divisor, not the word: 14 of 18 hits
  had constituent caps of 16–64 against targets of 74–88, the same pattern
  as the w = 28 campaign (`notes/682-172-78.md`: 33 of 38 hits with caps
  ≤ 16).
- Weight-13 words (w = 26): one hit in the budget (k = 160, cap exactly 76
  = target), not pursued; odd weight loses the x + 1 factor and k becomes a
  multiple of 10, which lands between the useful rows.
- (a, a⁸) partners of the same words are not better: [[682,132,≤87]] and [[682,130,≤88]] after the same 2M ladder, i.e. the two Frobenius pairings of one word land within a unit of each other here, unlike the w = 28 campaign where (a, a²) and (a, a¹⁶) collapsed.

## Reproduction

Let C(S) be the 341 × 341 binary circulant whose row i has ones at columns
(i + s) mod 341 for s ∈ S. With a and b above, H_X = [C(a) | C(b)] and
H_Z = [C(b)ᵀ | C(a)ᵀ]. Equivalently b = {4j mod 341 : j ∈ a}.
Sampler: unions of f multiples of 11 and (12 − f)/2 orbits {j, 32j}, f ∈
{6, 4, 2}, seed 102 of the campaign described above.
