# [[888,170,3]] contracted affine checkerboard core, exact d = 3

## Direction & hypothesis

Issue #2939 recorded a contraction sweep of the affine mod-5 checkerboard
cores that was killed at 11 of 36 seeds while still descending. Its 36x27,
phases (2,2), order 2 base (k = 170) was one of the three bases that never
started. This entry is that base run over all six seeds; the best chain stops
at n = 888.

Construction and tooling are #2938's (`research/geometric_grafts/campaign.py`,
@MathysRennela): rectangle completion of the affine mod-5 checkerboard grammar
of `notes/961-169-3.md`, projection onto the encoding core, then pivot moves
of degree at most 3 (XOR an own-side row into every other own-side row
covering a qubit, delete the row, delete the qubit) accepted only when k is
unchanged, d >= 3 holds exactly, and the stabilizer group stays one connected
block. Our part is running the sweep to completion and filing what it found.

## What was searched

All 36 planned chains, one process per (base, seed), on a laptop and on a
Perlmutter CPU node; the sixth base of #2939's list, 34x29 at order 0, is
incomplete in the constructor and yields no chain, as campaign.py itself
reports. The per-base summary is recorded on issue #2939; for this base:

Two of six seeds end at 888 and the other four at 890, so the base has
converged on a two-qubit band. This entry is the seed-2 chain: base 36x27,
phases (2,2), order 2; projected onto its encoding core, n = 964, k = 170;
76 accepted moves, deterministic. Pivot moves leave a few empty or repeated
check rows behind (four on each side here); those are dropped when the
matrices are written out, which changes neither the stabilizer group nor the
code.

## Evidence trail

- Lower bound, d >= 3: the campaign's exact test (no weight-1 or weight-2
  logical, decided by column enumeration against the opposite checks plus
  rowspace membership), re-run on the final matrices.
- Upper bound, d <= 3: weight-3 witnesses on both sides in the JSON, found
  by the submit pipeline's RIS and re-checked by the gate.
- Certificate: `certs/888-170-3.json`, level `proof_log`. `verify/sat_certify.py`
  encodes "a nontrivial logical of weight <= 2 exists" per side; kissat 4.0.4
  reports UNSAT on both and drat-trim verifies both refutations; the XOR path
  (pycryptosat) agrees. The entry claims `confidence: exact` on both sides,
  which the verifier accepts as an upper bound until the certificate is read.
- Gate (`verify/validate_candidate.py` against main at 9de3faad): passed, no
  exact or WL duplicate, refuter found nothing lighter, `board_advancing`
  true, `dominated_by: []`, cell `weight-8 x local-2d-bilayer`.

## Where it sits

Max check weight 8 and max check diameter sqrt(20) = 4.47 after contraction,
so the cell is `weight-8 x local-2d-bilayer`. The cell's entries with k >= 170
at d >= 3 sit at n >= 897 (`[[897,173,3]]`, `[[899,173,3]]`); this one reaches
k = 170 at n = 888. It dominates none of them (lower k) and none dominates it
(higher n), so it is a new Pareto point. The weight-4 records near this k,
`[[945,172,3]]` and `[[923,173,3]]`, are in a different cell and are untouched.

## Dead ends

- The weight-4 x local-2d-single cell stays out of reach by this move set
  (#2939, "What is dead"); the capped variant stalls near n = 950.
- The 31x31 base at k = 169 reaches n = 869 and the 36x26 base at k = 164
  reaches n = 838 in the same sweep; both are filed separately.
