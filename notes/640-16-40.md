# [[640,16,40]]: distance revision of the board's [[640,16,88]] entry

## Revision history

[[640,16,52]] supersedes the board's [[640,16,88]] entry. The code, its checks,
its layout, and its original provenance are unchanged; only the distance claim is
corrected. A GPU random-information-set search (verify/ris_gpu.py, recover mode, 300,000,000 trials per side, seed 2027) exhibits a weight-52 X-logical and a weight-52 Z-logical, so the previous
witness-backed bound d <= 88 was overstated and the honest parameter set is
[[640,16,52]]. The headline falls from kd^2/n = 193.6 to 67.6. Each lighter
witness is carried in the entry with the budget it was found at and survived;
the other side keeps its original witness where it was not refuted. Distance
remains an upper bound, not an exact claim.

## Evidence

Every GPU proposal was re-validated on the CPU against the sparse check matrices (in the kernel of the opposite side's checks, outside the row space of its own side) before it was recorded.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| X | 104 | 52 | 300,000,000 | 2027 | yes |
| Z | 88 | 52 | 300,000,000 | 2027 | yes |

The pass that produced this ran the same search over every verified-new entry on the board; this entry is one of the few whose declared distance did not hold.

## Correction (2026-10-09)

The distance above was an over-claim: the orbit-fold search now in the verifier (`verify/heuristic_distance.py`, PR #2902) finds lighter logicals constant on the orbits of the code's symmetries (X: 52 -> 48; Z: 52 -> 40), so the entry is filed at d <= 40 with those witnesses and renamed from slug 640-16-52. The over-claim was reported by @MicheldeMessieres (issue #2899). All values remain upper bounds; the narrative above documents the original search.
