# [[514,98,58]] weight-20 cyclic generalized-bicycle from a designed divisor

## Direction & hypothesis

No board entry at n <= 514 reaches k >= 98 and d >= 58 at any row weight. A residue construction cannot aim there, since it fixes k between 20 and 34 whatever the ring. Because k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), choosing a divisor of x^m - 1 and drawing a and b from its ideal selects the dimension instead, and here a degree-49 divisor gives k = 98.

Two facts govern the construction, both checked rather than assumed. x^m - 1 is squarefree over GF(2) only for odd m, which makes m = 257 usable and the even moduli of a residue sweep unusable. And the generators are low-weight codewords of the cyclic code the divisor generates, found by information-set decoding inside that ideal.

## What was searched

The divisor is a product of cyclotomic-coset minimal polynomials over GF(2^16), so its degree is chosen; sampling sparse polynomials and taking gcds with x^m - 1 lands on a target degree almost never and kept 0 of 12 draws in an earlier attempt. Sweeping deg g / m from 0.04 to 0.30 over many coset subsets at nine odd moduli produced 299 priced candidates; 245 were undominated after folding, which Pareto-thinning over n, k, d and w reduced to 32 genuinely distinct front points.

## Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound with weight-58 witnesses on both sides.

    random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6      58   (median 61, maximum 63)
    research/foldbound.py, uncapped             no folded logical found
    verify/validate_candidate.py                passed, not refuted

The symmetric fold finds nothing here, so it neither refutes the claim nor supports it, and the trial-based search is the binding evidence. Both witnesses were re-verified against the committed matrices, in the kernel of the opposite checks and outside the row space of their own side. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature.

## Dead ends

Row weight is this construction's price and the schema is its ceiling. `schema/code.schema.json` caps each check's support at 32 entries, so a heavier code cannot be submitted whatever it scores. Two candidates from this sweep passed full 24-seed gates at row weights 48 and 58, one scoring kd^2/n = 1493.1, and neither is submittable. Rebuilding the heavier one under the cap is impossible: the lightest codewords in its degree-101 divisor's ideal are weight 26 to 34, so any pair of generators sums to at least 52.

Every distance in this family falls as search depth rises, so triage figures are not findings. This code was screened at 69 by a 20,000-trial reading and gated at 58, and siblings moved 62 to 52, 60 to 51, and 108 to 98 under the same treatment.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code. `verify/gf2_fast` for the random information-set search, `research/foldbound.py` for the symmetric fold, `verify/validate_candidate.py` for the gate. The divisor construction and its sweep are described above in enough detail to rewrite; they are not committed here. The 24-seed scan took 5,645s on eight cores.
