## What was searched

The designed-divisor search fixed the encoded dimension and screened candidate
support pairs with progressively larger information-set searches. The
100,000-, 500,000-, and 2,000,000-trial stages produced global upper bounds
88, 90, and 88. A fresh 8,000,000-trial depth-8 RIS-fast run per CSS
direction, using 18 threads and seed 1470671, found independently validated
weight-88 X and weight-87 Z logical operators.

## Distance revision

The entry was filed as `[[674,128,87]]` and was refuted at that reading: a
deeper campaign found lighter operators on both sides, and the committed
witnesses now have weight 80 on X and weight 82 on Z (PR #605, "witnesses at
weight 80 and 82").

The included witnesses therefore support only the upper-bound claim `d<=80`;
no distance lower bound or exact-distance claim is made. The resulting reported
score is

```text
k d^2 / n = 128 * 80^2 / 674 = 1215.4302670623144.
```
