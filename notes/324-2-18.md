# [[324,2,18]] — quantum Tanner code over PSL(2,8)

## Direction & hypothesis

Aimed at the weight-4 unrestricted CSS cell with larger quantum Tanner codes.
PSL(2,8) with a length-2 repetition seed code gives n=324, d=18 at weight 4.

## What was searched

Random quantum Tanner codes over PSL(2,8) with RepetitionCode(2), seeds 0..7,
screened at 300 trials per side and re-screened at 5,000 trials per side.

## Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound d<=18 on both X and Z sides. Full gate refutation
passed on a fresh seed.

## Dead ends

Bipartite variants at n=126/196 did not improve d as much; several seeds gave
d=14 only.

## Tools

qLDPC library (QTCode.random), challenge research kit, gf2_fast. Model: Beam 1.0.

## Reproduction

Group PSL(2,8), seed code repetition length 2, random seed 2, bipartite False.
