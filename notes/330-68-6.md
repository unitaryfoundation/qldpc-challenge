# [[330,68,6]] — self-dual PSL(2,11) {5,5} hyperbolic surface code

Second of three codes from one paper; see `notes/84-38-3.md` for the shared
construction and `notes/546-236-4.md` for the third. All three are rebuilt from
the algebra of arXiv:2610.10948 by `research/hyperbolic_psl.py`.

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: **weight-6 / unrestricted**, family `topological`.

The {5,5} tiling gives **check weight 5** and asymptotic rate 1/5, which is
rarer on the board than weight 6 or 8 — most low-weight entries in this cell are
weight 6. The hypothesis was that a weight-5, rate-0.2 code at moderate n would
find unoccupied ground. It did, and the gate confirmed the gain on `w` as well
as `k` and `n`: the low check weight is doing work the paper's efficiency
headline never advertises.

## What was searched

Same algebraic search as `notes/84-38-3.md`, over generator classes for
`p = 5, q = 11`: `ord(x) = ord(y) = 5`, `ord(xy) = 2`, `y = JxJ⁻¹` for a
projective involution `J ∈ PGL(2,11)`, deduplicated on check-support signature.

That enumeration yields **660 distinct classes**, all giving `n = 330`,
`k = 68`. Every class shares `n`, `k` and check weight; only `d` can vary, and
all measured classes returned the same `d`. The filed class is the first from
the enumeration. `n = 330`, `k = 68`, `d = 6` match the paper's Table 2 entry
`[[330,68,6]]` exactly.

Screening: `research/kit/surrogate.lightest_logical` on a fresh seed, witnesses
confirmed by `validate_logical`.

## Evidence trail

| 4,000 | 6 | 6 |
| 8,000 | 6 | 6 |

Flat on both sides at both depths, so the claim is a witness-backed upper bound
`d ≤ 6`, **not** certified exact. The witnesses carried in
`codes/330-68-6.json` are X = `[5, 63, 112, 132, 157, 168]` and
Z = `[24, 47, 209, 234, 258, 311]`.

`./qldpc submit` re-searched independently through its 2,000,000-trial
accelerator pass and did not tighten either side; it returned its own witnesses
rather than the sweep's, and the committed file carries the submit pass's
operators.

Gate verdict: `passed: true`, `board_advancing: true`, `dominated_by: []`,
`advances_by: [k, n, w]`.

## Dead ends

- **The paper's efficiency result is unreachable.** `kd²/n ≈ 33` is achieved at
  `n = 51330`, which is 73× over the `n ≤ 700` cap. The admissible instances
  carry `kd²/n` of 4–11, an order of magnitude below the cell's headline. The
  records here are Pareto records on the joint `(n, k, d, w)` frontier, not
  efficiency records, and the note should not be read as claiming otherwise.
- **Measure-after-dedup, not during enumeration.** Running the distance search
  inside the class loop cost the entire time budget at `q = 11` for no gain.
  Deduplicating on the check signature first, then measuring one representative
  per size, is what brought this inside a workable budget.
- **`p = 5, q = 5` gives [[30,8,3]]**, already on the board; dropped as a
  duplicate.
