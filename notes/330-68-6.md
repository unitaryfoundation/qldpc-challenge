# [[330,68,6]] [[330,68,6]], a self-dual PSL(2,11) {5,5} hyperbolic surface code at check weight 5, landing in weight-6/unrestricted where weight-5 entries are scarce and topping that cell's small-n frontier on k.

## Direction & hypothesis

weight-6 x unrestricted, family `topological`. The {5,5} tiling gives weight-5
checks and asymptotic rate 1/5, which is rarer than weight 6 or 8 on the
board; the gate reports the gain on w as well as k and n, which is the low
check weight doing work the paper's efficiency headline does not advertise.

## What was searched

Generator-class enumeration for p=5, q=11 with the same relations and the same
support-signature deduplication, giving 660 distinct classes, all with n=330
and k=68. Measured on a fresh seed at 4000 trials per side; every class
measured yields the same n, k and check weight, and the class filed is the
first from the enumeration. n=330, k=68 and d=6 match the paper's Table 2
exactly.

## Dead ends

- The paper's efficiency headline (kd2/n ~ 33 at n=51330) is unreachable here:
  the blocklength cap is n<=700, extended to 1000 only for w<=8 and d<=40, so
  every large instance in the paper is inadmissible.
- Enumerate-then-measure in one pass was abandoned: measuring all 660 classes
  at q=11 exceeded the time budget for no gain, since every class shares n, k
  and w and only d varies. Measuring one representative per size and
  deduplicating first is what made this tractable.
