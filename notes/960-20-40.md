Rajpurohit, Lalitha, Garani. Data and scripts: https://github.com/rajendrarajpurohit/CLP_QLDPC.

- Search-time QDistRnd, 2 x 10^5 rounds per side: d <= 56.
- This repository's fast RIS search, 8M trials, seed 6980315: nothing below 42 on either side; weight-42 X-type witness.
- This repository's orbit-fold search (pynauty automorphisms): 42 at 300 trials; weight-40 Z-type logical at 3000 trials, seed 1, carried as the witness; 30000 trials find nothing lighter.
- Classical construction: a coset lifted product code in the framework of arXiv:2610.12782 (Definition III.1), obtained by the search procedure described in that paper over the GAP SmallGroups library. No AI model.

## Direction and hypothesis

Extension of the paper's search to m = 240 at check weight 6, followed by an entry-resampling walk around the
best W6rest pair. The walk keeps the group pair and layout fixed and resamples one or two entries of the
protographs per step; this code is a neighbour of the W6rest m = 240 family.

## Dead ends

board's entry; the m = 216 and m = 240 pair searches at K >= 20 produced four sieve survivors, all refuted at
