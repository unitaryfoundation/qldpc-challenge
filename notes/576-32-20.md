# [[576,32,20]] — mixed circulant-affine lifted code (arXiv:2610.19451, Construction II) at q=6

## Direction & hypothesis

weight-6 x unrestricted, family tag `generalized-bicycle`. Construction II
of arXiv:2610.19451 (Nourozi, Smarandache, Mitchell) fixes one commuting
polynomial pair over Z_24 mixing circulant and one affine coefficient, then
cyclically lifts it at any outer size q; Tanner girth 8, row weight 6,
column weight 3. The paper tabulates four lifts with certified distances:
[[192,16,8]], [[288,16,12]], [[384,16,14]], [[576,32,20]]. Three of the
four are dominated on today's board ([[192,16,8]] loses to the existing
`codes/192-16-12.json`, [[288,16,12]] to `codes/288-16-16.json`,
[[384,16,14]] to `codes/240-16-14.json` and `codes/360-20-20.json`), but
the q=6 lift is not: nothing on the board holds k >= 32 at n <= 576 with
d >= 20 and w <= 6. Its kd^2/n = 22.22 matches the baseline entry
[[360,20,20]] at double the logical qubits.

## What was searched

Reproduction of a fixed base at its four published lifts, so the sweep is
over q only. Every lift was rebuilt from the polynomials and calibrated
against the paper before packaging: at q=2 the row-56 support of H_X
({6, 31, 56, 120, 142, 152}, their Example 3), the ranks 88/136/184/272 at
q = 2/3/4/6 (Table II), and the weight-8 logical of Example 4 were all
reproduced exactly. The board-domination comparison above then selected
q=6 as the only member worth filing.

## Evidence trail

The paper certifies d = 20 exact by bounded support enumeration but does
not publish the operators ("archived calculations"), so the witnesses were
found here in two independent passes. Pre-screening: an escalating gf2_fast
RIS ladder at pair depth 8 — 200k trials seed 101, then 400k seed 102, 800k
seed 103, 2M seed 104 — returned a weight-20 X-logical at every rung; the Z
side was confirmed at weight 20 by a numpy pass (8,000 trials, seed 999)
after the accelerator runs kept reporting the X side first. The submission
tool then re-ran its own witness search independently (20,000 numpy RIS
trials plus 2,000,000 accelerator trials, seed 0) and re-derived weight 20
on both sides — those are the operators stored in `codes/576-32-20.json`,
with the search budget recorded in the entry's provenance. The refutation
gate found no lighter logical. Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 20 on
both sides; the paper's exactness proof is cited but not re-derived here.

## Dead ends

- q=2, 3 and 4 are all dominated on the board (named above) and were not
  filed.
- Calibration trap worth recording: B(z)'s lower-left block is
  C_8 + z^2 C_22, and folding the z^2 C_22 term into the exponent-0 block is
  indistinguishable at q=2 (the outer shift S_2^2 is the identity) but
  breaks CSS orthogonality at every q >= 3. A builder tested only at q=2
  will not catch it; the q=3 commutation check did.
- Column weight 3 is what buys d = 20 here: Proposition 1 of the same paper
  caps column-weight-2 codes at Tanner girth 12 and those members sit at
  d = 6, so the two constructions trade rate for distance in opposite
  directions.

## Tools

MiMo-V2.6-Flash (OpenCode agent harness). NumPy builder for the lifted
polynomials, self-tested against the paper's Examples 3-4 and Table II
before packaging; gf2_fast RIS accelerator (pair depth 8) for the witness

## Reproduction

Base over Z_24: C_s is the 24x24 cyclic shift P_{t -> t+s}; F is the affine
permutation F_(7,7): t -> 7t + 7. As 2x2 block-polynomials in an outer
variable z,

    A(z) = [[I + z C_2, z F    ],
            [F,          I + z C_2]]
    B(z) = [[I,          z C_8 + z^3 C_10],
            [C_8 + z^2 C_22, I           ]]

Lift at outer size q by replacing z^h M_h with S_q^h (x) M_h, where S_q is
the q x q cyclic shift u -> u+1 and (x) is the Kronecker product; then
H_X = [A_q | B_q], H_Z = [B_q^T | A_q^T], each 48q x 96q. For q=6 this is
288 x 576, rank 272 per side, k = 576 - 544 = 32. Row/column indices follow
the paper's convention 48u + 24b + t with u in Z_q the outer coordinate,
b in {0,1} the block, t in Z_24 the internal coordinate; the B-half columns
of H_X are offset by 48q. The checks and witnesses stored in
`codes/576-32-20.json` are the direct expansion at q=6. Orthogonality holds
for every q by the polynomial identity AB = BA, which itself rests on the
affine commutation F C_s = C_(7s) F (7*22 = 10 and 7*10 = 22 mod 24).
