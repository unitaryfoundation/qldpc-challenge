# [[682,122,55]] — weight-16 Frobenius-paired cyclic generalized bicycle on Z_341

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the best kd²/n among codes of check weight at most 16 (and 18, 20).
The leader at w ≤ 16 was `codes/674-86-87.json` ([[674,86,87]], w = 16,
kd²/n = 965.8), a cyclic generalized bicycle over Z_337; at w ≤ 20 it was
`codes/682-122-81.json` (1173.7, w = 20). The Z_341 Frobenius family (see
`notes/682-172-78.md` and `notes/682-132-84.md`) had so far been worked at
weights 10–16 per polynomial (w = 20–32); the question was whether weight-8
words (w = 16) still carry shared divisors of degree ≥ 41 (k ≥ 82) with
distances in the 80s.

The family: a(x) over Z_341 invariant under j → 32 j (mod 341), so each
full-order decic of x^341 − 1 divides a with probability 2⁻⁵ instead of 2⁻¹⁰;
b = a(x⁴) has the same zero set, the pair shares the divisor and
k = 2 deg gcd(a, x^341 − 1).

## What was searched

Three samplers (3 threads each, ~1,000 words per batch) on symmetric words
of weight 8 (fixed-point counts 4/2/6 and 2/4) and weight 7 (3/5/1), zero
sets read off exactly in GF(1024); kept when the shared degree is ≥ 41 (≥ 40
for weight 7) with at least four vanishing full-order decics. Per hit: the
constituent cap (`gf2_fast.circulant_gb_witness`, 60k then 400k trials,
single-block words of C_g), dropping hits whose cap is below the d their k
needs; survivors entered the ladder 1k → 20k → 500k → 2M RIS trials per
partner, discarding as soon as the bound fell below the target.

Each sampler's first hit passed the cap and survived the whole ladder:

| Code | word | w | cap | needs | ladder (2M) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **[[682,122,≤86]]** (this entry) | weight 8, a = [2,64,74,110,178,240,308,322] | 16 | 88 | 74 | 86 |
| [[682,92,≤95]] | weight 8, a = [50,84,88,143,176,236,301,308] | 16 | 101 | 85 | 95 |
| [[682,80,≤96]] | weight 7, a = [25,45,76,118,121,165,176] | 14 | 96 | 91 | 96 |

Each was the first hit of its sampler's first 1,000-word batch (3,000 words
sampled in all), so weight-8 symmetric words with shared degree ≥ 41 are
not rare in this family; the samplers were stopped there to spend the CPU
on confirmation.

## Evidence trail

Submitted word (weight 8; fixed points 110, 308; pair-orbits {2, 64},
{74, 322}, {178, 240}):

```
a = [2, 64, 74, 110, 178, 240, 308, 322]
b = [8, 30, 99, 209, 256, 265, 278, 296]   (= {4j mod 341 : j in a})
```

Shared divisor of degree 61 ((x + 1) · six full-order decics), k = 122,
check weights 8 + 8 = 16, Tanner graph connected, one stabilizer block.

| Stage | Budget (trials/side) | Lightest logical |
|---|---:|---:|
| structural cap (single-block words of C_g) | 400,000 | 88 |
| RIS ladder | 1,000 / 20,000 / 500,000 / 2,000,000 | 86 |

| `qldpc submit` (2,000 python RIS + 2,000,000 accelerator trials, its own seeds) | 2,000,000 | 85 (X), 102 (Z) |

The claim is a witness-backed **upper bound**; the exact distance is open.
The sibling `notes/682-132-84.md` lost three units between a 10M-trial run
and the CI gate's 8M-trial pass, so a few units of further movement would
not be surprising. Score at the bound: 122 · 85² / 682 = 1292.4. It stays
ahead of 965.8 down to d = 74 and ahead of 1173.7 down to d = 81.

## Dead ends

- The sibling survivors above are weaker on the headline and are reported,
  not submitted: [[682,92,≤95]] (1217 at the bound, 90 after 5M fresh trials) and
  [[682,80,≤96]] (1081 at its own cap, 96 after 5M fresh trials).
- Weight-8 words with fewer than four vanishing full-order decics (shared

## Tools

Model: Claude Fable 5.1 (Claude Code); human author @vaibhav-baj. NumPy
sampler with GF(1024) zero sets; the gf2_fast C++ accelerator
(`verify/gf2_fast.cpp`, built locally with MinGW-w64) for the cap, the ladder

## Reproduction

Let C(S) be the 341 × 341 binary circulant whose row i has ones at columns
(i + s) mod 341 for s ∈ S. With a and b above, H_X = [C(a) | C(b)] and
H_Z = [C(b)ᵀ | C(a)ᵀ]. Equivalently b = {4j mod 341 : j ∈ a}. Sampler: unions
of f multiples of 11 and (8 − f)/2 orbits {j, 32j}, f ∈ {2, 4}, seed 202;
the hit was the first of that seed.
