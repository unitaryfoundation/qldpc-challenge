# [[396,10,37]]: weight-6 2BGA over Z_66 × Z_3

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: `weight-9plus × unrestricted` (checks are weight 6, so the code
also competes on the weight-6 board). Family: two-block group-algebra (2BGA)
codes over abelian groups, following `fieldnotes/2026-07-28-gap-campaign-results.md`:
at weight 6, abelian groups consistently reach higher distance than
non-abelian ones. This campaign extended that work to all abelian groups of
order 100–200 (208 groups, all invariant-factor types).

## What was searched

- ~1,500 random weight-6 support pairs `(a, b)` over the 208 abelian groups of
  order 100–200 (seed 1).
- Screened with the kit funnel: `k ≥ 6`, RIS at 3,000 trials (gf2_fast),
  scored by `kd²/n`. 241 candidates passed.
- Deep re-screen of all survivors at 200,000 trials/side; screening distances
  collapsed heavily (screen d=63 → ≤55 for this code).
- Top survivors re-searched at 1,000,000 trials/side.

## Evidence trail

b=[2,190,124,194,134,158]):

The first submission claimed d ≤ 59; CI's independent RIS-fast pass found a
weight-39 X-type logical (seed 1882473206), which is now embedded as the

bound** (`confidence: upper_bound`, both sides), packaged at **d ≤ 39**.
Efficiency `kd²/n = 10·39²/396 = 38.41`. The trusted validator returned
`passed: true`, `board_advancing: true`; literature novelty unverified.

Sibling finds from the same sweep: [[400,12,≤52]] and [[288,16,≤36]] (both
gate-passed and board-advancing, submitted separately); [[384,12,≤54]]
(valid but dominated in its cell).

## Dead ends

- 3,000-trial screening distances are unusable as ranking signals at these
  sizes (10–65% distance loss at 200k trials across the top records).
- Gate-time refutations are real even after a 1M-trial floor: this very code's
  packaging witnesses (X ≤ 60) did not survive the gate's deeper independent
  search, which found 39 — a much larger collapse than the packaging pass
  suggested. Sibling candidates were refuted too ([[216,10,26]] → 24, an
  earlier [[288,16]] packaging → 33).
- Most screened survivors fell below `kd²/n = 15` once distances were honest;
  the productive region was N = 160–200 with two/three-factor invariant types.
