# [[682,142,86]] — weight-28 cyclic generalized bicycle

This submission claims a witness-backed **d <= 86**, with maximum check
weight 28 and score `k*d^2/n = 1539.929619`. That is **5.71330%** above
the posted unrestricted headline 1456.703812 for [[682,172,76]]. This comparison
uses the board's upper-bound convention; no exact distance or literature
novelty is claimed.

## Direction & hypothesis

The target was the unrestricted weight-9plus frontier. Sparse polynomial
pairs sharing a degree-71 cyclic gcd retain k=142. Changing the relative
Frobenius alignment of two weight-14 words preserves their separate ideals
while changing the mixed logical search problem. Exact A and B below specify
the resulting pair without any dependency on an earlier submission.

## What was searched

A catalog contained three prior sparse words in the selected ideal. A
fixed-seed constructor search (92630001) added 60 words of weight at most 16
in 302,793 iterations, about 144 seconds; candidates with extra gcd factors
or duplicate shift/Frobenius orbits were excluded. This was constructor
work, not quantum-distance search.

Thirty fixed pairs were selected with seed 92630002: four distinct
alignments of the two weight-14 words and 26 additional pairs from the
catalog. Each received a structural 20,000-trial call and 10,000 general
trials per side, pair depth 8, two threads. Twenty-nine survived this initial
screen; independent constituent, norm and character checks left 27. This submission
was the first of the four weight-28 alignments in that declared order.

## Evidence trail

Initial structural and general calls at seeds 92631001 and 92631002 returned
weights 96 and 93. An independently verified pure-constituent witness reduced
the retained bound to 88. Norm lifts from q=11 and q=31 quotients returned
full-code weights 155 and 110, so they did not tighten that bound.

Six single-character restrictions received 20,000 trials each, pair depth 32,
one thread, seeds 927400000–927400005. The factor bitmasks in search order
were 59,37,41,47,55,61, and returned weights were 96,128,128,112,128,128.
Every lifted or restricted witness was checked on the unrestricted original
matrices. These are searches in subsets of the logical space, not proofs.

A fresh **500,000 native trials per side** at pair depth 64 used five
100,000-trial slices, two threads and seeds 928300000–928300004. Returned
weights were 87, 87, 88, 87, 89; the retained bound became 87. These calls took
about 485 seconds in total.

A further **1,000,000 fresh trials per side** at pair depth 8 completed in ten 100,000-trial slices, with four threads and seeds 928410001–928410010. Returned weights were 91, 86, 88, 89, 87, 90, 91, 90, 89, 89; the retained bound was 86.

An additional audit searched all 15 paired-character restrictions at 20,000
trials each: 300,000 restricted trials, pair depth 32, one thread, seeds
92855000–92855014. It used the full original logical basis, checked every
returned support on the original matrices, and found no improvement; the
best restricted weight was 96. In lexicographic order of pairs from the
sorted factor list 37,41,47,55,59,61, returned weights were 138, 140, 136, 124, 140, 136, 140, 128, 140, 136, 116, 122, 96, 144, 120.

The submitted X witness was found by native RIS at seed
928410002, pair depth 8, in a 100,000-trial-per-side
slice. The opposite Pauli witness is its blockswap/reversal image. Both
supports were independently checked for zero syndrome and nontriviality
modulo stabilizers on the original matrices, and are included in
`codes/682-142-86.json`.

The final unchanged candidate gate passed at seed 1140071755, finding no lighter logical in 8,000 RIS trials, no exact duplicate and no Weisfeiler–Lehman match. It labels the entry board-advancing and literature novelty unverified.

This matrix pair has received no GPU audit, no completed 8-million-trial
full-basis battery, and no exact-distance certificate. Searches performed
on a different relative alignment are not counted here.

## Dead ends

One of the 30 initial pairs fell to 85 and two further pairs to 80 under
character restrictions. In the wider campaign, a k=140 candidate retained 88
through a million native trials before a GPU witness reduced it to 82.
A different k=142 relative alignment retained 86 through general searches
before a character restriction found 80. These failures motivated retaining
all known witnesses and using independent restricted and general searches.

## Reproduction

Let m=341 and C(S)[i,j]=1 exactly when `(j-i) mod m` belongs to S. All
indices are zero-based. Use:

```text
A = [0,1,28,90,105,127,138,150,155,181,187,259,292,307]
B = [0,1,32,55,88,99,102,132,167,195,202,229,319,326]
HX = [C(A) | C(B)]
HZ = [C(B)^T | C(A)^T]
```

Both matrices have 341 rows and rank 270, giving n=682 and k=142. Every
check has weight 28. Both support polynomials have the degree-71 gcd
with x^341+1 whose integer coefficient bitmask is 2817392718240873866201.
The reconstruction was compared entry by entry with the submitted JSON.

The cyclic involution maps q<m to `m+(-q mod m)` and q>=m to
`-(q-m) mod m`. For the character restrictions, take each factor bitmask f
listed above, block b in {0,1}, residue r in {0,...,10} and t in
{0,...,30}. Add a parity constraint on `b*341+r+11*((t+i) mod31)` for each
set bit i of f. Append these constraints to HZ and use the complete
Z-logical basis as the detector observables. Run the trusted detector-matrix
RIS routine with the recorded settings, then validate returned supports on
the unrestricted original code. For a paired-character restriction,
multiply the two factor polynomials over GF(2) and apply the same constraint
construction using the product's coefficient bitmask.
