# [[30,2,5]] — quantum Tanner code over PSL(2,5)

## Direction & hypothesis

Aimed at the weight-4 unrestricted CSS cell, looking for a small-n, high-d quantum
Tanner code. Quantum Tanner codes over projective special linear groups with a
short repetition seed code give constant-rate-ish small instances with weight-4
checks; smaller groups (PSL(2,5)) keep n under the cap while retaining d above
the surface-code point.

## What was searched

Random quantum Tanner codes `QTCode.random(PSL(2,5), RepetitionCode(2), seed, bipartite=True)`
for seeds 0..11. Each instance's X/Z parity checks were packaged as a CSS code,
prescreened for commutation, connectivity, and block structure, then screened
with the randomized logical search at 300 trials per side. Survivors were
re-screened at 10,000 trials per side; d=5 stayed stable.

## Evidence trail

Candidate: [[30,2,5]], weight 4, CSS, unrestricted. Witness-backed upper bound:
X and Z sides both have a logical of weight 5 and none lighter was found in the
screening budget. Full gate refutation passed on a fresh seed.

## Dead ends

Non-bipartite PSL(2,5) rep-2 instances with n=60/100 were not dominated-improving
in the same way; rep-3 instances landed at w=6 with d around 3-6 and did not
advance their cell. Cyclic-group Tanner codes screened at d<=4.

## Tools

qLDPC library (QTCode.random), challenge research kit (surrogate, prescreen,
submit), gf2_fast accelerator. Model: Beam 1.0.

## Reproduction

Group: PSL(2,5); seed code: repetition code of length 2; bipartite construction;
random seed 0. Rebuild with `QTCode.random(PSL(2,5), RepetitionCode(2), seed=0, bipartite=True)`.
