## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the best kd²/n among codes of check weight at most 22 (and 20). The
leader there was `codes/682-122-81.json` ([[682,122,81]], w = 20,
kd²/n = 1173.7, after its distance revision), a weight-10 word of the Z_341
Frobenius family. This entry is a weight-11 word (w = 22) from the same
campaign as `notes/682-132-64.md`, where the construction, the sampler and
the screening ladder are described; this note records only what is specific
to the k = 130 code.

The family: a(x) over Z_341 invariant under j → 32 j (mod 341), so each
full-order decic of x^341 − 1 divides a with probability 2⁻⁵ instead of 2⁻¹⁰;
b = a(x⁴) has the same zero set, so the pair shares the divisor and
k = 2 deg gcd(a, x^341 − 1). Odd weight loses the x + 1 factor, so k is a
multiple of 10 here: deg 65 = one quintic + six full-order decics, k = 130.

## Reproduction

Let C(S) be the 341 × 341 binary circulant whose row i has ones at columns
(i + s) mod 341 for s ∈ S. With a and b above, H_X = [C(a) | C(b)] and
H_Z = [C(b)ᵀ | C(a)ᵀ]. Equivalently b = {4j mod 341 : j ∈ a}. Sampler: unions
of f multiples of 11 and (11 − f)/2 orbits {j, 32j}, f ∈ {5, 3, 7}, seed 104
of the campaign in `notes/682-132-64.md`.
