# [[64,18,d<=8]] — 2BGA reconstruction from Lin–Pryadko database

## Direction & hypothesis

Reconstruct a known 2BGA code from the Lin–Pryadko public database
(github.com/QEC-pages/2BGA-codes, commit 403d194c) to fill a gap on the
weight-8 × unrestricted board. The database entry for GAP SmallGroup(32,21)
reports exact d=8 for [[64,18,8]], which would advance the weight-8 cell.


## What was searched

Single reconstruction from the public database. The code is a generalized
bicycle (2BGA) over the group G = SmallGroup(32,21) with:

- a(x) = 1 + g2 + g3 + g17
- b(x) = 1 + g7 + g22 + g31

where g_i denotes the i-th group element in GAP's ordering. The construction
follows the database's specification exactly; no search or mutation was applied.


## Evidence trail

- Validator gate (verify): passed — schema, n/k/CSS commutation, weight class,
  and both witness checks all OK.
- Refutation gate: not refuted — 5060 RIS trials per side found no lighter
  logical (seed 239599230).
- Deduplication: not an exact duplicate of any board entry.
- Board novelty: advances the weight-8 × unrestricted cell.

Distance claim: witness-backed upper bound `d <= 8` on both X and Z sides.
The paper reports exact d=8, but this submission retains the repository's
conservative upper-bound confidence until trusted certification.

## Circuit tier (contributed 2026-10-22, @willzeng)

The entry now ships flag-protected syndrome-extraction memory circuits with
d_circ = 7 in both bases (code distance 8), replacing nothing: the entry had no
circuit tier before.

Why the generic schedules fall short. The generator in `research/circuit_autogen.py`
cannot interleave this code (its two-block decomposition into commuting
permutations fails: the group SmallGroup(32,21) is non-abelian and the left and
right terms do not pair up), so it falls back to the generic sequential schedule,
which measures d_circ = 3 in both bases under a 10-minute RIS search. The loss is
hook errors: an X fault on a weight-8 ancilla after its j-th CNOT puts X on the
remaining 8-j data qubits, and the exact hook-cost oracle of
`fieldnotes/2026-09-18-hook-cost-and-flag-schedules.md` shows that the best
bare-ancilla CNOT order of every check still admits a single hook within 5 data
errors of a logical, so no bare-ancilla schedule of this code can exceed 6.
Combinations of hooks on different ancillas are worse: with one flag per check,
a SAT search over CNOT orders that forbids every pair and triple of flagged
hooks near a logical converged, but a 10-minute RIS search still found weight-4
fault sets built from three unflagged weight-2 hooks after the second flag
coupling.

The schedule. X checks then Z checks each round, joint reset and measurement
layers, one ancilla and two flag qubits per check (256 qubits in total). Flag A
is coupled to the ancilla after the 1st and 7th data CNOT and flag B after the
2nd and 6th, so a hook after the 3rd to 6th CNOT flips both flags (3 faults to
hide, including the two flag-measurement flips) and one after the 2nd or 7th
flips flag A (2 faults). The reachable error sets below their weight are then,
per check order: windows of 2 to 4 consecutive CNOTs inside positions 3 to 6
and the windows 2-6 and 3-7 at cost 2 (two hooks on one ancilla cancelling the
flags), and the suffixes of length 3 to 5 plus the windows 2-4, 2-5, 4-7 and
5-7 at cost 3. Each check's CNOT order and the 12 CX layers of each block (a
proper edge coloring, no data qubit twice in a layer) were found by a SAT
solver (CaDiCaL via python-sat) with the static constraints that every cost-2
set has hook cost at least 5 and every cost-3 set at least 4, and lazy
constraints: after each solution, every combination of two to four such sets
plus data errors reaching a nontrivial logical at fewer than 7 faults was
located exactly by a meet-in-the-middle search over syndrome-and-logical keys
and forbidden as a clause. Both blocks were admitted after 31 iterations.
Target 8 is statically infeasible for this flag layout on both sides (no
check order keeps every cost-2 window at hook cost 6).

Witnesses. The committed `circuit.d_circ` witnesses were constructed from the
analysis rather than found by search: in each basis, two flagged error sets of
two checks plus one data error, realized as faults in the last round, and
checked against the derived detector error model with the verifier's own GF(2)
witness check. A 10-minute RIS search per basis on the committed detector error
models (34,368 mechanisms each) found nothing lighter than 7, which the
analysis says is the minimum over all hook mechanisms.

The scheduling scripts (hook-cost oracle, SAT scheduler, flagged builder,
witness constructor) are local staging output of this contribution and are not
committed here; the method above is meant to be enough to rewrite them.
