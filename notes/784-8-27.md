# [[784,8,27]] — two local reductions of a planar tile code

## Direction & hypothesis

The published `codes/786-8-27.json` is a weight-8, single-layer 2D tile
code with eight logical qubits and explicit weight-27 X and Z logicals.
We tested whether a local two-row Z pivot followed by an X pivot could
remove two physical qubits while preserving that witnessed bound. The
reported distance remains an upper bound.

## What was searched

The first reduction combines Z rows 126 and 148 of the published code
to the local support `{548,568}` and removes qubit 548. The staged
785-qubit intermediate has `k=8` and retained weight-27 X and Z
witnesses. Its one-row scan found 100 structural 784-qubit variants,
49 of which retained rank eight, connectivity, weight-8 checks,
single-layer radius-4 layout, and valid inherited logicals with
neither side below 27. Projection of 3,912 light supports, seeded by
validated logicals and closed under stabilizer-row XOR, produced a
checked weight-below-27 logical
for 48 variants. X394/pivot17 was the sole survivor of that screen.

## Evidence trail

The unchanged trusted candidate gate passed the X394/pivot17 JSON
with seed 1431747074 against the 2026-10-17 board. It checked CSS
commutation, `n=784`, `k=8`, maximum check weight 8, a connected
one-layer radius-4 layout, explicit weight-27 X and Z logicals,
deduplication, and an 8,000-trial RIS refutation search. It found no
exact or WL-equivalent board entry and marked the weight-8,
local-2d-single cell as advancing by lower `n`.

An independent two-step reconstruction from `codes/786-8-27.json`
exactly matched the final checks and coordinates. The Z witness
matches projection of the published parent; the X witness is a
validated accelerated RIS result on the final matrices.

On the exact 784-qubit matrices, accelerated RIS returned a weight-28
X logical at 10,000 pair-depth-8 trials (seed 792404000), followed
by weight-27 X logicals at 100,000 pair-depth-512 (seed 1296494000),
100,000 pair-depth-4096 (seed 4880494000), 500,000
pair-depth-512 (seed 1296894000), 1,000,000 pair-depth-4096
(seed 4881394000), and 1,000,000 pair-depth-512 trials
(seed 1297394000). Every returned support was
checked on those matrices. On 2026-10-18 a 20,000,000-trial
accelerated RIS search with the trusted `verify/heuristic_distance.py`
(gf2_fast, pair depth 8, seed 20261018) on the final matrices returned
a validated weight-27 X logical and no logical lighter than 27 on
either side; its lightest Z find was weight 31.

The witnessed score is `8 × 27² / 784 = 7.439`, versus 7.420 for
the published 786-qubit parent. **`d≤27` is a witness-backed upper
bound.** The bounded searches do not prove minimum distance 27.

## Dead ends

All 48 connected direct one-row shortenings of the published 786-qubit
code yielded validated weight-26 logicals under projection of saved
parent supports. After the two-row Z pivot, 48 of 49 connected second
reductions likewise fell to validated light logicals.

## Reproduction

Start from `codes/786-8-27.json`. XOR Z rows 126 and 148 to obtain
`{548,568}`. Replace Z row 126 by that combination, clear qubit 548
from every other Z row, then delete row 126 and qubit 548. Shorten
X rows and remove coordinate 548, shifting larger indices down by
one. In the resulting 785-qubit matrices, X row 17 has support
`{0,20,394,409,410}`. Use it to clear qubit 394 from other X rows,
delete X row 17 and qubit 394, shorten Z rows, and remove coordinate
394. Preserve the other row orders. The final JSON contains the two
weight-27 logical supports.
