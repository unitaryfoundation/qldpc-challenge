## Direction & hypothesis

Aim at the weight-8 x local-2d-bilayer track cell with a single code-calculus move from
a board entry that was itself submitted after the last code-calculus campaign.
The hypothesis was that the codes added to the board since
`cc-deletion-peel-scaled-2026-11-18` (2026-11-18) are fresh, untried parents for
the calculus, so a one-step child can land on a Pareto point the earlier sweep
could not reach. This entry is one such new frontier point: it is
`board_advancing: true` in cell weight-8 x local-2d-bilayer at the gate, advancing on `k`, `n`.

## What was searched

The parent pool was every CSS code added to `upstream/main` after the last
campaign's final submission (194 codes total; 57 CSS, 137 stabilizer). Each of
the 57 CSS parents was expanded with the code-calculus v0.3.0 planner
(`sector_targeted=True`, `target_sector` inferred, `support_search` budget 512
at max weight 6, `sector_swap=True`, `ranking='pareto'`, domination-reduced),
the top four children per parent were prefiltered for per-cell Pareto novelty,
and survivors were spent on the trusted gate. Its first shard alone generated
8,360 children and spent 60 gates; six gate-confirmed, mutually non-dominating
children were kept. This entry is the `C-DELETE-CHECK` child of
`codes/120-65-2.json`.

## Evidence trail

## Dead ends

refuted by the gate (41 of 60 gated children failed). `E-PUNCTURE` and

dominated by [[111,31,1]] from the same parent and was dropped before
