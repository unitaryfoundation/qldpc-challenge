# [[512,128,19]] — pair-partition CPM code at period 32, check weight 9

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: CSS, unrestricted, weight-9plus (w <= 9). Family: pair-partition (other).
Aimed at the 512-qubit PP cell where the board already carries
`codes/512-128-20.json` (d = 20, w = 10). Hypothesis: the quaternary PP construction of
arXiv:2610.06820 (period 32, 3x8 checks, rate 1/4) admits a w = 9
presentation with d = 19 that is not dominated by the existing w = 10 entry,
because the w axis is part of the Pareto frontier.

## What was searched

Construction taken directly from the companion repository
`github.com/yeon-lee/public-regular-logical-frames` (Jong Yeon Lee, 2026),
code id `pp-f4-512-128-19-w9`: coefficient array and exponent lift for a
period-32 cyclic group, 3x8 quaternary check matrices, full-rank augmentation.
No new search was run; the distance certificate (completed exhaustive search
through weight 18 plus a verified weight-19 witness) is the paper's and the
companion repo's. The entry was converted to the challenge schema and re-gated
locally (`verify/validate_candidate.py`): passed, board-advancing, no
dominators, no refutation in 8000 RIS trials per side.

## Evidence trail

- Claimed distance d = 19 (upper bound, witness-backed).
- X and Z witnesses of weight 19 are carried in `codes/512-128-19.json`.
- The companion repository documents the exhaustive exclusion (through weight

## Dead ends

The w = 10 and w = 11 variants at the same (n, k, d) are dominated by the
existing `512-128-20` entry and are not filed. The w = 9 variant at d = 19 is
the only presentation in this family that advances the frontier.
