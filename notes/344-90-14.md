# [[344,90,14]] — pair-partition CPM (3,8) code at the prime lift P = 43

Witness-backed upper bound d <= 14 (explicit weight-14 logicals on both
sides); no exact distance is claimed.

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: CSS, unrestricted x weight-8. The high-rate end of that cell is the
(3,8) pair-partition CPM family of Okada and Kasai (arXiv:2607.14091):
n = 8P, k = 2P + 4, row weight 8, column weight 3. A grid of the smallest
undominated d at each (n, k), read from codes/*.json with the cell rule of
TRACKS.md (research/does_it_advance.py), showed the family seeded densely at
P = 13-37, 42, 49, 54, 59, 61, 64, 71-73, 79, 83, 89, 96-103, 113, 116 and
122, but nothing at P = 41, 43, 53 or 67, and at P = 47 only the
halving-constrained draw codes/376-98-8.json. At n <= 344 no w <= 8 entry
with k >= 90 reaches d = 7, so any P = 43 draw with d >= 8 is a new Pareto
point; d = 14 matches codes/392-102-14.json (P = 49) at smaller n and lifts
the cell's best kd^2/n below n = 392 from 37.7 (codes/336-88-12.json) to
51.3. The pilot in notes/568-146-18.md had seen only d = 12 at P = 41 in a
handful of draws, so the hypothesis was that d = 14 exists at P = 41-47
but is rare, and that a few thousand cheap girth-8 draws would find it.

## What was searched

Design system of arXiv:2607.14091 as used in notes/712-182-20.md: the cube
1-factorization M0 = (0,4)(1,7)(2,6)(3,5), M1 = (0,7)(1,2)(3,4)(5,6),
M2 = (0,2)(1,5)(3,7)(4,6), cell (i, j) of the 3 x 3 array using
M[(i - j) mod 3]; the 36 paired-difference equations
E_x[i][a] - E_x[i][b] - E_z[j][a] + E_z[j][b] = 0 in the 48 exponents have
nullity 19 over F_P. The sampler was calibrated by rebuilding
codes/568-146-18.json bit for bit from the arrays in its note. Per draw:
a uniform coefficient vector, rejected unless both exponent arrays have no
vanishing 4-cycle or 6-cycle sum (Tanner girth 8); 600 single-coefficient
moves accepting any move that keeps girth 8 and does not raise the number of
vanishing 8-cycle sums; GF(2) ranks for k; a 2,000-trial RIS screen per side
(research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness over verify/gf2_fast, pair
depth 8). A reading at or above the lift's target got 30,000 more trials on
a fresh seed, and in the first batch 300,000 more. One draw costs about one
CPU-second; 18 cores ran the sweep.

and two later ones were run through the same ladder as a control (below).

## Evidence trail

Every number is the lightest nontrivial logical the search returned,
re-validated in Python (zero syndrome against the opposite checks, outside
the row space of its own checks). Column weight 3 makes every logical of
this family even in weight (the all-ones vector is the sum of either check
matrix), so 13 cannot occur and a refutation would read 12.

pattern the family showed at every lift here: the 2,000-trial screen was
already reading the lightest logical the accelerator finds at eight million.

Claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 14, X <= 14 and Z <= 14, confidence
upper_bound. The construction, the design system and the matchings are
Okada and Kasai's; the contribution here is the draw at a lift they did not
publish and its screening. Prior-art check for these parameters: Table I
of arXiv:2607.14091 lists (3,8) instances at P = 29, 31, 37, 59, 61, 73,
139, 167 and 223 only; the authors' data repository
(github.com/kasaikenta/pair-partition-cpm-css-codes @ 9c2a6f2,
`data/reconstructed_instances/`) carries (3,8) instances at P = 53, 59 and
61 only; the tables of arXiv:2609.30069 (Lee, Okada, Maskara, Kasai, Zhou)
and the Error Correction Zoo entry for the family name no code with
n = 344; and notes/264-70-12.md, which screened the authors' catalogue page
row by row, found nothing admissible and absent at this length. The live
catalogue page itself could not be reached from this session, so
literature novelty stays unverified, as the gate labels it.

## Dead ends

- P = 41: 2,234 girth-8 draws and no reading above 12 (913 at 12). A
  [[328,86,12]] would itself be undominated, but it was not filed: the
  same budget bought d = 14 at P = 43, and notes/712-182-20.md records
  that the cube triple is the only disjoint matching triple admitting
  girth 8, so a different design system is not available at this weight.
- The 8-cycle objective does not separate readings. At P = 43 the draws
  reading 14 had 2,000-2,350 vanishing 8-cycle walk sums after the climb
  and the draws reading 10 had 2,150-2,450; at P = 67 the spread was the
  same for 12 and 16. The climb was kept because it is cheap, not because
  it helped.
- P = 47: 27 draws read 14 and held 300,000 trials ([[376,98,14]]). Each
  would beat codes/376-98-8.json on d alone, so each needs the
  matched-depth pair audit of research/audits/leader_audit.py before it
  is filed; they stay in local staging output and are not part of this PR.
- P = 53 and 67 reached 14 and 16 but never 16 and 18, matching the
  catalogue's girth-6 values at nearby lifts; they beat nothing the board
  does not already hold at better n and were not filed.
- No exact distance search was run; verify/certify.py is out of its band
  at k = 90.

## Reproduction

P = 43. H_X[i*P + r, l*P + ((r - E_x[i][l]) mod P)] = 1 and the same for H_Z
with E_z (i in 0..2, l in 0..7, r in 0..42):

E_x = [[4, 2, 15, 25, 4, 24, 7, 21], [11, 20, 11, 8, 34, 13, 26, 33], [37, 42, 31, 24, 8, 12, 37, 29]]

E_z = [[38, 41, 32, 12, 38, 11, 24, 17], [42, 21, 10, 38, 22, 0, 25, 34], [38, 25, 38, 30, 9, 18, 1, 12]]

Each pair (a, b) of M[(i - j) mod 3] satisfies
E_x[i][a] - E_x[i][b] - E_z[j][a] + E_z[j][b] = 0 mod 43, which gives
H_X H_Z^T = 0; neither array has a vanishing 4- or 6-cycle sum, so both
Tanner graphs have girth 8; rank H_X = rank H_Z = 127, so k = 344 - 254 = 90.
The same arrays are printed in the entry's provenance.construction.
