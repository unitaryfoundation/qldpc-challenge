# [[282,96,6]] — Construction I, N=47

## Direction & hypothesis

Aimed at the CSS weight-6 / unrestricted cell using the constructions of arXiv:2610.19451 (Nourozi, Smarandache, Mitchell). Construction I is a 2x6 circulant family with analytic distance 6 and girth 12; Construction II is a mixed circulant-affine 2-block family with larger certified distances.

## What was searched

The paper's fixed constructions were rebuilt from the construction described in arXiv:2610.19451 and screened against the board Pareto frontier. The undominated members listed above were submitted.

## Evidence trail

Construction I carries the paper's analytic witnesses (Eq. (12) and its isomorphism mirror), so d <= 6 is a witnessed upper bound. Construction II (q=6) carries a witness found by a fast-RIS ladder; the paper certifies d = 20 by bounded enumeration but does not publish the operators.

## Dead ends

Construction I with N = 63 and Construction II with q = 2, 3, 4 pass validation but are dominated by existing board entries and were not submitted.
