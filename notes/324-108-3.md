## What was searched

This sweep (seed 703) drew 400,020 codes in a parallel generate-and-rank prefilter; 865 had k >= 18 and a connected Tanner graph (research/kit/prescreen.block_structure). Those went through research/kit/search.screen with the gf2_fast backend at 400 trials; 1 advanced the board frontier at screen depth and 1 were taken to 20,000 / 200,000 / 2,000,000 fast trials (research/kit/surrogate.distance_rand_witness, pair depth 8), dropping a candidate as soon as its running bound stopped advancing; 1 survived that and was staged.
