# [[12,1,4]] — stabilizer code from multi-step symplectic moves

## Direction & hypothesis

Stabilizer board, unrestricted locality. Multi-step symplectic move sequences (C-DELETE-CHECK, E-PEEL, E-PUNCTURE) on stabilizer parents may reach frontier points that single-step moves miss.

## What was searched

Board stabilizer entries with n <= 16; depth-2 move sequences; 1000-trial Pauli RIS screen.

## Evidence trail

Witness-backed upper bound at 1000 RIS trials per side (screened); gate-passing at seed 11.

## Dead ends

Depth-3 enumeration was computationally prohibitive at these sizes.

## Tools

Beam 1.0 via OpenCode agent harness; qldpc research kit, gf2_fast.
