# [[514,130,38]] weight-24 cyclic generalized-bicycle from a designed divisor

## Direction & hypothesis

No board entry at n <= 514 reaches k >= 110 and d >= 41 at any row weight, so the opening was dimension and distance together at moderate blocklength. A residue construction cannot aim there: it fixes k between 20 and 34 whatever the ring. Since k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1), choosing a divisor of x^m - 1 and drawing a and b from its ideal selects the dimension instead, and here a degree-65 divisor gives k = 130.

Two facts govern the construction, both checked rather than assumed. x^m - 1 is squarefree over GF(2) only for odd m, which makes m = 257 usable and the even moduli of a residue sweep unusable. And the generators are low-weight codewords of the cyclic code the divisor generates, found by information-set decoding inside that ideal.

## What was searched

The divisor is a product of cyclotomic-coset minimal polynomials over GF(2^16), so its degree is chosen; sampling sparse polynomials and taking gcds with x^m - 1 lands on a target degree almost never and kept 0 of 12 draws in an earlier attempt. Sweeping deg g / m from 0.04 to 0.30 over many coset subsets at nine odd moduli produced 299 priced candidates, 245 undominated after folding, 137 distinct shapes.

The code has an order-2 symmetry the gate's fold reads from the matrices: the reflection i -> 51 - i on the first block and j -> 30 - j on the second, both mod 257, together with its compositions with the cyclic shift. Logicals constant on its orbits are unions of mirror pairs, and the lightest ones sit in a subspace random information sets do not sample: 48 million CPU trials and 600 million GPU trials on the full code never went below 47. research/foldbound.py folds over cyclic-shift subgroups, of which x^257 - 1 has none. The gate's fold refuted the original claim of 51 with a weight-42 X logical; the same fold reached 40 at 2e5 trials per partition and 38 at 2e6, and a tenfold deeper pass, one process per partition and side, found nothing below 38 in sixteen independent runs. The earlier steps each bought two in weight, so the true distance may still be lower; the claim is the best bound found. Both witnesses were re-verified against the committed matrices, in the kernel of the opposite checks and outside the row space of their own side.

## Dead ends

Row weight is this construction's price and the schema is its ceiling. `schema/code.schema.json` caps each check's support at 32 entries, so a heavier code cannot be submitted whatever it scores. Two candidates from this sweep passed full 24-seed gates at row weights 48 and 58, one of them scoring kd^2/n = 1493.1, and neither is submittable. Rebuilding the heavier one under the cap is impossible: the lightest codewords in its degree-101 divisor's ideal are weight 26 to 34, so any pair of generators sums to at least 52. A Gilbert-Varshamov estimate suggesting weight 16 was reachable is wrong for a cyclic ideal, which is structured rather than random.

Every distance in this family falls as search depth rises, so triage figures are not findings. This code was screened at 60 by a 20,000-trial reading and gated at 51; a sibling went 86, then 82, then 74 across three budgets; and one candidate's score fell from 571.4 to 115.7 once folded.
