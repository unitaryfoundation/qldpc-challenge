## Original note, with the file paths updated

# [[684,18,106]] — Kasai affine 2BGA interior-gap extension, kd^2/n 295.7 at n=684

## Direction & hypothesis

Advance the unrestricted weight-9plus cell's frontier at n=684 with
two-block group-algebra codes over the affine group Aff(F_19): our merged
[[684,14,107]] (kd^2/n 234.3) held the cell with no competitor at k>=14,
and the interior k=16-26 band was empty at high distance. The hypothesis:
the affine-2BGA family carries interior points strictly dominating the
k=12-14 anchors once the exponent vectors are searched past the seed
weight-8 corner.

## What was searched

Lane kasai-interior-1120: two 4,000-draw sweeps at q in {17,19} (weight-8
exponent seeds, check weight 16) screened at 1,000 fast-backend RIS trials
(seeds 42001/42002, 36 survivors), then a 6,000-draw q19 batch (seed 42004,
24 survivors). Screens read d=124 for this draw (42004-4823, k=26) —
the screen's high-efficiency readings proved unreliable on three of the
batch's heads (two later measured at true d<=22 and <=18), so every
survivor went through a multi-budget ladder before any claim.

## Evidence trail

a fresh seed, numpy and gf2_fast instruments, reading the lightest logical
found:

The rung-26 search (2M trials, pd12, seed 93644) found a weight-106
Z-side logical; the witness was recovered by deterministic seed replay and
verified (commutes with H_X, outside the H_Z row space). The claim is the
witness-backed upper bound d <= 106, never exact: the RIS estimator's
floor over 26 deep rungs bounces in the 106-114 band, and the 106 sighting
is the lightest witnessed reading. The submitted document re-measures both
sides through `cli/qldpc.py submit` (accelerator 2M trials per side), which
tightened the opposite side to 112 and kept d = min(112, 106) = 106.
