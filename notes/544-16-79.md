# [[544,16,79]] — Kasai affine 2BGA interior-gap extension, kd^2/n 183.6 at n=544

## Direction & hypothesis

Extend the n=544 band's frontier on the k axis with two-block
group-algebra codes over the affine group Aff(F_17): our merged
[[544,20,76]] (kd^2/n 212.4) held the band at k=20, and no board entry
at n<=544 carried k>=16 with d>=77. The hypothesis: the affine-2BGA
family carries interior points that trade k against d against the
k=20 anchor — a k=16 point at d>=77 is dominated by nothing.
