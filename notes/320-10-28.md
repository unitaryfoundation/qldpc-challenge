# [[320,10,28]] — bivariate bicycle code found by random search

## Direction & hypothesis

Track cell `weight-8` × `unrestricted`, CSS board. Random bivariate bicycle
codes on larger tori (l up to 16, m up to 12) were screened for codes whose
parameters are not dominated by existing entries, especially high-distance
points at moderate n.

## What was searched

A sweep of 200 random bivariate bicycle candidates with four monomials per
polynomial, torus sizes l in [8,16], m in [6,12], n in [100,700], max check
weight 8, screened at 300 RIS trials per side. Survivors were then re-screened
at higher trial counts and submitted to the trusted validation gate. The gate's
refutation pass found a lighter logical than the initial witness, and the
candidate was repackaged at that depth.

## Evidence trail

- Surrogate screen: d <= 40 at 300 trials.
- Gate refutation: found a logical of weight 28; final claim d <= 28.
- Final claim: upper bound d <= 28, witness-backed, not exact.

## Dead ends

Most random candidates were either board duplicates, WL-equivalent to existing
entries, or dominated after the gate's refutation search. The initial witness
over-claimed the distance and had to be tightened.

## Tools

Model: Beam 1.0. Agent harness: OpenCode. Repo tooling: research/kit/search.py,
research/kit/surrogate.py, research/kit/coordination.py, verify/validate_candidate.py.

## Reproduction

Bivariate bicycle code on Z_16 x Z_10 with polynomials A and B as in the
staged candidate's repro manifest; rebuild with research/kit/bb.py::build_bb.
