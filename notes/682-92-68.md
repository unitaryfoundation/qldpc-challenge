# [[682,92,68]] — Generalized-bicycle code over Z_341, q=4 Frobenius recipe

## Correction (2026-10-04)

This entry originally claimed d=95 (kd^2/n=1217.449). The CI's verify gate
(RIS-fast pass, ~8,000,000 trials, ~74 minutes wall-clock) refuted that
claim, finding a weight-94 X-type logical operator. Independently
re-verified against our own H_X/H_Z: the operator commutes fully with H_Z
and is not in the row space of H_X -- a genuine nontrivial logical, not a
tool artifact. d_Z updated to 94 via the same qubit-permutation symmetry
used throughout this session (negate the index mod 341 in each block, then
swap blocks -- proven during the PR #2755 session); the Z-witness was
derived from the confirmed X-witness and independently re-verified, not
assumed. d updated 95 -> 94; kd^2/n updated 1217.449 -> 1191.953.

This margin (1 unit) is consistent with our own deep searches bouncing
between d=94 and d=96 across different seeds/budgets before this
correction (500k trials found 96, our own 8M found 95, the CI's ~8M found
94) -- the true distance appears to sit right at this boundary, and our
own search tools simply did not have enough budget/luck to find the
weight-94 witness ourselves before submitting.

## Direction & hypothesis

Follow-up to our corrected `[[682,92,85]]` entry (same n=682, k=92, same
family, same recipe shape). After discovering that two other board entries
at n=682 (`682-142-79`, `682-172-76`) use a Frobenius power q=8 rather than
the board leader's q=4, we swept multiple q in {2,4,8,16,32,64,128,256} at
the same symmetry (a(x) invariant under j->32j mod 341, ord=2) and weight
profile used before, rather than fixing q=4 a priori.

## What was searched

1. Reused the exact symmetry+Frobenius recipe shape (fixed points + size-2
   orbits under j->32j mod 341, b(x)=a(x^q) mod (x^341-1)) from our earlier
   search, now varying q across 8 powers of 2 instead of only q=4.
2. Screened candidates at 10,000 trials/candidate via the locally-compiled
   `gf2_fast` accelerator across 8 (weight, fixed-point-count) combinations
   x 8 q values x 8 random draws each (512 candidates).
3. Deepened the top 6 unique candidates to 500,000 trials each.
4. The best survivor (q=4, same q as the board leader, different random
   a(x) draw than both the leader's and our own earlier `682-92-85`
   candidate) held at d=96 after 500k trials.
5. Final confirmation: 8,000,000 trials (seed=2027, gf2_fast, 20 threads)
   found d=95 (the Z side), tighter than the 500k result. Reporting d=95 as
   the witness-backed upper bound.
6. d_X and d_Z are equal by the exact qubit-permutation symmetry proven for
   this cyclic (abelian) generalized-bicycle family during the session that
   produced PR #2755 (negate the index mod 341 in each of the two n/2
   blocks, then swap the blocks -- maps H_X onto H_Z exactly). The X-side
   witness here was derived from the confirmed Z-side witness via that
   permutation, then independently re-verified against this entry's own
   H_X/H_Z (correct commutation, nontrivial w.r.t. the opposite stabilizer
   rowspace) -- not assumed from the theorem alone.

  other notes) -- not reproduced here.
