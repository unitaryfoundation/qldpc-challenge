# [[384,96,15]] — GF(4) pair-partition code at even lift P = 24

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: `weight-9plus` x `unrestricted` (CSS), at rate `k/n = 1/4` (`n = 16P`, `k = 4P`, `w = 10`).
Inspired by arXiv:2610.06820 (*Designing Group-Valued Codes with Full Regular Low-Weight Bases*) and the $\mathbb{F}_4$ pair-partition (PP) family (arXiv:2609.35601), we explored even lifts `P` solved over the ring $\mathbb{Z}_P$ via unit-pivot nullspace rather than restricting to odd prime lifts. At `n = 384, k = 96, w = 10`, any distance `d >= 9` advances the `weight-9plus` x `unrestricted` frontier.

## What was searched

25 random draws from the 9-dimensional unit-pivot nullspace of the 36-equation `M_ALT` pair-partition overlap system over $\mathbb{Z}_{24}$ (with `q = (5, 7, 4, 6, 2, 0, 3, 1)` and the published $\mathbb{F}_4$ coefficient arrays `EPS`/`DELTA`), screened at 2,000 `gf2_fast` RIS trials per draw (`seed = 42`).

## Evidence trail

- Screen (2,000 `gf2_fast` trials): best candidate at `t = 20` reached `d = 15`.
- Confirmation (50,000 `gf2_fast` trials, `pair_depth = 16`, `threads = 8`): `d <= 15` (`d_X <= 15`, `d_Z <= 15`).
- Gate (`verify/validate_candidate.py`): `passed: true`, `board_advancing: true`, `exact_duplicate_of: null`, `wl_equivalent_of: null`, no lighter logical found in 8,000 RIS refutation trials.
- Final claim: witness-backed `upper_bound`, `d <= 15`.

## Dead ends

- Separable exponent ansatz (`E[i,l] = p_i + u_l`) over $\mathbb{F}_4[C_P]$ forces 4-cycles in every $2 \times 2$ block and collapsed to `d = 2`.
- Even lift `P = 18` (`[[288, 72, d]]`) confirmed at `d = 12` and was dominated by `codes/280-74-12.json`.
