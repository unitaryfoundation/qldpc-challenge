# \[\[360,12,29]] CLP code, check weight 9

Primary reference: [arXiv:2610.12782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12782), *Coset Lifted Product Codes*,
Rajpurohit, Lalitha, Garani, Table II. Data and scripts: https://github.com/rajendrarajpurohit/CLP\_QLDPC.

## Direction and hypothesis

Part of the paper's search over 14,843 pairs (G,H), 30 <= m <= 100, at check weights 7 to 10, for codes
with kd^2/n above the published bar at each weight. This code is one of the paper's Table II candidates,
whose distances are upper bounds only; the paper's integer program certification does not reach d > 20.

## This code

G = SmallGroup(270,25), H = <f2*f3*f4> = C3, m = 90, n = 4m, check weight 9. Protographs a and B in the entry's construction field.

## Evidence trail

\- QDistRnd, 10^6 rounds: d <= 31.

\- Trusted gate (this PR): weight-30 logical by orbit-fold, then weight-29 X-type logical by RIS-fast, seed 940231952, carried as the witness.

\- Local verify/gate\_changed.py --seed 20261106, 8M fast trials: no logical lighter than 29.

\- The next arXiv version adopts d <= 29.

## Dead ends

In the paper's screen, six of seven candidates with bounds at or above 25 lost weight between 2 x 10^5 and
10^6 rounds. Non square layouts and 3x3 shapes with single element entries were closed as negative results.

## Tools

GAP 4.15.1 with SmallGroups and QDistRnd, the construction and driver scripts of the repository above,
no LLM in the search loop. Witness and verification: this repository's qldpc submit with --trials 1000000.

## Reproduction

gap/rebuild.g in the repository above, with G, H, a, B as given, rebuilds HX and HZ.

## Update after CI

The gate's orbit-fold search found a weight 30 logical. The claim is lowered to d <= 30 with that witness; the paper's Table II figure of 31 will be corrected in the next arXiv version.
