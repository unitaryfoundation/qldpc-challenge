# [[864,20,40]] CLP code, check weight 6

Primary reference: [arXiv:2610.12782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12782), *Coset Lifted Product Codes*,
Rajpurohit, Lalitha, Garani. This parameter set is not in the current version of the paper; it comes from
the same search extended to longer codes and will appear in the updated version, with the data in
https://github.com/rajendrarajpurohit/CLP_QLDPC (folder large_m/864_20_40).

## Direction and hypothesis

The paper's search covered 120 <= n <= 400 (m = [G:H] from 30 to 100). The question was whether the same
construction scales: does the 1x1 over 2x2 layout at check weight 6 keep its distance growing when m goes to
120 to 250, that is n from 480 to 1000, and where do such codes sit on this board's weight-6 frontier.

## What was searched

25,240 pairs (G,H), H cyclic of order 2 to 9, core free, [G:N_G(H)] in {2,3,4}, [N_G(H):H] >= 4, from
SmallGroups of order 240 to 2000 (orders with more than 2000 groups skipped). 1,500 random draws per pair of
a with |a| = 3 and B with entry weights 2 1 / 1 2 or 1 2 / 2 1, then 20,000 draws per pair on the 6,836
pairs with 560 <= n <= 700 whose best k was at least 16, logging any draw whose (n, k, d) was not Pareto
dominated by the weight-6 cell of this board on 24 Oct 2026. Screening ladder: 50 and 500 QDistRnd rounds
with an early abort at the required distance, then 20,000, then 200,000 on both sides.

## This code

G = SmallGroup(648,720), H = <f5*f6*f7> = C3, m = 216. a = 1 + f1*f3*f4*f5*f6*f7 + f1*f2*f3^2*f5*f7;  B = [[1, 1 + f1*f3^2*f4*f5^2*f7], [1 + f1*f3^2*f4*f5*f7, f1*f3^2*f4*f5^2]].

## Evidence trail

| stage | rounds / trials | result |
|---|---|---|
| search log | 200,000 QDistRnd per side | d <= 40 |
| rescreen | 1,000,000 QDistRnd per side | d <= 40 |
| this tool | 500,000 RIS trials per side | X <= 40, Z <= 40 |

The witnesses in the entry are the ones this tool found; each was checked by the verifier. Not certified
exact; the paper's integer program certification does not reach these distances.

## Dead ends

Shapes with |a| = 2 or |a| = 4 (B entry weights up to 2 2 / 2 2) raise k to 24 and far beyond at these
lengths but over 9,560 pairs never paired that k with a distance above the frontier for its k. Bounds from
the 200,000 round stage overestimate by 2 to 16 at n near 1000; only the 10^6 figures are reported.

## Tools

GAP 4.15.1 with SmallGroups and QDistRnd, the construction and driver scripts of the repository above
(large_m/search), no LLM in the search loop. Witness and verification: this repository's qldpc submit with
--trials 500000.

## Reproduction

gap/rebuild.g in the repository above, with G, H, a, B as given, rebuilds HX and HZ; verify.py there checks
the structure and this board's verifier checks the witnesses.

## Update after CI

The gate of PR #4477 (seed 1516409015, orbit-fold search) found a weight 36 logical. The claim is lowered to d <= 36 and that witness is carried in the entry. Our 10^6 round QDistRnd screen and the 500,000 trial RIS search had both stopped at 40.
