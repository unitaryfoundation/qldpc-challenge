# [[392,8,15]] — planar BB (local-2d-bilayer weight-6)

## Direction & hypothesis

Open-boundary planar bivariate-bicycle (Liang-Eberhardt-Chen / Liang-Yang-Iosue-Chen, arXiv:2504.08887) on a 14x14 grid; f = x + x^2 + y^2, g = 1 + x^2 y + x^2 y^2; built with the directional anyon-condensation truncation, corner-resolved, bilayer layout.

Origin: submission.
Novelty status: known_parameters.

References: arXiv:2504.08887, arXiv:2410.11942.

LITERATURE NOVELTY: NOT NOVEL. This is the L=14 member of the flagship planar BB family f=x+x^2+y^2, g=1+x^2 y+x^2 y^2 from Liang, Eberhardt & Chen, 'Planar quantum LDPC codes with open boundaries' (arXiv:2504.08887), Eq. 9 / Table V. The paper already publishes this exact (f,g) family at L=6,8,10,12 ([[72,8,4]],[[128,8,6]],[[200,8,9]],[[288,8,12]]); [[392,8,15]] is a mechanical lattice-size extension, not a new construction. It is also the same family as the board's own local-2d-bilayer weight-6 bar-holder [[288,8,12]] (L=12).
