# [[52,26,2]] — PP-SCALE explicit extension of a pair-partition base, code-calculus v0.3.1rc

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: weight-4 / unrestricted, aiming to extend the high-rate
(k/n = 0.5) d ≤ 2 frontier at n = 52. The lead is the v0.3.1rc release's
second new constructor: `PP-SCALE`, promoted to rung 3 (`transition-defined`)
in this release — the Kasai 2026 explicit-extension scaling of pair-partition
codes through a Smith-normal-form parameter bundle, evaluated at a target
lift size P. The 2026-11-22 yield fieldnote had found 0/8 PP-SCALE
derivations connected on block-diagonal bases; the open question was whether
the SNF-bundle route, evaluated at untried sizes, yields connected codes the
board does not already hold.

## What was searched

A deterministic SNF-bundle grid: base at P0 = 3 (root r = 2 of order 2,
reproducing slope patterns {1, −1} through Q rows in {0, 1}), shift patterns
from the five classic block-mixing patterns, and target lift sizes
P ∈ {7, 11, 13} with roots_r = [P − 1] — 960 parameter tuples, of which 192
derived and 144 had a one-component Tanner graph. The first 20 connected
derivations were packaged and gated: 6 passed (all at P = 13) and 14 were
refused locally as exact duplicates — the unit-slope P = 7 and P = 11
members coincide with matrices the board or open PRs already carry. This
note's code is the canonical `all0-alternating` member at P = 13.

## Evidence trail

- Derivation: the SNF bundle reproduces the base code at P0 = 3
  (the consumer's reproduction check) and evaluates cleanly at P = 13
  (12² = 1 mod 13); the paired relative-affine orthogonality premise holds.
- Connectivity: one Tanner component (the gate's `block_structure` check).
- Distance: the verifier's witness search found weight-2 logicals on both
  sides at 8,000 RIS trials per side; the submitted claim is d ≤ 2
  (`upper_bound`). No lower endpoint is claimed.
- Gate: `passed: true`, `board_advancing: true`, `advances_by: [d, k, n]`,
  `dominated_by: []`, cell weight-4/unrestricted.
- Note: at Q = 0 the bundle evaluates to the plain CPM-PP pattern at p = 13,
  so this entry is the p = 13 member of the pair-partition family reached
  through the PP-SCALE transition; the parameters n = 52, k = 26, w = 4.

## Dead ends

- All-(−1)-slope bundles (Q rows = 1): 12/12 staged derivations were exact
  duplicates — negating every column slope is a position reflection, so the
  matrices coincide with the unit-slope grid the board already holds
  ([[28,14,2]] ×2, [[44,22,2]] ×4, [[52,26,2]] ×2 sources).
- Uniform-slope P = 7 and P = 11 members: exact duplicates of existing
  entries, refused locally before spending a witness search.
- Identical X/Z shift patterns: disconnected (p 4-qubit blocks), refused at
  staging.

target pattern (P = 13, L = 4, unit slopes, X shifts (0,0,0,0), Z shifts
(0,1,0,1)) and writes `52-26-2-b.npz`; run it, then

```
./qldpc submit 52-26-2-b.npz --authors @mathysrennela
```
