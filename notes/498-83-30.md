# [[498,83,30]] — mirror fold (symplectic halving) of a [[996,166]] pair-partition CPM code

## Direction & hypothesis

Fold a strong X<->Z symmetric CSS code along its mirror to get a non-CSS stabilizer code with half the qubits and half the logical qubits. Hypothesis: the fold keeps most of the parent's distance (the parent's light logicals rarely contain many mirror pairs), so k d^2/n stays high. Score of this entry: 150.0. Derived, not a new construction.

**Derived from:** an unpublished [[996,166]] CSS code of ours (5 x 12 pair-partition CPM template of Okada and Kasai, arXiv:2607.14091, prime lift P = 83, check weight 8; same 5 x 12 template and column pairing as our board entry codes/996-166-34.json, with different circulant exponents; lightest logical found 36, upper bound), by @MicheldeMessieres. The parent's check matrices are not needed to use this entry (the generators below are complete); the parent is rebuilt by symplectic doubling H'_X = (A|B), H'_Z = (B|A), which is the parent with its qubits indexed by the pairs {q_i, tau(q_i)}.
**Fold technique (not ours):** symplectic halving and the CPM halving map of arXiv:2609.30069 (Prop. 4; board fieldnote fieldnotes/2026-09-28-symplectic-doubling-and-halving.md, @MathysRennela); mirror codes of Khesin and Lu (arXiv:2603.05496); the reverse-fold shape of @MinboGaoTHU's codes/485-97-25.json, and his parent-symmetry witness transfer (as in his distance revision of @jacksongquera's codes/480-12-34.json), which our shift-scored search also uses.
**Our contribution:** the parent code, the mirror choice, and the distance search below.

## What was searched

- Mirrors: all maps (l,t) -> (sigma(l), u t + b_l) sending X-check rows to Z-check rows; exactly one fixed-point-free involution exists (u = -1), up to conjugation by the cyclic shift.
- Distance: fold logicals correspond to parent logicals v (in ker H_X, outside row H_Z) with Pauli weight |v union tau(v)|/2, so every parent logical found was scored under all 83 cyclic shifts. Information sets were drawn pair by pair (a Y enters or leaves as one unit).

## Dead ends

- A Stern search directly on the fold, without the parent's shift symmetry, is several times weaker at this size (33 after 5 min on codes/485-97-25.json).
- Pure-type logicals (all X, all Z or all Y) are far heavier than d on these folds.
