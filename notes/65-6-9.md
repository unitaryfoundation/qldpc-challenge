# [[65,6,9]] one-qubit peel of [[66,6,10]] 2BGA, 2D-local bilayer

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: CSS, weight-8 x local-2d-bilayer. Campaign
`research/campaigns/cc-2dlocal-deep-verify-gpu-2026-11-15/campaign.json`
(issue #5812) asks whether 2D-local code-calculus children keep their distance
at 300M trials or were inflated by shallow screening. A child inherits its
parent's bilayer layout, so if its distance holds it is a 2D-local frontier
extension next to the parent.

## What was searched

671 2D-local CSS parents from `codes/` (n <= 99, check weight <= 8). Each was
expanded with the code-calculus moves C-DELETE-CHECK, E-PEEL, E-PUNCTURE and
sector swap, up to 3 steps at beam 3, and every connected child was tightened
with the `verify/gf2_fast.cpp` accelerator at 2,000,000 trials (seeds 11 and
12, pair depth 32). 5,990 children were screened; 1,039 were non-duplicate at
d >= 3, 996 of those were dominated on the board, and 35 remained after a
within-batch Pareto filter. All 35 went to the GPU.

## Evidence trail

Confirmation ladder for the submitted code, each rung a fresh seed unless
marked otherwise, reading the lightest logical found:

| budget (trials/side) | lightest logical |
| ---: | ---: |
| 2,000,000 (gf2_fast screen, seeds 11 and 12, lightest over both) | 9 |
| 300,000,000 (verify/ris_gpu.py, seeds 51 and 52, lightest over both) | 9 |
| 1,000,000 (gf2_fast, seed 9201) | 9 |
| 1,000,000 (gf2_fast, seed 9202) | 9 |

4 fresh-seed rung(s) agree at the best bound, the deepest of them at
300,000,000 trials per side. The submitted witness reproduces it: X: d <= 10
(upper_bound), Z: d <= 9 (upper_bound).

Every rung used pair depth 32. The sides read d_X <= 10 and d_Z <= 9. None of
the 35 GPU-measured children read lower at 300M trials than at 2M.
`verify/gate_changed.py` was also replayed on this file as a new entry against
the board at e289cf5 (seed 1016508323): one 32,800-trial Python RIS seed and
the 4,920,000-trial fast pass found nothing lighter than 9; the orbit-fold
pass found no symmetry to fold. The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, not
an exact distance. The later fresh-seed rungs, the gate replay and the
packaging were done after the search, in a separate Claude Code session
(Claude Fable 5.2).

## Dead ends

- Three of the 35 GPU-measured children had the WL signature of an existing
  entry (`codes/11-3-3-d.json`, `codes/15-3-3.json`, `codes/63-35-3.json`) and
  were dropped.
- The 35 files are 24 distinct codes by WL signature; of the 17 parameter
  points left after the drop, 13 have d = 3.

## Tools

Model: Gemini 4 Carbon (Antigravity). Repo tooling: `verify/gf2_fast.cpp`,
`verify/ris_gpu.py`, `verify/gate_changed.py`. The local verdict is
`verify/validate_candidate.py`; the bundle was assembled by
`research/kit/promote.py`. Compute: one Brev node with 4x A100 80GB and 48
vCPUs; 48.6 CPU-hours for the campaign screen and 0.14 GPU-hours for this
code's 300M rung. The code-calculus moves come from the external code-calculus
library (development snapshot of 2026-11-16).

## Reproduction

Take `codes/66-6-10.json`. Delete qubit 26 (0-indexed) from every check, drop
X checks 26, 29, 31, 32 and Z checks 29, 31, 32 (0-indexed rows of the
parent), and renumber the remaining 65 qubits in order. The layout is the
parent's coordinates with the deleted qubit removed. This is the code-calculus
E-PEEL chain the campaign recorded (E-PEEL); the qubit and check indices were
recovered afterwards by comparing the child with the parent, the match is
unique, and rebuilding from this recipe reproduces the committed checks and
coordinates.
