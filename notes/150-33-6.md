# [[150,33,6]] — lifted product over a group of order 30, check weight 7

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the unrestricted x weight-8 cell at n in 111..220 with check weight 7. Only board entries with
check weight <= 7 can dominate a weight-7 code; at the time of the search no such entry had
n' <= 150, k' >= 33 and d' >= 6, so d = 6 was enough here. The gate's novelty check
reports that it advances the frontier (k, n, w);
board-relative novelty from verify/validate_candidate.py, literature novelty unverified.

Hypothesis: a lifted product of two one-row bases over a non-abelian group, A = [a1, a2] and B = [b1, b2] with entries of weight 2 and 3, has n = 5|G| and k >= |G|; because left and right supports need not commute, the weight-2 entries need not create the short cycles an abelian group forces, so d can reach 6 at rate above 1/5. Mixing a weight-3 entry into one base lifts the check weight to 7 and buys extra k over the weight-6 shape.
