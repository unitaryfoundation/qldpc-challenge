# [[504,26,16]] — weight-6 2BGA code on the metacyclic group C_42 x|_11 C_6

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: unrestricted x weight-6 (check weight 6), n band 401 to 600. The weight-6 frontier there is thin at k >= 16: below n = 576 the entries are low-rate (k <= 16, e.g. [[434,16,22]], [[510,16,26]]) or high-rate at small distance ([[520,44,8]] at weight 5, [[540,112,8]]), with only a few mid-rate points such as [[378,26,10]] and [[480,24,18]] between them, and [[576,32,20]] the first mid-rate entry above them in n. The hypothesis: two-block group-algebra codes on non-abelian groups reach k well above what cyclic weight-6 codes give at these lengths without the distance collapse of the high-rate entries. At (n, k) = (504, 26) the smallest distance that is not dominated by a board entry with n' <= n, k' >= k, w' <= 6 was 11 when this was gated.

## What was searched

- Family: two-block group-algebra codes H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] on the non-abelian metacyclic groups C_p x|_r C_q (r^q = 1 mod p, r != 1) of order 201 to 300 (n = 402 to 600); supports a, b of 3 or 4 elements containing the identity (a -> ga and b -> bg give equivalent codes), drawn at random; k computed exactly in the worker and only k >= 14 screened (about 2 percent of draws).
- Funnel as for the cyclic sweep: 600 gf2_fast RIS trials (1 thread), promotion to 20,000 and 200,000 trials only while the reading clears the frontier threshold for (n, k, w), then 1,000,000 and 20,000,000 fresh-seed trials and the ladder. A second, weight-6-only run (3+3 supports, k >= 16) used the same funnel.

## Dead ends

- Screening inflation at n >= 500 is the dominant trap. Fresh-seed 1,000,000-trial re-measures of the sweep's
  200,000-trial survivors: a [[584,26]] weight-8 cyclic pair read 61 at 200,000 and 34 at 1,000,000; a [[576,20]]
  metacyclic 2BGA 64 then 34; a [[584,22]] cyclic pair 63 then 44; a [[600,20]] metacyclic 2BGA 62 then 44. Even
  1,000,000 trials is far from settled: a [[534,24]] cyclic pair read 46 at 1,000,000 on two independent seeds and
  33 at 20,000,000, and a [[510,26]] pair read 41, 36 and then 32 at 20,000,000. A 20,000,000-trial descent was
  therefore run before anything was staged, and every staged claim was laddered on 3 fresh seeds (20,000,000
  trials each for claims d >= 30, 10,000,000 each below that).
- Degenerate divisors (cyclic sweep). A divisor g that is a multiple of x^m' + 1 for a proper m' | m has many
  weight-4 multiples of the form (1 + x^m')(1 + x^i), and every such pair had d <= 7 at 600 trials; these divisors
  were excluded from the sweep.
- Weight-(3,3) cyclic pairs, enumerated exhaustively for every m in [201, 300]: no pair with k >= 18 cleared the
  weight-6 frontier threshold, so the cyclic weight-6 family does not reach the k 20 to 60 region of that cell.
- Random 2BGA on metacyclic groups of order 201 to 300: k = 0 in 49 percent of 400,000 draws and k >= 14 in about
  2 percent; the weight-8 metacyclic survivors that read 39 to 74 at 200,000 trials were cut sharply by every deeper
  pass (first item).

## Reproduction

Construction (also in provenance.construction): Two-block group algebra (2BGA) code over the non-abelian metacyclic group C_42 semidirect_11 C_6 of order 252 (research/kit/group_algebra.py metacyclic(42, 6, 11) and build_2bga): element (i, j) = x^i y^j at index i*6 + j, product (i1, j1)(i2, j2) = (i1 + 11^j1 * i2 mod 42, j1 + j2 mod 6); H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] with L, R the left and right regular representations; a = [0, 133, 227], b = [0, 39, 243] (element indices). n = 2|G| = 504, k = 26, check weight 6 = 3+3.
