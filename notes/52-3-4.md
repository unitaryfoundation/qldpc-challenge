# [[52,3,4]] even-d multiband dense-packed surface code (d=4, rows=3, m=2, pitch=2)

## Direction & hypothesis

Target the `local-2d-single / weight-4` track cell at `d = 4` by resolving
Open Problem 4 of `fieldnotes/2026-09-01-multiband-dense-packing-method.md`
(even-`d` behaviour of the multiband dense-packed surface-code family in
`research/multiband_surface.py`). For even `d`, odd-band horizontal origins
`x0 = d + 1` sit on the opposite parity sublattice; setting `rows = 3` and
even `pitch = d - 2` sandwiches the staggered `r = 1` bridge band between two
even bands (`r = 0, 2`), restoring CSS commutation, single-component Tanner
connectivity, check weight 4, single-layer locality (`r = sqrt(2)`), and
closed-form parameters `n = (2d^2 - d)m - d`, `k = 2m - 1`.

## What was searched

Enumerated even-`d` (`d in {4, 6, 8}`) configurations in
`research/multiband_surface.py` over `rows in {2..9}`, `m in {2..33}`, and
even `pitch in {2..2d}`, computing exact GF(2) rank `k`, CSS commutation, and
Tanner connectivity, then screening undominated points with
`research/kit/surrogate.py` and `verify/sat_certify.py`. At `d = 4, rows = 3,
m = 2, pitch = 2`, the construction gives `[[52,3,4]]` with max check weight 4
and single-layer 2D coordinates.

## Dead ends

- Odd pitches (`pitch = d - 1 = 3`) at `d = 4` shift band origins across
  parity sublattices and fail CSS commutation (`css = False`).
- Even pitches `pitch >= d` (`pitch = 4` at `d = 4, rows = 3`) unlock larger
  `k` but introduce weight-3 boundary logicals.
- Odd band counts `rows in {5, 7, 9}` at `d = 4, pitch = 2` preserve `d <= 4`
  but hold `k = 2m - 1` fixed while increasing `n`.
