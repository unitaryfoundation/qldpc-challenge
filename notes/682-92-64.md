# [[682,92,64]] — Generalized-bicycle code via champion's exact Frobenius-pairing recipe

## Tightening (2026-10-03, same day as the correction below)

This cyclic (abelian, t=1) generalized-bicycle construction has an exact
qubit permutation symmetry: negate the index mod 341 within each of the
two n/2 blocks, then swap the two blocks. This maps H_X onto H_Z exactly
(verified by direct computation: H_X's columns reindexed by this
permutation equal H_Z as a set of check rows). Consequence: d_X and d_Z
are mathematically forced to be equal for this entire family (any
cyclic/abelian 2-block generalized-bicycle code) -- no search needed to
show it. Applying this permutation to the already-verified weight-85
X-type witness produces a weight-85 Z-type witness (independently
verified: commutes with H_X, increases rank of H_Z when appended -- a
genuine nontrivial logical, not a derivation artifact). d_Z updated from
the stale 95 (an un-re-deepened carryover from the original,
since-refuted submission) to 85, now matching d_X exactly. `d` (=85) and
`kd^2/n` (=974.633) are unchanged -- both sides were already <=85, this
only replaces a loose bound with the exact matching one.

## Correction (2026-10-03)

operator, not a tool artifact. A local greedy reduction (XOR against every

the CI's accelerated "fast pass" exploits; more trials alone did not close

not independently re-deepened after this correction. Before pushing this
correction we ran the CI's own gate (`verify/gate_changed.py`) locally with
3 independent random seeds against the corrected d=85 claim -- none found
anything lighter, per our own post-incident protocol (never submit or
re-submit without reproducing the actual CI gate first, not just our own
ad-hoc `gf2_fast` calls).

The rest of this note (below) describes the original, un-refuted search
that found the (a,b) pair -- the construction and the a_sup/b_sup values

## Direction & hypothesis

Targeted the `generalized-bicycle` family at n=682 (m=341), the exact ring
size used by this board's leader, [[682,172,78]]. Hypothesis: the leader's
published construction note (`notes/682-172-52-b.md`) documents a specific,
reproducible recipe -- not a lucky random pair -- and testing that exact
recipe (rather than generic random a,b) would find stronger-than-generic
candidates even if not matching k=172 specifically.

## What was searched

1. Extracted the leader's exact a,b supports directly from its committed
   `H_X` row 0 and confirmed, by direct GF(2) polynomial gcd computation,
   that b(x) = a(x^4) exactly (degree-86 shared divisor, matching
   `notes/682-172-52-b.md`'s claim) and that a(x) is invariant under the
   symmetry j -> 32j (mod 341), ord(32,341)=2, giving 30 fixed points
   (multiples of 11) and 155 size-2 orbits.
2. Reproduced that exact symmetry+Frobenius-pairing recipe: sampled a(x)
   as f fixed points + (w-f)/2 size-2 orbits (weights/fixed-point counts
   (w,f) in {(14,6),(14,4),(16,6),(16,8),(16,4),(18,8),(18,6)}, matching
   the leader's note), with b(x) = x^s * a(x^4) for shift s in
   {0,1,7,97,170} (not only s=0).
3. Screened ~140 such candidates with a locally-compiled gf2_fast
   accelerator (this repository's own `verify/gf2_fast.cpp`, built here
   with a one-line MSVC portability patch for `__builtin_popcountll` ->
   `__popcnt64`; not part of this submission, build-only) at 20,000
   trials/candidate (~4s each).
4. Deepened the top 5 by triage kd^2/n to 500,000 trials each
   (~140s/candidate); the winner (w=14,f=6,s=0) held at d=95 (down only
   ~9% from the 20k-trial estimate of 104 -- contrast with ~90%+ collapses
   seen on generic random-symmetry candidates during this same search
   session, which is the signal that this candidate's structure, not
   luck, is driving the result).
5. Final confirmation: two independent 8,000,000-trial runs (CI-equivalent
   budget), seeds 999 (500k, found d=95) and 42 (8M, found d=96) via
   `distance_rand_witness(..., threads=20)`. Reporting the tighter of the
   two, d=95, as the upper bound; both searches are witness-backed and
   independently reproducible from the (a,b) supports below.

## Dead ends

- Generic random (a,b) with independent supports: kd^2/n ~60 at this n.
- a symmetric under j->32j but b=q*a with generic q (no fixed recipe
  weights/shift): best confirmed kd^2/n after deep search ~500 (m=341,
  r=8 variant), well below this entry.
- A systematic "fixed-point quality map" (which of the 30 individual
  fixed points correlate with good results, built from 60 triage
  samples) suggested points {308,264} were favorable and {198,66}
  unfavorable; a follow-up causal test (full 6-point forced sets, 25
  deep samples/group) refuted this: "best 6" averaged kd^2/n=348 (~equal
  to unconstrained control, 340) and "worst 6" averaged 465 (higher than
  "best 6"). The original correlation was small-sample noise, not a
  deterministic pattern -- documented here so the next searcher does not
  redo this specific test.
- A neighborhood sweep across nearby m (329-347) and generic symmetry
  orders (r_sim in {2,4,8}) with the same recipe shape, deepened to
  500,000 trials: all top candidates converged to kd^2/n ~110-120,
  confirming the exact (m=341, symmetry=32, q=4) triple matters, not
  just "some m near 341 with some Frobenius pairing."
