A direct one-row scan of the staged 787-qubit predecessor found 98
structural variants preserving maximum weight 8, radius 4, and inherited
X-27/Z-27 witnesses. Exact rank, connectivity, and projection of the
predecessor's GPU logicals retained 48 connected variants across 47
side/target choices; two connected variants were refuted by projected
GPU logicals. A set of 245 validated predecessor weight-27 logicals,
assembled from inherited, ancestor, GPU, and RIS witnesses and
stabilizer-row XOR closure, projected to validated logicals lighter than
27 in 34 of those variants. Fourteen variants remained. A 10,000-trial
pair-depth-8 accelerated RIS screen of all 14 found no lighter logical.

Deeper RIS runs found validated weight-26 logicals for all 13 other
variants in that set: X37, Z377, Z380, Z382, Z385, Z386, Z387, Z391,
Z399, Z402, Z405, Z406, and Z408. Each low-weight logical lifted to a
valid weight-27 logical in the 787-qubit predecessor. Stabilizer-row
closure expanded the predecessor's known set to 332 supports; their
projection did not refute Z407.

Z407 produced no lighter logical in 10,000 pair-depth-8 trials.
It returned best weight 27 through 100,000 pair-depth-2048 trials,
500,000 pair-depth-512 trials (seed 837701407), 100,000
pair-depth-4096 trials (seed 1195701407), and 100,000
pair-depth-8192 trials (seed 1605301407). Every returned logical was
validated on the exact reduced matrices.

A Perlmutter GPU search ran 50 million pair-depth-8 trials per CSS side
(seed 8407). Both sides returned best weight 27. The GPU wrapper
CPU-verified both returned logicals, and independent validation on the
exact submitted matrices confirmed their supports. This bounded search
found no logical below 27.

The unchanged trusted candidate gate passed with seed 558695926. It
recomputed CSS commutation, `n=786`, `k=8`, maximum check weight 8, one
layer, radius 4, both weight-27 logicals, and Tanner and stabilizer-group
connectivity. Its 8,000-trial RIS pass found no lighter logical. It found
no exact or Weisfeiler–Leman-equivalent board entry and marked the code
as advancing the weight-8, local-2d-single CSS cell by lower `n`.
An independent eight-step reconstruction from `codes/794-8-27.json`
exactly reproduced the submitted checks, coordinates, and logicals.

**`d≤27` is a witness-backed upper bound.** The bounded refutation
searches above do not prove the minimum distance is 27.
