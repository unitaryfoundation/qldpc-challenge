# [[866,128,12]]: [[4,2,2]] amplification of 186-64-6

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: check weight 7, so the weight-8 class, unrestricted locality (no layout submitted), CSS board. One step of the distance amplification of Liang, Gu, Chen, Eisert and Wang (arXiv:2609.37231) with the [[N,N-2,2]] amplifier turns a CSS code [[n,k,d]] whose retained checks are linearly independent into a [[(N+1)n - k, (N-2)k, 2d]] code, at check weight max(w_X + 1, w_Z + 1, N + q_Z, N + q_X) with q the largest column weights. The distance doubles exactly, for X and Z separately (Lemma 4 there), so every board base with a certified exact distance gives an amplified code with a proved exact distance. Hypothesis: some of these are new points of the frontier, in particular where the board's amplified entries keep dependent base rows (which only lengthen the code) or use bases without a certificate. Here the base is codes/186-64-6.json ([[186,64,6]] code of Construction I of arXiv:2610.19451, board entry of @MathysRennela), certified exact in certs/186-64-6.json.

## What was searched

All CSS entries of board commit 8042f9da with an exact certificate were amplified on paper with the [[N,N-2,2]] amplifiers for N = 4, 6, 8, 10 (935, 784, 664 and 567 bases with (N+1)n - k <= 1000). Of the admissible candidates with check weight at most 12, 89 are not dominated by a CSS entry in (n, k, d, w) and 57 of these also not by each other; most have d = 4 (high-rate bases of distance 2). The eight with d >= 12 were built and verified, with linearly independent base rows spanning the row spaces chosen to minimize the check weight (randomized greedy; a counting bound shows that no choice of rows gives a lower weight). This code is one of them.

## Evidence trail

- Construction: Definition 1 of the README of the package cited below, with the base rows listed in provenance.construction left out (61 of 62 X-checks and 61 of 62 Z-checks kept); n = 866 = 5 x 186 - 64, k = 128 by GF(2) ranks, check weight 7, qubit degree at most 10 (6 X-checks, 6 Z-checks).
- Distance (proved): d_X = 2 d_X(base) = 12 and d_Z = 2 d_Z(base) = 12 by Lemma 4 of arXiv:2609.37231 (written out in the coordinates of this entry as Proposition 4 of the README), since d_X(base) = d_Z(base) = 6 is certified in certs/186-64-6.json (tropidist b03ec2d4077f, level solver). Witnesses: u (x) (e_0 + e_1) for the witnesses u of codes/186-64-6.json.
- Consistency check (computed, heuristic): an information-set search with 1.1e5 trials per side found nothing lighter than 12; the 22878 and 22878 distinct words of weight 12 it found (X, Z) all have the form l (x) (e_a + e_b) with l a weight-6 logical of the base, as the proof predicts (Lemma 5 of the README; with the 3813 such logicals of the base found by a saturated search there are 6 x 3813 = 22878 of them per side).
- Direct certification (computed): tropidist b03ec2d4077f, run by us in the board's encoding (one thread), proves d_X = d_Z = 12 in 2.3 s per side, without the lemma; recorded in certs/866-128-12.json (same pull request).
- Board placement (computed at 8042f9da): no CSS entry dominates or equals it in (n, k, d, w); it dominates 936-48-12 (better on n, k). At check weight 8 the entry 712-182-20 is better in n, k and d; this code is undominated through its check weight 7.
- Final claim: d = 12 exactly, by proof. The entry claims exact, backed by certs/866-128-12.json.

## Dead ends

- Most amplifications of certified bases are on the board already or dominated: by our audit, 70 of the 80 amplified entries of 8042f9da are amplifications of certified bases (so their distances are exact by the same lemma), and for example [[90,8,10]] and [[108,8,10]] give [[442,16,20]] and [[532,16,20]] at check weight 7, dominated by the board's [[252,16,d<=20]].
- The [[4,2,2]] step costs more than one unit of check weight when a column weight of the base exceeds w - 3: [[48,4,7]] and [[60,4,8]] (w = 5, column weight 3) give [[236,8,14]] and [[296,8,16]] at check weight 7, dominated by [[140,8,14]] and [[180,12,d<=16]].

## Tools

Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code); Python (standard library) for the construction, ranks and checks; an information-set search in C; tropidist (Cheng, Guo, Pan, arXiv:2611.08247) at the board's pinned commit b03ec2d4077f for the direct runs.
