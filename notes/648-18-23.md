# [[648,18,23]] — open-boundary tile code on an 18x18 lattice (weight-8, bilayer)

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell `weight-8 / local-2d-bilayer`, family `tile`. Same opening as its
siblings `[[612,18,21]]` and `[[722,18,25]]`: the k=18 tile ladder jumped from
`codes/578-18-20.json` to `codes/792-18-27.json` with nothing in between, and
the hypothesis was that those sizes were simply never built. The working
prediction — that d is set by `min(Lx, Ly)` and therefore steps in even-ish
jumps rather than smoothly in n — makes an 18x18 lattice the rung-23 size.

## What was searched

Every lattice `(Lx, Ly)` with `Lx <= Ly` in `[14, 23]^2` and `2 Lx Ly` inside
(578, 792) — 24 of them — built with `research/local2d/boundary_engine.
build_planar(Lx, Ly, S_F_TILE4, S_G_TILE4_MAIN)`, after calibrating the
generator against `codes/924-18-31.json` by X and Z check-matrix equality.
Screening 60,000 gf2_fast RIS trials per lattice, survivors deepened to
400,000 fast trials, every packaged candidate put through the real gate
(`verify/validate_candidate.py`, run unchanged via
`research/kit/coordination.py`). Layout from
`research/local2d/planar.grid_coordinates`, layers=2, interaction radius
4.2426.

## Evidence trail

- 60,000 fast trials: d <= 24
- 400,000 fast trials (packaging): d <= 23, X witness of weight 23
- **1,500,000 fast trials, fresh seed 101**: d <= 23 still (X 23, Z 28
  measured separately at the NumPy budget)
- CI's gate re-verifies at PR time with a deeper fresh-seed pass

**Witness-backed upper bound, not exact**: k=18 is far outside the certifier's
measured envelope (`verify/certify.py` has certified only d <= 13 and k <= 12
today), so `d<=` is the honest and only label available. The claim is
refutation-tested at PR time and again by the weekly board sweep.

## Dead ends

- The 16x\* and 17x\* sizes around this one carry d <= 20 and d <= 21
  respectively; the 17x\* codes advance the cell and are submitted as their
  own entries, the 16x\* ones are dominated by `codes/578-18-20.json`.
- `[[720,18,24]]`, an 18x20 lattice, **collapsed from d <= 24 to d <= 23** at
  the 1.5M-trial confirmation. It is the same rung as this entry at a larger
  n, so it was not promoted; rung 24 was left unfilled rather than claimed on
  a soft measurement.
- `[[722,18,26]]` (19x19) collapsed from 26 to 25 at the same depth and was
  re-staged at the deeper number before promotion.
- No lattice witnessed d=22 at any budget.

## Tools

Model: MiMo-V2.6-Flash, driven as an OpenCode agent harness. Repository
tooling only: `research/local2d/boundary_engine.py`,
`research/local2d/planar.py`, `research/kit/surrogate.py` with the `gf2_fast`
accelerator (`make fast`), `research/kit/submit.py`,
`research/kit/coordination.py`. About 15 CPU core-hours for the shared sweep,
~2M fast trials behind this entry's own claim.
