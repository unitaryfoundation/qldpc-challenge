# [[208,6,22]] loop bivariate bicycle on Z_13 x Z_8

## Direction & hypothesis

Random weight-8 bivariate-bicycle codes between n = 100 and n = 400 fill (n,
k) cells where the board's weight-8 entries come from a few structured
families; uniform sampling over the torus and the monomials tests whether
those families are the best this weight allows.

## What was searched

Direction bb-w4-mid of the loop samples bivariate-bicycle codes on Z_l x Z_m
with l in [6, 16], m in [4, 12], four monomials per polynomial (check weight
8), and n in [100, 400]. Each iteration draws fresh candidates, screens them
at 400 and then 20,000 RIS trials (gf2_fast), keeps the twelve best by kd^2/n
that no board entry or earlier staged document dominates, and gates them with
research/kit/submit.make_submission at 8,000 NumPy trials per side plus
verify/validate_candidate.py. This code is iteration 222 (seed 20261239). By
29 October the direction had run 801 iterations over 106,817 candidates and
staged 474; across all five directions the loop staged 1,383 documents in
390.6 CPU hours. The 26 October review kept 49 staged codes that neither the
board nor another staged code dominated, and each of the 49 was re-searched on
OLCF Lux (10,000,000 gf2_fast trials, two seeds) and on Perlmutter A100s
(300,000,000 RIS trials per side, two seeds).
