## Original note, with the file paths updated

# [[684,20,106]] — Kasai affine 2BGA interior-gap extension, kd^2/n 328.5 at n=684

## Direction & hypothesis

Take the k axis of the unrestricted weight-9plus cell at n=684 past the
k=16/18 pair ([[684,16,110]] kd^2/n 283.0, [[684,18,106]] kd^2/n
295.7, both merged from this campaign): no board entry at n<=684
carried k>=20, and a k=20 point at d>=101 would lead the cell outright.
The hypothesis: the affine-2BGA family's exponent-vector search carries
high-k interior points once the high-k screen phantoms are filtered by
full-budget ladders.

## What was searched

Lane kasai-interior-1120, high-k census (three 2,000-draw sweeps at
q=19 with --min-k 17, seeds 42009/42010/42011): only two survivors in
6,000 draws, both k=20 — this draw (42011-1018, screen d=120, eff 421)
and a sibling (42011-1224, screen 121). The high-k screen band is
phantom-dominated (a prior k=26 head screening at 124 measured d<=18),
so both went through the ghost filter and full multi-budget ladders.

## Evidence trail

Confirmation ladder, each rung a fresh seed, reading the lightest
logical found:

| budget (trials) | lightest logical |
| ---: | ---: |
| 3 x 20,000 (numpy pd10) | 118, 117, 122 |
| 3 x 200,000 (fast pd12) | 118, 116, 114 |
| 2 x 2,000,000 (fast pd12) | 110, 111 |
| 2 x 8,000,000 (fast pd10) | 111, 106 |

The rung-10 search (8M trials, pd10, seed 92030) found a weight-106
X-side logical; the witness was recovered by deterministic seed replay
and verified (commutes with H_Z, outside the H_X row space). A Z-side
numpy hunt (ker(HX), 50k trials, seed 88005) gave a weight-120 witness;
the submission accelerator (2M trials per side) kept it. The claim is
the witness-backed upper bound d <= 106, never exact. The sibling head
([[684,20,121]]) settled at d <= 104 under identical budgets and is
dominated by this document; the family's convergence behaviour across
the two siblings (2M floors 110-111, 8M floors 104-106) is mutually
corroborating.

## Dead ends

- The high-k screen phantom band: [[684,26,124]] (screen eff 584)
  measured d <= 18 and [[684,20,124]] (screen eff ~430) measured
  d <= 18 in earlier batches; of six high-k screen heads across the
  campaign, only the two in this batch survived their ladders, and
  only one holds the seat.
- [[684,20,121]] (screen 121): full ladder to d <= 104, dominated by
  this document (same n, same k, lower claim). Its pd12 8M probes
  (110/108) failed to break its own pd10 floor.
- The RIS floor's budget descent (118 -> 114 -> 111 -> 106) is not
  fully flat; the claim is the lightest witnessed reading, and the
  CI refutation battery (pair depth 8, 8M trials) cannot reach the
  pd10 instrument depth.
