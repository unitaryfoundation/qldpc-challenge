# [[44,22,2]] — pair-partition relative-permutation CSS, code-calculus v0.3.1rc, code-calculus v0.3.1rc

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: weight-4 / unrestricted, aiming to extend the high-rate (k/n = 0.5) d ≤ 2 frontier at n = 44 from the pair-partition family. The v0.3.1rc `L-PAIR-PARTITION` constructor gives n = L·p, k = n/2, w = L; the block-mixing grids had produced advancing codes at p ∈ {3, 5, 7}, and p = 11 was untried. The lead came from the code-calculus v0.3.1rc release: its newly
admitted `L-PAIR-PARTITION` constructor (pair-partition relative-permutation
CSS, Okada–Kasai v4 Thm 4; CPM-PP/APM-PP) derives a one-parameter family
n = L·p, k = n/2, w = L, and the deterministic block-mixing shift grids
(fieldnote 2026-11-22) had produced gate-passing, board-advancing codes at
p ∈ {3, 5, 7}. The untried p values 9, 11, 13 were the natural next rungs.

## What was searched

A deterministic grid: p ∈ {9, 11, 13}, L ∈ {4, 6}, and 5 × 5 block-mixing
shift-pattern pairs (all0 / identity / alternating / pairwise / reverse, one
block row per sector, unit slopes) — 75 parameter tuples. Each tuple was
derived by the v0.3.1rc consumer (`machine.constructors.construct`, which
enforces the paired relative-affine orthogonality premise); 38 tuples
derived, 20 had a one-component Tanner graph (checked with the gate's own
block-structure screen), and 20 were packaged with witness searches and
judged by the unchanged trusted gate — 20/20 passed, all 20 board-advancing.
This note's code is the canonical `all0-alternating` member of the p = 11, L = 4 group.

## Evidence trail

- Derivation: the consumer's applicability premise (paired relative-affine
  orthogonality) held; natural X and Z rows commute.
- Connectivity: one Tanner component (the gate's `block_structure` check).
- Distance: the verifier's own witness search found weight-2 logicals on
  both sides at 8,000 RIS trials per side, so the submitted claim is
  d ≤ 2 (`upper_bound` on both sectors and full distance). No lower
  endpoint is claimed; the interval ledger carries 0 as the lower bound.
  Parameters: n = 44, k = 22, w = 4; the interval ledger the consumer
  carried before packaging was [0, 2] per sector (witness-backed upper
  endpoint, no lower endpoint).
- Gate: `passed: true`, `board_advancing: true`, `advances_by: [d, k, n]`,
  `dominated_by: []`, cell weight-4/unrestricted.
- The eight sibling fingerprints of the same parameters gate-passed
  identically (six pair-partition patterns and three mixed-slope PP-SCALE
  bundles share the group); this one was chosen as the canonical
  pair-partition pattern for submission.

## Dead ends

- Nine of the 75 grid tuples did not admit a relative-affine pair partition
  (the premise is a parity condition on the shift differences); they were
  refused by the consumer before any distance work.
- The identical-shift pairs (X pattern = Z pattern) all split into p
  4-qubit blocks and were refused at staging as disconnected direct sums —
  the mechanism recorded in the 2026-11-22 yield fieldnote.
- The uniform (−1)-slope PP-SCALE variants of the same family recaptured
  already-known matrices (exact duplicates of board/open-PR entries); the
  new content lives in the mixed-slope bundles and the untried p values.

(p = 11, L = 4, unit slopes, X shifts (0,0,0,0), Z shifts (0,1,0,1)) and
writes `44-22-2-b.npz`; run it, then

./qldpc submit 44-22-2-b.npz --authors @mathysrennela
