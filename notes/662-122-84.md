# [[662,122,84]] — cyclic generalized bicycle at prime length 331, no imposed symmetry

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the kd²/n headline at check weight ≤ 32 (the board's admissible
maximum), after the correction wave of issues #2899 / #2949. Every top entry
at n = 674–690 — this author's `codes/682-172-52-b.json`, `codes/682-130-56.json`
and `codes/682-82-35.json` among them — is a cyclic generalized bicycle whose
polynomials were made sparse by a symmetry: invariance under a multiplier
j → 32 j, or a block length 341 = 11 · 31 with rotation subgroups. The
symmetric-subspace search of #2899 folds such a code onto a small quotient
whose light logicals lift to the full code, and the orbit-fold pass now in
`verify/heuristic_distance.py` finds them in seconds.

This entry is the opposite construction: a **prime** block length, so Z_331
has no rotation subgroups, and a polynomial pair with **no multiplier
relation**, so the only automorphisms are the cyclic shifts and the fold
search has nothing to act on. The price is that sparse multiples of a
divisor are rarer without a symmetry: at degree 61 the lightest weigh 12–16,
which keeps the check weight at 30, inside the cap. A companion entry from
the same campaign family applies the recipe at L = 337, k = 86 and is
submitted separately.

## What was searched

x^331 − 1 = (x + 1) · eleven irreducible factors of degree 30. Divisors of
degree 61 = 1 + 2 · 30 (k = 122): 55 ideals, visited in random order (seed
323). Per ideal:

1. Sparse codewords of the ideal by the kit's Prange search
   (`research/cyclic_gb.py`, `prange_sparse_words`, 40,000 trials, weights
   10–16; 120 words kept).
2. The structural cap once per ideal: single-block logicals are words of the
   [331, 61] code generated by (x^331 − 1)/g and do not depend on the pair,
   so `gf2_fast.circulant_gb_witness` (60k, then 300k trials) decides whether
   any pair from the ideal can reach the target. Ideal 1: cap 76, below the
   83 needed for kd²/n > 1250, skipped. Ideal 2: cap 94.
3. Per pair (|a| + |b| ≤ 32): k = 2 deg g checked, Tanner-graph and
   stabilizer-group connectivity checked, then the verifier's own
   `orbit_fold_min_logical` (300 fold trials, 90 s cap) — **no symmetry to
   fold** (0 partitions) — then an RIS ladder of 2k → 50k → 500k → 2M trials
   per pair, discarding as soon as the bound fell below the target.

The first pair of ideal 2 survived the whole ladder and is this entry.

The claim is a witness-backed **upper bound**; the exact distance is open.
Score at the bound: 122 · 84² / 662 = 1300.4. Without exploitable
structure, a lower bound can only come from more random search, which is
the same tool every refuter has; the family's symmetric entries lost 20–50
units to the fold search, and this construction is chosen so that it cannot.

## Dead ends

- Sparse multiples at prime length are the limiting resource: at degrees
  64 and 85–91 (k = 128–182) the lightest ideal words found weigh 24 or
  more, so a first campaign without the check-weight cap produced
  [[674,128,≤88]] (w = 66) and [[662,182,≤74]] (w = 80) that the board cannot
  accept. A 40,000-trial Prange search does reach weight 12–16 words at
  degrees 61–64; the k = 128 campaigns at L = 337 are running on that basis.
- The first ideal visited (cap 76) shows that the constituent [331, 61]
  code, not the pair, is the first filter; the cap search rejected it in
  seconds, before any RIS trial.

## Reproduction

Let C(S) be the 331 × 331 binary circulant whose row i has ones at columns
(i + s) mod 331 for s ∈ S. H_X = [C(a) | C(b)], H_Z = [C(b)ᵀ | C(a)ᵀ] with a
and b above. The divisor is gcd(a, x^331 − 1) = gcd(b, x^331 − 1), degree 61.
